# Accessibility

Vamp runs on the Shanling Q2's small screen and is driven by its wheel and buttons. There is no screen reader or spoken feedback.

- **Contrast:** Light Mode switches between light and dark looks, and the accent colour can be changed.
- **Motion:** Settings > Display > Reduce Motion turns slides, zooms and bounces into short fades or nothing (pages
  cross-fade, Control Center lands still, the selection bar does not lean, the charging sweep stands still, Now Playing's art keeps its size on pause and a new song and the cover into Now Playing cross-fade in place). Scrolling,
  progress and the volume bar still move.

If something gets in your way, open an issue saying what you tried and what stopped you.
