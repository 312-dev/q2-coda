# iPhone mode: Car Audio and route 1's experiments

Route 1 of the car design has the Q2 present itself to a car as an iPhone in media mode over USB: iAP2 over a HID interface, the audio as USB audio PCM. The design is in the user's notes (`car-modes-design-20261119.md`); this page covers Car Audio as the image has it ([below](#car-audio-in-the-image)), then the tooling built for the first experiments and what the desk and the car found. The user's own step-by-step runbook for the car is `car-experiments-runbook.md` beside the design.

**Apple's identity ships in every build.** The iPhone gadget uses Apple's vendor ID and the strings "Apple Inc." and "iPhone", in release builds as in `--dev` ones, with no build flag (the user's decision, 2026-11-20), as two public GPL projects already do: [PodBox](https://github.com/anthonyfletcher/podbox) and [OrangeCar](https://github.com/sbuysse/orangecar). No authentication is defeated: the car is the one with the MFi chip, and the Q2 only verifies it.

<a id="car-audio-in-the-image"></a>
## Car Audio in the image

**What the user sees.** Settings > General > USB has a new first choice, **Automatic**, the default (`USBPICK` 0, the value Ask Every Time had; Ask Every Time is now 5 and still opens the chooser). On Automatic every cable with a host on it shows the host an iPhone first. A car takes it: the car's USB source plays the Q2, its screen shows Now Playing and its buttons work, and the Q2 toasts **Playing through BMW 83828 (USB)** (the car's own name from its identification) once the car's audio flows. A computer does not: the iPhone is removed and the cable is Transfer Files, the SD card, as if picked. A charger only charges. USB Debugging is never reached this way, only by picking it; any explicit pick (Transfer Files, USB Audio, Charge Only, USB Debugging, Ask Every Time) skips the iPhone entirely. A mode page's Stop now goes back to Automatic. If the car's audio goes wrong the Q2 says so: **Car audio problem: dropouts** (packets missed on the bus, or the player ran dry) or **Car audio problem: no sound** (the car asked for audio and took none for 5 s), a toast each time a new problem starts.

**The pieces.** At a cable in on Automatic, demo (`patch/usb_ui.c`, `patch/usb_car.c`) holds stock off the cable (`g_usblink_status` 3) and runs `/usr/bin/q2car start` (`patch/q2car.sh`), which returns at once and, detached, parks a `--dev` build's ADB gadget, loads `/module_driver/usb_f_uac1.ko` if it is not yet loaded (beside the vendor modules, not in the list `S11module_driver_default` loads at boot; [the module](#the-uac1-module)), makes the gadget the drive plan made (`full:iap2:uac1`: Apple's `05ac:12a8`, four configurations, configuration 2 UAC1 with `req_number` 4 ([empty packets](#empty-packets)) on interfaces 0 and 1 and the HID on 2), and runs `/usr/bin/q2-iap` (`patch/q2_iap.c`) on it with `--state /tmp/q2car.state`. The player's way 3 is in every image's `q2player`. While the iPhone is on the cable the shim writes Now Playing and takes the car's buttons with Car Remote off too (`patch/coda_rc_shim.c`), and the Bluetooth connect a cable in starts (`headset_bt_usb`, [Bluetooth on USB power](internals.md#bluetooth-on-usb-power)) waits: a car that keeps the iPhone never gets it, a host that does not gets it with Transfer Files. A cable out runs `q2car stop` in the background, and so does a pick in the USB picker with the iPhone up, whose own gadget follows once the stop has returned; either asks the player first to let go of the car's PCM ([the stream's life](#the-stream-s-life)).

**Telling a car from anything else** (`q2_iap.c` `decide`, written to the state file demo reads every 200 ms):

| The host | Verdict | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Apple's `C0 53`, or iAP2's detect (`FF 55 02 00 EE 10`) in a HID `SET_REPORT` | car | stays; iAP2 runs |
| configuration 1, 3 or 4 chosen, usbmux's `C0 45`, or the role switch `40 51` (wired CarPlay) | computer | `q2-iap` exits 20 at once; `q2car` removes the gadget and writes `down=1`; demo gives the cable to Transfer Files |
| addressed the Q2 and asked nothing Apple's within 3 s (30 s once it chose configuration 2) | silent | exits 21; the same as a computer |
| never addressed the Q2 (a charger) | none | after 3 s demo shows the charge toast, once; the gadget stays, unseen, and a host that comes later is decided as any other |

A computer seen after `C0 53` still wins (a host that sends `C0 53` and then picks a PTP configuration is not a car); after the detect it does not. Apple's charging request `40 40` decides nothing. **The windows:** in the car's log (2026-11-20) the BMW sent `C0 53` about 0.2 s after it addressed the Q2, and at the desk (2026-11-19) Windows chose configuration 1 and sent `C0 45` within about 0.3 s, so 3 s from the address is ten times what either took, short enough that a computer's Transfer Files comes within a few seconds, and equal to the probe's `USB_PROBE_MS`. Choosing configuration 2, the iPod USB Interface, is itself what only an iPod accessory does, and OrangeCar measured a cold-started car silent for 22 to 27 s after configuring, so that host gets the design's 30 s. The window runs from the address (`q2-iap` reads the controller's state every 100 ms, and any request counts), not from the plug, so a car whose USB wakes slowly is not cut short. The emulator has no USB host, so these were not measured there; `test/iap_probe.py` drives each case through a stand-in `ep0`.

**What a computer goes through, and the cost left.** Each plug into a computer on Automatic shows it an iPhone for the fraction of a second it takes to choose a configuration, then a storage device. Windows remembers a driver per vendor and product ID, so the first plug installs or looks up a driver for an "iPhone" (`05ac:12a8`; with Apple's driver installed, "Apple Mobile Device USB Composite Device", as the desk saw), and every plug plays the device chimes twice and may flash AutoPlay for the PTP camera before the storage device replaces it. A Mac may open Photos or Finder's device sidebar for the moment the iPhone is there (its "open Photos for this device" choice applies). Neither was measured: no computer was at hand for this change. A computer that is a regular for Transfer Files is spared all of it by picking Transfer Files.

**The health check** (`q2_iap.c` `health_poll`, every second while the car has asked for audio): flowing when the module's `in_sent` (`/sys/module/usb_f_uac1/parameters`) moves; dropouts when more than 1% of a second's packets came back unsent (`in_missed`; with one request in flight each is resent, which puts the samples back in order but still leaves the car a millisecond without a packet); underruns when the player's count in `/tmp/q2-iap.under` moves (the car's PCM found dry while a song flowed into it, a pause's, a seek's and an open's excepted: `player/output.c` `sink`, counted by `cartrace.c` `car_trace_underrun` and written aside and renamed when it moves, looked at once a second with [the car's trace](#car-trace), `under=`); no sound when nothing was taken 5 s after the car asked. A problem is over after 10 clean seconds. The player's count is not covered by a host test: the host player writes to a file, which never runs dry.

**The log.** `/mnt/mmc/.q2muse/logs/q2car.log` (Send Logs takes it; the kernel's last lines `q2car.log.dmesg`): `q2car`'s lines, every iAP2 packet and message as the experiments logged them but the position update a playing song sends twice a second and the bare acknowledgements either way, which the link's health line counts instead (`since the last, not logged: N position updates, N bare acks in, N out`; a packet sent again is logged; on this machine 1,085 B/s and 10.9 lines a second while a song played became 224 B/s and under one, `test/iap_probe.py` `test_position_updates_are_counted_not_logged`), the verdict with what decided it and its milliseconds since the address (`host: computer (configuration 1), 212 ms after it addressed the Q2`), the audio's start and problems, and at the end one line that judges the cable, also added with the module's counters to `q2car-summary.log` beside it, which keeps every cable's (cut to its newer half past 256 KB). At the next cable a car's log becomes `q2car.1.log`, the older cars' moving to `q2car.2.log` and on, the oldest going once the cars' logs pass 40 MB together, never one of the newest two, or 7 days, never the newest (`q2car.sh` `rotate`, `CARLOG_MAX`, `CARLOG_KEEP`, `CARLOG_DAYS`): a cable's own log can reach 12 MB (q2-iap's `--log-cap`), so a long drive's log is still there beside the next car's; a computer's or a charger's is kept only until the next as `q2car.other.log`, so plugging in a computer to read the logs never pushes a drive's out (the drive of 2026-11-22 lost three car cables' logs to the next car, a second drive and that computer; `test/iap_probe.py` `test_cars_kept_and_others_never_push_them_out`). q2-iap's lines go to a child over a pipe, which writes them to the card, so a slow card never holds the loop (on the 2026-11-22 drive three stalls of 109 to 278 ms were log writes; a full pipe costs lines, counted and said, `test_a_slow_log_holds_nothing`), and within a cable the file is capped: past 6 MB (`--log-cap`) no packet trace, past 12 MB only q2-iap's own events (`probe` and `car` lines) and the link's, each mark said once (`test_the_log_is_capped_within_a_cable`); at q2-iap's end the child syncs the file, and q2-iap waits up to a second for it:

```text
summary: host car (iAP2's detect, 212 ms), car "BMW 83828", identified yes, audio asked yes, flowing yes, in_sent 120345 in_missed 12 (0.0%), underruns 0, problems 0, p_rate 44100 rate_sets 1 rate_asked 44100
```

demo's unified log has a line at the plug (`Automatic: the iPhone shown`) and one for the verdict.

## The gadget and the probe

`q2iap` is one configfs gadget: `05ac:12a8`, `bcdDevice` `0x1001`, class `00/00/00`, "Apple Inc.", "iPhone", a 40-hex serial made once in `/mnt/data/q2-iap.serial`, every configuration `0xC0` and 500 mA. The `full` variant has four configurations: 1 PTP, 2 "iPod USB Interface" (audio, then a HID interface), 3 PTP with Apple Mobile Device, 4 those with Apple USB Ethernet. `min` has configuration 2 alone. Configuration 2's audio is the kernel's `uac2` with the Q2 as the source (`p_chmask` 3, `c_chmask` 0, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit), or `uac1` from the module. The HID interface is a FunctionFS instance carrying the 208-byte report descriptor (vendor page `0xFF00`, input reports 1 to 12, output reports 13 to 21), one interrupt IN endpoint of 64 bytes every 8 ms. The FunctionFS flags are `0xC3`, so requests addressed to the device, Apple's vendor requests among them, reach the probe.

The probe answers `81 06 22xx` with the report descriptor, `81 06 21xx` with the HID descriptor, Apple's `C0 53` with four zero bytes (`--carplay`: `01 00 00 00`) and `GET_REPORT` with zeros. It reads and logs every OUT request's data, and stalls every other IN request. With echo on, a `SET_REPORT` whose payload after `[id][link control]` is `FF 55 02 00 EE 10` is echoed as the smallest input report that holds it (report 2: `02 00 FF 55 02 00 EE 10 00 00`) on the interrupt endpoint, written from a child so the event loop never waits on the host. The kernel answers the host's descriptor requests itself, so those never reach the log. The controller's state (`udc=default/addressed/configured/not attached`) is logged at each change instead.

<a id="sessions"></a>
### Sessions and plans

A session is a plug: it begins at the first `ENABLE` (the host chose a configuration) and ends when a `DISABLE` or `SUSPEND` is not followed by another `ENABLE` or `RESUME` within 8 s (`--gap-ms`), counted on `CLOCK_BOOTTIME`, so a Q2 suspended for hours after a `DISABLE` ends the session at its wake, where the monotonic clock, which stops in suspend, counted none of it. A session's end writes its summary, removes the player's car file and forgets the iAP2 link (`iap2_session_end`), so a bus that comes back without a new detect gets nothing of the old session, and the next `ENABLE` is a new session. Every timer and deadline of q2-iap and of the link runs on that clock (`boot_ms`); a pass that finds the boot clock a second or more ahead of the monotonic one since the last says `suspend: the Q2 slept N ms`, and a link that was up is lost (`sleep_poll`). `--clock-file` adds the milliseconds a file holds to the boot clock alone, a sleep for the host tests (`test/iap_probe.py` `test_a_sleep_ends_the_session_and_the_link`, `test_a_session_s_end_takes_the_car_s_audio_and_the_link`). In the car a cable out was a `SUSPEND` (the controller left `suspended`, `usb_online` 0), never a `DISABLE`, so the first car run stayed in its first phase. A host's reset during enumeration is therefore not a new session, while a cable out and in again is. A plan is phases run in order, each a gadget made, probed and removed. `car` is `full`, `full:echo`, `min:echo`, one session each, then the restore; `m1` is `full:iap2`, `min:iap2`; `m3` is `full:iap2:uac1`, `full:iap2`. `desk` is `full:echo:tone` until the time limit. Tokens: `full` or `min`, `:echo`, `:iap2`, `:noaudio`, `:uac1`, `:linuxid` (desk only: Linux's `1d6b:0106` in place of Apple's, so Windows' own audio driver takes the audio function), `:tone` (a -20 dBFS 1 kHz tone played into the gadget's sound card).

`start` returns at once. The runner, detached with `setsid` and with hangups ignored (adb's shell ends when `start` returns, and its hangup killed a runner that had not reached its own session yet), parks `q2adb` after `--delay` (at least 2 s, so `start`'s own lines reach the computer), and runs the plan. `--for` (default 900 s, at most 3600) is the limit from start, measured on the wall clock. The probe exits at it, and a separate watchdog forces `stop` 60 s later if the runner has not finished. The restore is `q2adb-gadget unpark`. `start` refuses unless the USB picker remembers Charge Only (`USBPICK=1`) or USB Debugging (4): any other choice makes demo start stock's storage gadget at the next cable in. Arming needs adb, and picking Charge Only with the cable in parks the ADB gadget, so the way to arm is with USB Debugging, then Charge Only on the Q2 within `--delay`; the runner logs a warning if the picker is still not Charge Only when the delay ends. That park unmounts configfs, so the teardown mounts it again before it looks for the gadget. `--force` skips the check, which is right only when the cable is already in and stays in.

The log is `/mnt/mmc/.q2muse/logs/q2-iap-probe.log`, the shell's lines and the probe's together, stamped with `CLOCK_MONOTONIC` as dmesg is. `.dmesg` beside it gets the kernel log at each phase's end. The previous run is kept as `.prev.log`. **Send Logs** on the LocalSend page sends them, since it sends that whole folder.

## iAP2: milestone M1

`--iap2` (the `:iap2` token) puts the iAP2 files (`patch/iap2_*.c`) on the HID instance: a port of PodBox's link layer and the M1 part of its control session (GPL version 2 or later, under version 3 here; `iap2.h`'s head says what came from where). In order:

- **Transport:** the car's output reports (`SET_REPORT` data, `[id][link control][data]`) reassembled into packets; the Q2's packets cut into the smallest input report that holds what is left, up to report 7 (95 bytes), as PodBox sends at high speed.
- **Link:** the detect echoed; the car's SYN logged whole and answered SYN+ACK with its own parameters, the packet length capped at 8,192; the link up at the car's ACK of it. PodBox's traps are kept: a packet is never sent again to a car whose SYN gives no retries, and a bare ACK goes only to one whose SYN gives a cumulative acknowledgement timeout. One message to a packet.
- **Authentication, the Q2 as verifier:** `AA00`; to the certificate, a 20-byte challenge (32 to one under 800 bytes); 20 ms after the answer, `AA05` and `1D00`. Nothing is checked: the Q2 holds no key and needs none.
- **Identification:** the car's names and its two message lists logged by name; accepted 30 ms later (`1D02`), then the UUID (the serial's first 32 hex digits), the language `en` and the name "Q2 Muse", each only if the car takes it, and a Bluetooth pairing start and stop if it names a component.
- **What comes next, answered as an iPhone answers:** power (`AE01`, in an iPhone's order), the Bluetooth connection (`4E04`, nothing connected), CarPlay unavailable (`4300`, timed after the car's `PowerSourceUpdate` as PodBox found it must be), the library by name (`4C01`) and its songs (`4C04`, [the library on iDrive](#the-library-on-idrive); at M1 it went with no items), USB audio at 44.1 kHz (`DA01` index 7; M3 plays into it), and Now Playing (`5001`) from the shim's `/tmp/coda-rc.now`, which it writes while Car Remote is on (title, artist, album, length, state, the queue's place, the position every half second while playing), else the title "Q2 Muse". The car's button reports (`6802`) are logged, not acted on (M2).

The car's reports go out through a child process that writes them to the HID instance's `ep1` one at a time, fed by a non-blocking pipe, so a host that stops collecting the interrupt endpoint costs reports, never the loop that answers the car's control requests.

**Tested:** `test/iap_probe.py` drives the probe with a synthetic car through a pty standing in for `ep0` and a FIFO for `ep1`: the whole conversation to identification, power, now playing and audio, every packet's checksums and acknowledgements, and the no-retries trap (11 tests; each of three changes to `iap2.c`, a message dropped, the SYN+ACK's window and a checksum, fails them). At the desk (2026-11-19), `hid-car.ps1` played the car through Windows' HID driver against the Q2 in a `min:iap2` phase: every step answered, the 900-byte certificate crossing several output reports, the Q2 sending Now Playing for the song the shim had ("1901", 3:13). **Not tested:** the BMW itself, its real SYN and identification, and whether iDrive lists the Q2 as a source.

## In the car: M1 (2026-11-19, plan `m1`, V1K8i)

**iDrive listed the Q2 as a source and showed its song's title and artist, with the position current; no sound, no cover.** The BMW identified as "BMW 83828", model MGU-74-P, BMW Group, firmware 23.44.2, hardware 4.4.0. Its SYN: window 5, packets up to 65,535 bytes, retransmission after 200 ms and up to 10 times, a cumulative acknowledgement within 22 ms or after 3 packets, sessions 1 (control), 2 (file transfer) and 3. Its certificate was 608 bytes (an MFi 3.0 one, so a 32-byte challenge); it took `AA05`, identified, asked for power, the library (taken empty), Now Playing (attributes 0, 1, 4, 6 to 10, 12, 16, 18, 21 to 24, 26 the artwork, 27; playback 0 to 14, 16, 17), HID and USB audio. Its buttons came as `AccessoryHIDReport`s against the report descriptor in its `StartHID`: five bits on the consumer page, Play, Pause, Next, Previous and Random (`01`, `02`, `04`, `08`, `10`), each sent pressed then released; it sent Play by itself as the source was chosen. At the cable out the controller went suspended, and the Q2 sent the link's last packet again through its ten tries.

**Why no sound** (from `dmesg` and the stock kernel's code): the car opened the UAC2 stream (`UAC2: playback stream START, srate=44100`), then sent a class request addressed to the endpoint, which Shanling's `afunc_setup` refuses (`afunc_setup:2143 Error!`: the path taken when the request's recipient is not an interface, `addiu $7, $zero, 0x85f` before `_dev_err`, 0x85f being 2143), and closed the stream 7 ms later. That is USB Audio 1.0's way of setting a sample rate (a `SET_CUR` to the isochronous endpoint), which UAC2 has no place for, and a real iPhone is UAC1. FunctionFS cannot answer it: the kernel hands an endpoint's requests to the function that owns the endpoint. 5.10's `f_uac1` answers them (`audio_set_endpoint_req` takes `SET_CUR`, `audio_get_endpoint_req` the four `GET`s), so route 1's audio is the UAC1 module ([below](#the-uac1-module)).

## Car audio, buttons and cover (M3, M2, and the cover from M6)

- **The handoff.** At `StartUSBDeviceModeAudio` the probe writes `/tmp/q2-iap.audio`: the gadget's ALSA device (`hw:CARD=UAC1Gadget,0`, or `UAC2Gadget` in a `uac2` phase), then the rate it told the car (44100 unless the player asked for another: [per-song rate](#per-song-rate)). It removes it at `StopUSBDeviceModeAudio`, a link reset, a new plug and its own end. The player's way 3 (`player/output.c` `output_car`, `engine.c` `car_way`; [player: output](player.md#output)) takes over from the DAC's and Bluetooth's while it is there, in every image (`Q2_CAR_USB`, from `tools/build.py`'s `car_usb=True`): a song playing moves to the car where it is within 250 ms, and back when the file goes, as a seek moves; the car's rate through Bluetooth's resampler, 16 bits, unity volume (the car's knob is its volume), and the PCM kept across songs of any width, and of any rate that leaves the car's as it is, so the car's stream never stops between them; a song for another of the car's rates opens it again at the boundary ([per-song rate](#per-song-rate)).
- **A stream that runs on.** The gadget's `u_audio` requeues each packet while ALSA's stream is stopped, and its STOP zeroes the packets first (`uac_pcm_trigger`'s `memset`, read 2026-11-22), so a stopped PCM sends silence. It cannot pause: it advertises a pause, but its trigger has no pause case, answers `-EINVAL` and puts its read position back to the ring's start first, so the car heard the ring again, out of order, up to 371 ms of it. A pause on way 3 therefore takes the frames not yet heard back and stops the PCM (silence to the car, as an iPhone's stream runs on silent through a pause), and writes them again at the resume ([player: Pause](player.md#pause)); a seek or a skip takes its frames back (`snd_pcm_rewind`) instead of dropping them, so the stream runs on into ALSA's silence (`alsa_sw`'s silence size); and a close writes two periods of silence and waits them out, bounded, first. Before the gadget is unbound, the player is asked to close the car's PCM ([player: Releasing the car](player.md#releasing-the-car)): the unbind waits for the last close of its card. <a id="way-3-waits-for-the-car"></a>A car reads only from some time after it asks for audio (the BMW about 2 s), and stops when it changes source or the cable comes out. So a write with no room waits, looking at the car's file every 250 ms (`sink`, `CAR_LOOK_MS`): while the file is there the music stays on the car's way, as an iPhone's does while a car's USB audio is on, never on Bluetooth beside it; q2-iap removes the file at `StopUSBDeviceModeAudio` and `q2car` at the cable out, and that ends the car's way (`OUTPUT_CAR_GONE`) and the song goes back to demo's way, or is paused when nothing else can sound ([nowhere to play](#nowhere-to-play)). A stop or a skip (the engine's `cut`, `output_car_watch`) ends the wait, the frames dropped; ponytail: a pause or a seek waits until the car reads. `test/player/run.py` `test_alsa_sw_params` holds a car that reads after 2 s (waited for), one whose file goes while waiting (the way ends) and a stop while waiting.
- **Buttons (M2).** `StartHID`'s report descriptor is read for one-bit controls on the consumer page; each `AccessoryHIDReport` control that is down and was up goes to the shim down its command queue, the FIFO `/tmp/coda-rc.cmdq` (`coda_rc_cmdq.h` `crc_cmdq_send`: a line each, never waiting, so two presses inside the shim's 500 ms are two commands, `test_two_presses_inside_a_poll_both_reach_the_shim`), else, with no reader there, its command file `/tmp/coda-rc.cmd`, as the Remote app's commands do (the shim takes them while Car Audio's iPhone is on the cable, Car Remote on or off): Play `resume`, Pause `pause`, Play/Pause `toggle`, Next `next`, Previous `previous`, Random `mode` with shuffle turned over, Repeat `mode` with repeat stepped. `SetNowPlayingInformation`'s position is a `seek`. **Hold to scan:** the BMW's descriptor has no fast-forward or rewind usage, so Next and Previous act at their release (`iap2_hid.c` `release`): up within 500 ms (the car's taps were up 13 to 120 ms after the press), the skip; held longer, the Q2's own scan of a held button (`seek.c` `seek_car`, [scan](internals.md#scan): heard at 2, 4 and 8 times on q2player, then in growing jumps), started with the shim's `scan 1` or `scan -1`, said again every 500 ms while the button stays down (a car's scan stops after 2 s without it, a release that never came) and ended by `scan 0`, and the song plays on from where the scan reached. The first drive's scan, a seek every 500 ms, was uneven ("pretty inconsistent", 2026-11-20): each seek mutes, drops and starts a decode afresh, and the DAC's mute cycle in the log came about every 5 s, not every 0.5 s, so most seeks were not applied; where they were lost was not pinned (the shim reads its command file once a UI pass, up to a second apart while dark, and a newer command replaces one not yet read), and the scan no longer depends on it. With Car Remote on, the shim read q2-iap's commands only every 5 s beside its daemon's pipe, so the held button's `scan 1` came 5 s apart, past seek.c's 2 s (`SEEK_CAR_LOST_MS`), and the scan ran in bursts (the drive of 2026-11-22); while the iPhone is on the cable it reads them every 500 ms (`coda_rc_shim.c` `coda_rc_poll`, `test/fw/test_coda_rc_shim.py` `test_car_audio_s_commands_are_read_every_pass_beside_the_daemon_s_pipe`). While Car Audio's iPhone is on the cable the dark UI loop keeps stock's pace, as for Car Remote (`dark_slow_ok`).
- **The cover.** When the car asks for attribute 26, Now Playing names a file transfer (ids 80 to FF), set up on the file transfer session as `[ID, 04, 8-byte size, 00 02]`; at the car's `[ID, 01]` the JPEG goes in pieces as the link allows, `[ID, 80|00|40|C0, bytes]`. <a id="the-car-s-cover"></a>The JPEG is looked for in a child, never in the loop (`q2_iap.c` `art_child`, one song at a time, the last one's child ended when the song changes; on the drive of 2026-11-22 a cover only ffmpeg could make took 13.4 s), in this order: Cover Flow's 160 px copy (`/mnt/mmc/.coverflow/<key>.jpg`, the key FNV-1a of the artist and the album as `coverflow.c` makes it); one made for the album before; for a server song (its library id from `0x40000000`, a Stream Cache copy included) its app's cover, `/mnt/mmc/.<app>/covers/<album id>.jpg`, the album id from its row's path in the catalogue (`q2_iap_lib.c` `lib_app_album`, as `catalogue.c` `catalogue_cover` finds it for the Q2's own Now Playing); `cover.jpg`, `folder.jpg` or `front.jpg` beside the song; for a server song whose app's `q2app` says `covers=1`, the cover asked of the app (`art_ask`, the contract of [Car Remote's covers](internals.md#car-remote-covers) in a file and key of q2-iap's own: `/tmp/q2-<app>.coverask-iap` written whole with `<seq> 160 <album>`, the app named in `/tmp/coda-rc.wake` when its socket is not there, the app's answer `coverack-iap=<seq> <code>` in its state file, 0 saved, waited for 4 s, `--art-ask-ms`; `test_a_server_song_s_cover_asked_of_its_app`); else the song's own picture (an embedded cover), fitted into 160 px as a baseline JPEG by ffmpeg, niced, killed after 20 s or when the child is ended, and reaped (`art_ffmpeg_run`, as Cover Flow's `ff_run`; on the drive of 2026-11-22 every streamed song had one; `test/iap_probe.py` `test_a_song_s_own_picture_is_the_car_s_cover`). A new song's Now Playing waits up to a second for the quick sources (`iap2_nowplaying.c` `ART_HOLD_MS`), so its first update names its cover; once the child goes on to an ask or ffmpeg it goes with an empty one, and the cover follows in a second Now Playing once found. The key is the album's, else the song's path, so the album's next songs take the cover with no child, a baseline JPEG up to 64 KB, sent with each quantisation and Huffman table in a segment of its own (PodBox's car needed that) and its bytes otherwise as they are; none is an empty transfer. ponytail: an iPhone sends about 300 px; Cover Flow's 160 px copy is what the Q2 has without decoding a JPEG.
- **Now Playing.** `NowPlayingUpdate` comes from the shim's `/tmp/coda-rc.now`, which is [now_source.c's](internals.md#now-playing-source) answer: the Library's song, or Radio's or Internet Radio's station (`live=1`), whose song and artist come apart from the stream's StreamTitle, with the station as the album, no duration or position, and as its cover an empty file transfer: a cover left out of the update kept the last song's on the BMW's cluster (q2muse-1123, Radio over USB), and an empty one is how an iPhone says there is none. Every half second the whole update goes again on a new song, state or length; otherwise the position goes while playing, and while paused when it moved (a seek), never for a stream.
- **The window.** The BMW retransmits, so the Q2 keeps no more of its own packets unacknowledged than the car's window (5): a cover's dozen packets wait in a queue (`iap2_link.c` `enqueue`, `pump`) and go as acknowledgements free the window.

<a id="car-trace"></a>
### The car's trace

For the drive after the garbled one (2026-11-20: cut-outs of half a second to a second; the desk counter test then showed one isoc IN packet in three lost in the module and the PCM drained at 1.5 times, [the UAC1 module](#the-uac1-module)), the player says what it hands the car. While a way-3 output is open (`player/cartrace.c`, counted in `output.c` and timed in `engine.c`), each second's counts are looked at after a write. A second with something wrong in it (the PCM ran dry, a write over 50 ms, an underrun) is a line of its own in the unified log; the quiet seconds before it are one line, as are the quiet seconds of each minute (`QUIET_MS`) and those left as the output closes. The lines' windows follow one another, so their frames still add up to what the car was handed, and a drive's log holds a line a minute while all is well: at a line a second the trace was 1,525 of the 3,216 lines in `q2-log.0` and `.1` and rolled the 1 MB unified log in about 70 minutes of driving (2026-11-22).

```text
... I player car 1003ms: frames=44100 writes=43 wmax=23ms slow=0 dry=0/0ms back=0/0ms avail=5512 delay=16538 rmax=4ms cpu=37ms tap=176400/0 under=0 pos=61s played=2690100
```

| Field | What |
| --- | --- |
| `car Nms` | the window's length: a second for a line with something wrong in it, about a minute for the quiet seconds' line; a write or read that waited makes it that much longer |
| `frames`, `writes` | frames handed to the gadget's PCM, and the writes that took them: 44,100 a second is the car's pace, 66,150 the 1.5 times drain the desk test saw |
| `wmax`, `slow` | the longest write, its wait for room included, and the writes over 50 ms |
| `dry=N/Mms` | times the PCM ran dry and the silence that put in, in ms of audio: `sink` moving past what ALSA played as silence (`snd_pcm_forward`), or an `EPIPE` restart |
| `back=N/Mms` | times written frames were taken back unheard (a seek or a skip, `output_drop`, and the close: way 3's rewind) and how much |
| `avail`, `delay` | the PCM's `snd_pcm_avail` and `snd_pcm_delay` as the line is made (the file sink's: its 500 ms less what is ahead) |
| `rmax` | the slowest source read in the window, real ms: a decode, or a wait for a card app's bytes (`grow.c`) |
| `cpu` | q2player's CPU time in the window, all its threads (`CLOCK_PROCESS_CPUTIME_ID`) |
| `tap=B/L` | the tap's bytes so far this session, and bytes it could not keep |
| `pos`, `played` | the song's position as demo is told it, and the frames heard since the song's start, its last seek or its move to this output (`engine.c` `play_through`): the position moves only once `played` is past 0, so a position stuck at the start beside `played` at or under 0 is the PCM's backlog never draining, and one stuck beside a growing `played` is the report, not the clock |
| `under=N` | times since the player started the PCM ran dry while a song flowed into it (not after a pause, a seek or the open), also written to `/tmp/q2-iap.under` for [the health check](#car-audio-in-the-image) |

**The tap**, for an investigation, off unless `car-pcm.on` is in `/mnt/mmc/.q2muse/logs` (an empty file; delete it to turn it off again). The first 120 s of each car session's PCM, exactly as written to the gadget (S16, the car's rate, stereo: 44.1 kHz, or another the car moved to at a song's change; a session's tap is not cut there), go to `/mnt/mmc/.q2muse/logs/car-pcm.raw`, the two sessions before it kept as `car-pcm.raw.1` and `car-pcm.raw.2`: about 21 MB a session, 63 MB kept. A session is one `/tmp/q2-iap.audio` as the probe wrote it (its inode and mtime): a song after a stop goes on in the same tap, a new `StartUSBDeviceModeAudio` starts it over. The playback thread only copies each write into a 512 KB ring (about 3 s); a thread of the tap's own writes the ring to the card every 100 ms, so a slow card never holds a write to the car, and what does not fit is counted in `tap`'s second number and left out of the file. Measured on this machine (a1 then a2, 39.5 s into the car's PCM at twice real speed, two runs each): q2player's CPU 97 and 99 ms with the tap off, 98 and 118 ms with it on, the longest write 52 ms either way, 6,974,784 bytes tapped and none lost; that is under 1 ms of CPU per second of audio here, and its cost on the Q2 is the difference in `cpu` between a drive with `car-pcm.on` and one without. Read it with `ffmpeg -f s16le -ar 44100 -ac 2 -i car-pcm.raw car-pcm.wav`.

`test/player/run.py`'s `test_car_trace` plays a1 into the car as a card app writes it, a quarter there and the rest landing at 12 s, so the read waits and the PCM runs dry: the lines' frames add up to the car's PCM, the dry second is a line of its own and the quiet seconds lines of `Q2PLAYER_CAR_QUIET_MS` (1.2 s there), the one dry spell's silence is the read's wait at the test's four times speed less the PCM's half second, the underrun count is renamed into place, and with `car-pcm.on` the tap is the car's PCM's first 3 s (`Q2PLAYER_CAR_TAP_S`) frame for frame; `test_car_feed_reaches_the_car` writes no tap without it. The quiet-seconds lines failed the test on the line-a-second code, and the tap's absence on the tap-on-unless-off code. It failed with the file sink's dry count taken out (no dry spell) and with the ring's wrapped copy taken out (the tap not the PCM).

<a id="the-uac1-module"></a>
## The UAC1 module

A real iPhone's configuration 2 is USB Audio Class 1, and the stock kernel has only `f_uac2`. `tools/uac1_module/uac1_build.py` builds `usb_f_uac1.ko` from Linux 5.10.21's `f_uac1.c` with a private copy of `u_audio.c`, whose six exported names `q2_rename.h` changes. A module exporting a name the kernel already has is refused, and Shanling's `u_audio` in the kernel is modified, so the module must never bind to it. The kernel tarball is fetched from kernel.org and its SHA-256 checked against kernel.org's list. The tree is unpacked without the other architectures and most sources (387 MB), configured as `malta_defconfig` plus `q2.config`, and prepared for modules. The compiler is Ubuntu's MIPS cross gcc 12, fetched with `apt-get download` and unpacked under the work directory, never installed: installing it removes `gcc-multilib`, which other test builds need.

`uac1_build.py` fails unless all of these hold:

- `q2.config` was taken whole;
- the vermagic is `5.10.21+ SMP preempt mod_unload MIPS32_R5 32BIT ` (with the stock modules' trailing space);
- `struct module` is 0x180 bytes with `init` at 0xe0 and `exit` at 0x16c, as in `soc_aic.ko`;
- each of the 50 symbols the module needs is a `__ksymtab_` name of the stock kernel;
- the module exports nothing the kernel does;
- 47 offsets and sizes in `q2_layout.ko` equal those read in the stock kernel's own code, each with the instruction it came from (`STOCK` in `uac1_build.py`), and `ep->maxpacket` is loaded as the halfword stock writes.

The stock kernel's symbols come from `vmlinux-to-elf` on `~/q2-emu/audit/kernel/vmlinux.bin`, the same #303 build the Q2 runs.

What the stock kernel was read to have, and what each changes:

| Read in the stock kernel | Option | What it moves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `do_raw_spin_lock`, `do_raw_write_lock` | `DEBUG_SPINLOCK` | `spinlock_t` is 16 bytes, not 4 |
| `srcu_module_notify` but no `jump_label_module_notify` | no `JUMP_LABEL` | `struct module`'s `exit` 0x16c, not 0x174 |
| no `report_bug`, `_mcount` or tracepoints | no `BUG`, `FTRACE`, `TRACING` | `struct module` |
| `snd_power_wait`, standby through `/sys/power/state` | `PM`, `PM_SLEEP` (Malta needs its suspend gate relaxed in the throwaway tree) | `struct device` |
| `pinctrl_bind_pins`, `dma_alloc_from_contiguous` | `PINCTRL`, `DMA_CMA` | `struct device` 0x200 bytes, so `struct snd_pcm_str` 0x220 and `pcm->private_data` 0x51c (stock `g_audio_setup` writes it with `sw $16, 0x51c($4)`; Malta's layout put it at 0x50c, which would have handed ALSA a wrong pointer at the first stream open) |
| `snd_pcm_substream_proc_hw_params_read`; no xrun debug or OSS | `SND_VERBOSE_PROCFS` | `struct snd_pcm_str` |
| `alloc_pages_exact` shifts the size right by 12 | `PAGE_SIZE_4KB` (`malta_defconfig` has 16 kB) | `u_audio.c`'s PCM limits, multiples of the page: a buffer of at most 64 kB in periods of at most 4 kB, where 16 kB pages made them 256 kB and 16 kB |

The check was shown to fail: with `DMA_CMA` dropped, `dev->id` moved to 0x1c8 against the stock 0x1cc and the build failed (padding kept `pcm->private_data` where it was, so that row alone would not have caught it). The stock `uac_pcm_ops` table has the module's slot order (open, close, prepare, trigger, pointer at slots 0, 1, 5, 6, 8).

**At the desk (2026-11-20): the first two binds oopsed.** The module loaded and its function took its attributes, but the bind (the write of the controller's name to `UDC`) ended in an oops in `snd_card_register`: `device_add` found the card's parent no device (`kobject_get` warned, `kobject_namespace` faulted at `0x78bc`), and the writer was killed holding the gadget's lock, so every later gadget call, `q2guard`'s included, waited for ever and the Q2 needed a restart. The first try looked like a hang; `uac1-steps.sh` (each step synced to the card, the stacks of a blocked step dumped) showed the bind's exit 139 and the oops. The cause: Shanling's `struct usb_gadget` has two more 32-bit fields after `max_speed` (where upstream 5.12 put `ssp_rate` and `max_ssp_rate`), so `state` is at 0x34 and `dev` at 0x40, where 5.10.21's header puts them at 0x2c and 0x38; the module passed `snd_card_new` the gadget plus 0x38 as the card's parent. That offset had been printed by `q2_layout.ko` but was not among the stock-checked rows. `uac1_build.py` now adds the two fields in its throwaway tree's `gadget.h`, and checks 47 offsets and sizes against stock code, among them `gadget->dev`, `state`, `max_speed`, `ep0`, the gadget dev's `driver_data`, `cdev->req`, `config->cdev`, and `ep->maxpacket` from the compiled load (a bit-field); without the two fields it fails on `state` and `dev`. Every field of a kernel structure the module's two source files use is now one of the rows.

**At the desk (2026-11-20): every PCM open failed on a Q2 up 15 minutes** (60 of 60, `aplay`: "Unable to install hw params", the kernel: `page allocation failure: order:5`). The module had been compiled for `malta_defconfig`'s 16 kB pages and the Q2's are 4 kB, so `u_audio.c`'s buffer limit (16 pages) was 256 kB where Linux means 64 kB. `aplay` and `q2player` both ask for half a second, 88,200 bytes at 44.1 kHz, which the limit no longer capped; 5.10.21's `u_audio` has ALSA allocate the buffer as contiguous pages at each `hw_params` (`snd_pcm_lib_malloc_pages`, `alloc_pages_exact`), which for 88,200 bytes is a 128 kB block (order 5), and the Q2 had none free (44 of 64 kB, 35 MB free in all; the stock kernel has no memory compaction to make one). `q2.config` now sets 4 kB pages and the page size is one of the stock-checked rows; without the setting the build fails on `q2_size_page 0x4000, stock 0x1000`. Between the two builds the module's code differs only in those limits (`uac_pcm_open`, `g_audio_setup`). The buffer is now at most 64 kB: 16,384 frames, 371 ms at 44.1 kHz. A 64 kB contiguous block (order 4) at every open could still be missing on a fragmented Q2, so `uac1_build.py` also changes the one `snd_pcm_set_managed_buffer_all` call in the module's copy of `u_audio.c` from `SNDRV_DMA_TYPE_CONTINUOUS` to `SNDRV_DMA_TYPE_VMALLOC`: 16 single pages, which `u_audio` can use because it copies samples into each request with `memcpy`. The stock kernel's ALSA has the type (`snd_dma_alloc_pages` calls `__vmalloc`, `snd_dma_free_pages` `vfree`, `snd_pcm_mmap_data_fault` `vmalloc_to_page`).

**Running at the desk (2026-11-20, the usb_gadget fix, before the page size fix):** the bind returned, the card `UAC1Gadget` registered, Windows' own UAC1 driver took the function under `1d6b:0106` (a desk-only ID, `:linuxid`) and recorded the Q2's 1 kHz tone, continuous, no gap in 10 s, while the PCM's `hw_ptr` moved 44,100 frames a second; unbind, removal and adb's return all worked. Windows sends the same endpoint `SET_CUR` for the rate the car does. `tools/iap_probe/uac1-steps.sh` is that desk test (`run` records, `reopen N` counts failed opens), `capture-level.ps1` reads and, for the test's endpoint alone, unmutes Windows' capture level (it came up near zero). A crash costs a restart, nothing is flashed. With the Q2 on USB:

```sh
python3 tools/uac1_module/uac1_build.py                       # the module, all checks ok
tools/iap_probe/q2iap start --plan min:echo:uac1 --for 120 --force   # pushes it; the phase loads it
# 2 minutes later, adb is back:
tools/iap_probe/q2iap pull desk-uac1                     # usb_f_uac1.ko loaded/would not load, dmesg, the uac1 attributes
```

**In the car (2026-11-20, plan `drive`, `full:iap2:uac1`): the BMW chose configuration 2 and stopped.** At each `ENABLE` the kernel logged `configfs-gadget gadget: invalid control req81.06 v2100 i0002 l9`, `f_uac1`'s `f_audio_setup` refusing a request it does not know, and the car never sent iAP2's detect. The request is `GET_DESCRIPTOR` for the HID descriptor (0x21) of interface 2, where a real iPhone's HID is. 5.10.21's `f_uac1.c` makes three interfaces whatever the channel masks (control, AS OUT, AS IN), so with `c_chmask` 0 the module took 0 to 2, the HID was 3, and composite handed the car's request for interface 2 to `f_uac1`, which stalled it. Upstream gave `f_uac1` interfaces only for the directions in use in 5.12 (`254cb1e0d78c`, "usb: gadget: f_uac1: disable IN/OUT ep if unused"); `uac1_build.py` applies it and the commit before it (`a59c68a6a3d1`, which it needs to apply) from `tools/uac1_module/patches/`. With capture off the module has the control interface (0) and AS IN (1), the AC header lists one streaming interface, and the HID is 2. `test/uac1_bind.py KO` binds a built module under Unicorn with the probe's attributes and fails unless the HID is interface 2 and the AC header's `bInCollection`, `baInterfaceNr` and `wTotalLength` agree with the descriptors after it: the module the car ran fails it (HID at 3), and the patched build passes (control 0, AS IN 1, HID 2; `wTotalLength` 30). The desk and the car have not run the patched module yet.

<a id="missed-isochronous-packets"></a>
## Missed isochronous packets

**In the car (2026-11-20, plan `drive`) the sound was garbled and warbly and cut out for half a second to a second at a time.** At the desk the same day a frame counter (each frame its own number; `aplay` into the gadget, the Mac recording through Core Audio's HAL with `sox` and through AVFoundation) came back with the same steady pattern in both recordings: 88 or 89 frames in order (two packets), then 44 or 45 missing (one packet), 500 times a second, while the counter moved 66,149 frames per second of recording, 1.5 times real time. The host got a full packet at every poll; a third of the packets the gadget took from the PCM never reached it. In the car that is a hole every 3 ms (the garble) and the player drained 1.5 times as fast as the car played (it runs dry: the cut-outs). A 1 kHz tone hides it (a 44-frame hole moves its phase by a tenth of a sample), which is why the earlier tone checks passed.

The cause is in `u_audio`, with the controller's part read in the stock kernel. The Q2's `dwc2` has upstream's isochronous flow for buffer DMA (`91bb163e1e4f`, "usb: dwc2: gadget: Fix ISOC flow for BDMA and Slave": `dwc2_gadget_target_frame_elapsed` at 0x804935f4 has its 0x3fff and 0x7ff limits), which gives an IN request whose (micro)frame went by back with `-ENODATA` (-61, `li a3,-61` at 0x804995c8 in `dwc2_hsotg_epint`) and never sends it. 5.10.21's `u_audio_iso_complete` logs the status at debug level and otherwise ignores it: it refills the request from the ring and moves `hw_ptr` on, so the unsent packet's samples are gone and the PCM moves past what the host got. Upstream's `u_audio` still does so. Why `dwc2` misses one interval in three here was not worked out (its target-frame bookkeeping with two requests queued at a 1 ms `bInterval`); the module copes with it whatever the cause.

`uac1_build.py` changes the module's `u_audio.c` (`MISSED`): a playback request given back with a status is queued again unchanged, its buffer still the unsent packet, when the module parameter `resend_missed` is set (the default) and `req_number` is 1. With a second request queued behind it, already holding the samples after it, the packet would go out of order, so with more requests the module only counts. `q2-iap-probe.sh` sets `req_number` 1 (the plan word `:req2` gives upstream's 2, `:noresend` turns the resend off, both for the desk's comparisons) and logs the module's counts, `in_sent` and `in_missed` (playback completions without and with a status, since the module loaded), at each phase's end. `test/uac1_isoc.py KO` runs the module's own completion under Unicorn against a controller that gives back every third request unsent: without the resend (or with two requests) the host's frames are the desk's pattern, 88 or 89 then 44 or 45 skipped, and `hw_ptr` moves 1.5 times what the host got; with the resend and one request the host gets every frame in order and `hw_ptr` moves what it got. It fails on a module built before the change, and failed on the first build of it, which resent with two requests queued (the host's frames out of order, 44 at a time). On the Q2 the resend never fires: its dwc2 gives a packet it could not send back with status 0, not an error, and with one request in flight every other one ([empty packets](#empty-packets)).

## Per-song rate

**The module (branch `feat/uac1-multirate`; not in an image yet, nor run at the desk or in the car).** Upstream's multi-rate UAC1 (in 5.18: rate lists in configfs, `u_audio_set_playback_srate`, a "Playback Rate" ALSA control) sits on the feedback endpoint and volume controls that came before it, which reshape `u_audio`'s structures that `MISSED` and `test/uac1_isoc.py` depend on, so `tools/uac1_module/patches/q2-uac1-playback-rates.patch` does the same in 5.10.21's terms instead. The AS IN format lists the module parameter `p_srates` (32000, 44100 and 48000, the rates the BMW's identification offers) when the configfs `p_srate` is one of them, else `p_srate` alone as before; `q2car.sh`'s 44100 is the rate until the host sets one. The host's `SET_CUR` of the endpoint's sampling frequency (wValue 0x0100, the endpoint in wIndex's low byte) takes a listed rate: the next packet is sized for it (48 kHz: 192 bytes each millisecond), and the PCM opens at it from then on. `GET_CUR` answers the rate in use. `/sys/module/usb_f_uac1/parameters/p_rate` is the rate in use, `rate_sets` the host's rate `SET_CUR`s and `rate_asked` the last rate one carried, listed or not. `test/uac1_bind.py` fails unless the format lists the three rates, and `test/uac1_isoc.py` unless `SET_CUR` 48000 makes 192-byte packets and `GET_CUR` 48000; the pinned module before the patch fails both (one rate; 176 and 180 bytes after the `SET_CUR`, and `GET_CUR` echoes ep0's buffer). **Shipping it alone would be wrong:** a car that sets 48 kHz while the player writes 44.1 kHz plays fast. It goes in with the player's half below, which never writes at another rate than `p_rate`.

**The player's half (built, branch `feat/car-rate`; not yet run in the car).** The player is the rate's source of truth; the car only follows it:

- **`q2player` asks.** At each song on way 3 (`player/output.c` `output_car_song`, from `engine.c` `play_through`) the song's rate is mapped to one of the car's three (`car_rate_for`): 32, 44.1 and 48 kHz as they are; 96 and 192 kHz to 48 kHz and 88.2 and 176.4 kHz to 44.1 kHz, so the resampler's ratio is a whole number; anything else 44.1 kHz. The rate wanted is written to `/tmp/q2-iap.rate` (whole, by a rename, only when it changes). Nothing is asked when `/sys/module/usb_f_uac1/parameters/p_rate` is not there: a one-rate module plays at the car file's rate as before.
- **`q2-iap` tells the car.** `patch/q2_iap.c` `rate_poll` reads the file every 20 ms while the car takes audio (every 250 ms while a car is on the link otherwise, never with none) and hands it to `iap2_session.c` `iap2_audio_rate`, which sends `USBDeviceModeAudioInformation` (`DA01`) with its index (32000 6, 44100 7, 48000 8, the 48 kHz family above it 8, anything else 7; then the same two zero words) at `StartUSBDeviceModeAudio` and again whenever the index changes while the car takes audio. The car file's second line is the rate of the `DA01` sent at the start.
- **At a song for another rate, the player reopens.** `output_take` plays the last song's tail out, then 60 ms of silence (so the car hears silence up to the close: [missed packets](#missed-isochronous-packets)), waits for that to play (at most 600 ms), and only then asks, so the car never changes rate under the tail. It waits up to 500 ms (`CAR_FOLLOW_MS`) for `p_rate` to show the rate, then closes the PCM and opens it again at `p_rate`, and the song is resampled to it if it is not already at it. A car that does not follow keeps its rate: the song plays resampled into the PCM, which stays open, and that rate is not waited for again until another is asked for (an album would wait at each song). The PCM is only ever opened at `p_rate`. Crossfade into a song keeps the car's rate (its tail is already mixed at the new song's rate in `dsp.c`), so a fade never has a reopen in it. A click with nothing playing opens at `p_rate` and asks nothing.
- **A car that sets a rate by itself.** Every 50 ms a song playing on way 3 compares `p_rate` with the PCM's rate (`car_check`); when they differ the PCM's frames not yet heard (they would play off speed) are taken back, it is opened again at `p_rate`, and the song is resampled to it. The rate last asked for then counts as not followed.
- **The trace.** Each change is one line in the unified log beside the trace's lines: `car rate 44100->48000 followed: wait=36ms gap=96ms` (the wait for `p_rate`; the gap, the pad and the wait and reopen, from the tail's end to the new PCM), `... not followed, resampled: wait=501ms`, `... at the open` or `... not followed at the open, resampled` for a PCM opened fresh, and `... set by the car, reopened`.
- **Tests.** `test/player/run.py`'s `test_car_rate_per_song` plays 44.1, 48, 96 and 44.1 kHz songs (a 1 kHz tone each) into the car's way with a simulated car that writes `p_rate` 30 ms after each ask (`Q2PLAYER_CAR_RATE`, `Q2PLAYER_P_RATE`): three PCMs, 44.1, 48 and 44.1 kHz, every 100 ms window of each 1 kHz at its own rate, switches measured at 33 to 36 ms of wait and 93 to 96 ms of gap at four times speed. `test_car_rate_not_followed` (one PCM, the 48 kHz song resampled, one 500 ms wait) and `test_car_rate_set_by_the_car` (the car's 48 kHz mid-song: a new 48 kHz PCM, at speed) cover the rest; `test/iap_probe.py`'s `test_bmw_audio_rate_follows_the_player` the `DA01` indexes. The player before this fails all three (one 44.1 kHz PCM throughout), and `q2-iap` before it sends index 7 for a 48 kHz song.

**Still to do: the car test.** Load the patched module with `q2car.sh` unchanged and play an album at 44.1 kHz then one at 48 kHz: `rate_sets` and `rate_asked` say whether the BMW sets the rate at `StartUSBDeviceModeAudio`, at each `DA01`, or only after re-opening the interface (alt 0, alt 1), which would leave every change "not followed" and the songs resampled (correct speed, no gain); the trace's `car rate` lines give the wait and the gap, the tap (`car-pcm.raw`) the gap as heard, and no pitch change by ear. An iPhone capture (experiment 2) answers the same for a real phone, if one is to hand.

<a id="empty-packets"></a>
## Empty packets: the car's stutter

**Found by the counters (car, 2026-11-21 05:34Z, q2muse-1123):** every second about 1000 packets completed, exactly half of them empty (`zero=500-504`, `frames=22000-22277`), nothing missed or resent, no alternate-setting changes: the host got half the audio while the player's ring was taken at the full rate, so the car played about 300 ms and then waited about 340 ms for more.

**Why:** the Q2's `dwc2` runs isochronous IN in descriptor DMA (its debugfs `params`: `g_dma 1`, `g_dma_desc 1`; stock's `dwc2_init_params` takes the hardware's capability, and the built-in device tree does not ask for buffer DMA). The core runs the chain of descriptors built from the requests queued; a request queued again after the core has fetched the chain's last descriptor is past its end, the core runs off it (BNA), gives that request back with status 0 and actual 0, unsent, and builds the chain afresh. With fewer than three requests in flight every chain ends that way: one empty in two with one request, one in three with two. Synopsys' dwc2 maintainer said the same in 2018 (UAC2's two requests failed in descriptor DMA, four passed every test). The research is in the user's notes (`research-zero-packets.md`).

**Measured on the Q2 at the desk** (2026-11-21, Windows as the host at 48 kHz, plan `min:iap2:uac1:linuxid:tone`, the module's counters over each run):

| `req_number` | completions | empty | bytes a completion | frames a millisecond |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 27,042 | 13,521 (1/2) | 96 | 24 |
| 2 | 27,027 | 9,009 (1/3) | 128 | 32 |
| 3 | 26,037 | 0 | 192 | 48 |
| 4 | 26,036 | 0 | 192 | 48 |
| 8 | 26,038 | 0 | 192 | 48 |

**The cure:** `patch/q2car.sh` gives the function four requests (`req_number=4`): no empty packet, the full rate in ordinary 44- or 45-frame packets (48 at 48 kHz), and 4 ms more latency than one. A second cure was tried and dropped: one request, with an empty-completed packet sent again at once with the next interval's frames after its own (and `wMaxPacketSize` doubled) also reached the full rate at the desk (26,026 completions, 13,013 empty, 192 bytes a completion, packets of 384 bytes), but it gives the car two-millisecond packets and changes the descriptor the car has seen; with four requests it would never act. `test/uac1_isoc.py` models the core's chain (the requests fetched, one queued again with fewer than two left to fetch comes after the end and goes back empty, the chain then rebuilt): with the module, one request loses 450 of 900 completions, two 300, three and four none, the host's frames then in one unbroken run; and it fails while `patch/q2car.sh` gives fewer than three. The isochronous resend ([missed packets](#missed-isochronous-packets)) never fires on this kernel: nothing here completes an IN request with an error.

## The car's stream, counted

and the cable's summary adds the start latency (asked to taken), the longest gap, the alternate-setting toggles and the rate changes. ## The stream's life

Four of 5.10.21's u_audio and f_uac1 paths were wrong for the car's way, and `uac1_build.py` `LIFE` and `LIFE_F` change them (read in the module's own code under Unicorn: `test/uac1_isoc.py` `life_run`, which fails on the module pinned before them):

- **Pause.** The PCM advertises pause, but its trigger zeroed the ring position before its switch and answered `PAUSE_PUSH` and `PAUSE_RELEASE` with `-EINVAL`, so the player's `snd_pcm_pause` on way 3 left the stream running from the ring's start: up to a buffer (16,384 frames, 371 ms at 44.1 kHz) of earlier and later audio out of order in the car after each pause. Now a pause and a suspend keep the position and send silence; their release and resume go on from it; only a start and a stop begin the ring again.
- **Alternate 1 again** while the stream runs queued its four requests a second time, while they were still queued. Now it changes nothing.
- **A disconnect** (`f_audio_disable`: a reset, the host gone, the gadget removed) stopped capture only, so the playback endpoint stayed enabled and its requests were never freed if the gadget went before the next `SET_CONFIGURATION`. Now it stops playback too, and the host's rate goes back to `p_srate`, so the next host starts there and `GET_CUR` says so.
- **`in_eshutdown`** could never count: the completion returned on `-ESHUTDOWN` before the count. It counts there now, with `in_missed`, so a summary's `shutdown` shows a bus drop.

**The gadget's removal while a song is paused into the car.** Unbinding the function (`q2car.sh` `gadget_down`) ends in the kernel's `snd_card_free`, which waits for the card's last open file, and the player keeps the car's PCM open while a song is paused, in a wait only demo's next command ends (`player/engine.c`). On the Q2 (2026-11-22, the PCM held open by a test process) the write of `""` to `UDC` sat in `snd_card_free` for 6.4 s, until the PCM closed, and every read of the gadget's `UDC` waited behind it, q2guard's included; a pick in the USB picker ran that stop on demo's UI thread, which the player's next command could then never come from. **Now** the player is asked to let go first: demo sends `{mcl-carrelease\null}` ([player: Releasing the car](player.md#releasing-the-car)) and looks on its next UI passes, 2 s at most, for `/proc/asound/UAC1Gadget/pcm0p/sub0/status` to read `closed`, then runs `q2car stop` in the background (`usb_car.c` `usb_car_stop`); a picked mode's gadget waits for that stop's return (`/tmp/q2car.stopped`, `usb_car_busy`), never the UI thread. `gadget_down` itself, which a relink after a lost link also runs, sends the player SIGUSR2 and waits for the same `closed`, 2 s at most, logging which it saw. **Tested:** `test/fw/test_usb_car.py` (a pick and a cable out with the PCM held: the release sent, nothing run until it reads closed or 2 s pass, the pick after the stop; no card, no release; a cable back before the release, the stop then the start), red on the old `usb_car.c`; `test/iap_probe.py` `test_the_player_lets_go_of_the_car_pcm_before_the_unbind` and the 2 s and closed cases.

ponytail: `in_pkt_min`, `in_pkt_max` and `in_gap_max` are reset by q2-iap each second while completions update them, so a reset that meets an update spoils that one window's figure. At full speed the endpoint's `bInterval` of 4 (8 ms) would cut 44.1 kHz to what fits `wMaxPacketSize` 200; the car and Windows run the gadget at high speed.

## The link's pace

**The drive of 2026-11-21 (q2muse-1124):** the first song played with its cover; at the first track change the iAP2 link died, and nothing the car picked reached the Q2 after it. The log: the BMW's link parameters are a window of 5, a 200 ms retransmission timer and 10 tries. It takes the Q2's input reports from an interrupt endpoint, 64 bytes every 8 ms, so a 95-byte report is 16 ms and a 1,024-byte packet about 170 ms on the wire. The library push filled the window with 1,024-byte updates (most of a second on the wire), and the Q2 sent each again after 200 ms counted from when it handed it over, not from when it had left: the copies queued behind the originals, the car's acknowledgements came one every 170 to 200 ms, and the Q2's acknowledgement of the car's own packet (its "go" for the next cover, seq 49 from 344.071) waited behind them. The car sent that packet 11 times over 2 s, gave the link up at 346.24, stopped the USB audio at 347.2, and the Q2 then sent its own seq 189 ten times unanswered and called the link lost at 348.263; the car sent nothing more until the cable came out two minutes later. The library had gone 67 updates in 43 s, never to its end, and "identified no" in the summary was the flag cleared with the link, not a refused identification (`1D02` went at 298.480). Nothing was dropped or interleaved: every report goes through one pipe to one writer, in the order the single event loop hands them over, a packet's reports together, and no write was refused (no `writer is behind` line). The acknowledgements arrived, late: at 344.271 the acknowledgement of seq 49 sat behind more than a hundred reports of 1,024-byte packets and their copies, about 1.8 s of wire, past the car's 2 s of tries. The 1123 session ended otherwise: a `SET_REPORT` whose data never came held q2-iap in its read for 35 s (`read -1 (errno 4, 35361 ms)`).

**Now:** the writer child says when each report has gone (a byte on a pipe; `io_backlog`), and `iap2_link.c` sends a packet only while no more than 4 reports wait for the wire (a bare acknowledgement always goes: one report), sends one again only after the wire has been idle for the retransmission time, and sends the bulk (library updates, covers, the queue list) in packets of at most 768 bytes, so an acknowledgement waits behind about 130 ms at most, inside the car's 200 ms. A lost link with `--state` ends q2-iap with 22 and `q2car.sh` shows the car the iPhone again (the gadget off the controller and back, three times a cable at most), which is how a car starts afresh. The data stage of a request is read for 2 s at most (`DATA_STAGE_S`). The summary's "identified" is the cable's.

**The drive of 2026-11-21 (q2muse-1125):** the library's push ended (`library: whole sent`, 34110.909) and q2-iap did nothing else for 12.3 s: the record of what the car holds, 9,200 lines each in a write of its own, went to `/mnt/data`, which is UBIFS mounted `sync` ([hardware](hardware.md)). The car's next `SET_REPORT` waited unanswered meanwhile; its data stage then came back as 8 bytes of 14, `01 0b 00 00 01 00 00 00`, the car's next request read as data: `SET_INTERFACE`, alternate setting 0 of interface 1, which is the UAC1 function's streaming interface (`q2car.sh` links the audio function first, interfaces 0 and 1, the HID 2). The car had given the iPhone up and stopped its USB audio; the request never reached the UAC1 module (no alternate-setting change in the `car usb` lines), so the Q2 went on streaming to a car no longer playing it. From then to the cable out 23 minutes later the car sent nothing and took no input report. The reports already handed to the writer never went, so pacing sent no packet, 2,555 were dropped from a full queue, and the count of tries, which waits for a clear wire, never ran: the link stayed up, silent. The USB audio's packets kept going until 34282.6 (none silent), but nothing was heard from about 34116: by the request above, the car had stopped the stream at its side somewhere between 34111 and 34123. **Now:** the record is made in RAM and written in one write by a child (`q2_iap_lib.c` `lib_saved`, which logs `library: the sent record written, N bytes, T ms`); a `SET_REPORT` whose data stage is shorter than asked or not of the report it names is the link lost; and so is a link with something waiting (a packet queued or unacknowledged, or reports not yet taken) and nothing from the car for 10 s (`link_quiet_ms`, `--quiet-ms`), each through exit 22 and the iPhone shown again. **Tested** (`test/iap_probe.py`): `test_writing_the_sent_record_holds_nothing` (the record's temporary name a FIFO that is read only after the car's packet: acknowledged within 400 ms; with the old write, never), `test_a_request_read_as_data_shows_the_iphone_again`, `test_a_link_gone_quiet_with_packets_waiting_is_lost` (ep1's pipe filled, the car silent: exit 22 after 1.5 s; with the old code q2-iap ran on), and `test_a_quiet_car_with_nothing_waiting_keeps_the_link`. Not settled from the Q2's side: whether the car would have spoken again over a working ep0, or whether the controller's ep0 was left out of step (the dmesg of that cable had rolled past it; `q2car.sh` saves `dmesg` at each relink, in `q2car.log.dmesg`, so the next one keeps the controller's lines). **Tested** (`test/iap_probe.py`, the writer slowed to the bus's pace with `--report-ms`): `test_the_link_keeps_to_the_car_s_pace`, with the library flowing at 16 ms a report the car's packet is acknowledged within 400 ms, three times, no packet sent again and the link kept (with the old pacing, 1.9 s and then never, as in the car); `test_a_lost_link_shows_the_iphone_again` (exit 22, "identified yes"); `test_a_data_stage_that_never_comes_holds_nothing`; the library's push and resume above.

While Car Audio has a car on the cable, Bluetooth's auto-connect (at a stack relaunch, at Bluetooth on) leaves that car alone, matched by the name it gives over iAP2 ([internals](internals.md#bluetooth-connection-trace)): its audio already comes over USB.

<a id="other-sources-over-usb"></a>
### Other sources over USB

Car Audio's USB stream is the player's (`player/output.c` `output_car`). Radio, Internet Radio and Videos play through q2video, and Spotify through librespot's sink (q2video too), each to the device demo names (`videos.c` `output_device`). On the drive of 2026-11-22 Radio's station reached iDrive's screen over silence: its sound went to Bluetooth A2DP while the car listened to USB. Radio is not an analogue tuner: its sound is q2video's, a station's stream through a modelled FM receiver ([internals: Radio static](internals.md#radio-static)), PCM like the rest. **Now** the player stays the car card's one owner and plays the others' sound on to it:

- **The device.** While Car Audio takes the audio (the iPhone gadget on the cable and q2-iap's `/tmp/q2-iap.audio` there), `output_device` names `q2car` with the volume `h100`, unity, the car's knob its volume. `q2car` (`assets/alsa/30-q2car.conf`, in the image as `/usr/share/alsa/alsa.conf.d/30-q2car.conf`, `tools/build.py` `CAR_FEED_CONF`) is a `plug` to 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo (a 48 kHz source resampled there) over a `file` PCM that writes raw frames to `/tmp/q2car.feed` with a `null` slave.
- **The feed** (`player/feed.c`). The player makes that FIFO at its start and holds it open both ways, so a source's open never waits and its reads never end; a thread reads it into a 100 ms ring as the car takes the frames, so the car paces the source. Frames nobody takes for 300 ms (the car not reading, the player's own song playing) are read and dropped at the source's own rate, so a source is never held and nothing grows.
- **The session** (`engine.c` `feed_session`). With nothing of its own playing and the car's way the output, the player opens the car's PCM at 44.1 kHz as for a song and writes the ring's frames; a dry ring is the silence the never-stopped PCM plays (`alsa_sw`), and the same close writes silence before the PCM stops (no repeated packet's buzz). It writes only 150 ms ahead of the car (`FEED_LEAD_MS`, as a scan's lead), not the PCM's half second, so the sound trails a video's picture by the FIFO's 23 ms, the ring's 100 ms and that lead, under 0.3 s, where it was about 0.62 s: on the host the PCM was at most 172 ms ahead (500 ms before), and never ran dry in three runs with every core busy (`test/player/run.py` `test_car_feed_keeps_the_car_close_behind`, 2026-11-22; not yet measured on a Q2). Its writes are traced as a song's ([the car's trace](#car-trace)). A song, a click, another way or 2 s with nothing from the source (it ended, paused or died) ends it. Radio, Internet Radio and a video stop the Library first (`now_hold`, `player_stop`), so the handover either way is a close and an open, as between songs of different rates.
- **With nothing to play to.** The no-output rule ([internals](internals.md#no-playback-without-an-output)) still guards Radio's, an Internet Radio station's and a video's start; the car's USB audio counts as an output there (`usb_car_output`), so with no jack and no Bluetooth such a start plays into the car with no toast, and with no output at all it is refused with the rule's one toast.
- **The car's audio gone** under one of them (`usb_car.c` `usb_car_gone`): it is paused, whatever else is connected, as its sound was going to the car's feed and cannot follow to another output mid-stream.
- **Latency.** Between a source's write and the car: the FIFO (4,096 bytes, 23 ms), the ring (100 ms at most) and the car's PCM (its buffer, about 0.36 s as the car trace shows it), about 0.5 s. q2video's clock is the `q2car` device's, which holds nothing, so a video's picture leads its sound by that much in the car. ponytail: a video's lips; telling q2video the player's delay would mend it.
- **Tested:** `test/player/run.py` `test_car_feed_*` (a source's frames whole at 44.1 kHz in the car's PCM; a1, the source, a1 as three PCMs; a pause as a dry spell in one PCM; the source dying mid-stream and a1 after it; a source the car does not take dropped at its own rate), `test_alsa_sw_params` (way 3's close ends on a write of silence); `test/fw/test_radio.py` and `test_now_source.py` (Radio and Internet Radio exec q2video on `q2car` at `h100`), `test_usb_car.py` (a source paused at the car's audio going); the `q2car` definition under qemu with the image's libasound and `aplay`: 2 s of a 48 kHz tone came out as 88,191 frames at 44.1 kHz. ponytail: measured on no Q2 yet; the extra copy's CPU is unmeasured; Spotify's sink takes the same device (`spotify.c` `spot_output`) with no test of its own.

### Nowhere to play

**The cable out of 2026-11-21 (18:03:17):** the player went to the DAC (`output way 0 now (the car's audio gone)`) and played on, no jack in, for 2 h 09 min, the battery down 6.5 % an hour. The car's way is Q2 Muse's, the player's own, and stock never knew it. **Now** (`patch/usb_car.c` `usb_car_gone`): at a cable out, and when the car stops asking for audio while the cable stays, a song playing is paused where it is, through stock's pause (Play resumes it, and the DAC powers down after its own timer), when stock's `has_valid_output_device` finds nothing else (either jack, a Bluetooth link with its codec up, a USB DAC); with headphones in or Bluetooth linked it plays on there. The car's audio is itself an output to that check while the car asks for it or a relink may bring it back (`has_valid_output_device`'s hook, `ringnav_valid_output`: [no playback without an output](internals.md#no-playback-without-an-output)), so Headphones Required, always on, refuses nothing the car would play; `usb_car_gone` asks only once it has marked the car's audio gone. **Tested** (`test/fw/test_usb_car.py`): nothing connected pauses once; headphones in or Bluetooth linked plays on; a song not playing is left; the car stopping its audio pauses; a relink whose audio comes back never pauses, one that ends without it pauses, as does one past 30 s (the pauses red on the old `usb_car.c`); `test/iap_probe.py` the note written at the loss and removed at the audio's return.

<a id="the-loop-s-steps"></a>
### The loop's steps

So that the next stall or dead link names its cause in `q2car.log` (q2-iap's lines; the cars' logs are kept by size and age, as above):

- **A stall.** Each pass of the event loop is cut into named steps (`q2_iap.c` `loop_step`: an ep0 event's read, a request's data stage, an answer on ep0, the rate file, iAP2's tick, and within it the Now Playing file, the cover's read, the library's read and record, the car's audio file, the shim's files); a pass that holds the loop 100 ms or more (`--stall-ms`) is a line, `loop: a pass held N ms, M of it in <step>; the slowest log write K ms` (the log is on the card), and one of 1 s or more adds the kernel's USB lines. The library's end is timed apart (`library: its end taken in N ms`), and the record's writer says its own time.
- **ep0.** Every SETUP is a line as before but a `SET_REPORT` that carries the car's bare acknowledgement and took under the stall mark (counted in the link's health line); a read of ep0 that is not a whole event is one (`ep0: read N of an event's 12 bytes`), and so is a `SET_REPORT` whose data stage never came (nothing taken, the loop goes on) or was not its report (the link lost). The answer to an IN request has the data stage's 2 s too, and one ep0 does not take in them is a lost link, `link: lost (an answer on ep0 not taken in 2 s)` (`test_an_answer_ep0_does_not_take_is_a_lost_link`). A SYN that offers a maximum packet under 128 bytes is not taken (`MIN_PEER_MAX`: at 12 a cover's piece had no room and the car's go spun the loop for good; no car offers so little; `test_a_tiny_max_packet_is_not_taken`), and a SYN's parameters are logged only once its length is checked.
- **The link.** Every 5 s while it is up (`--health-ms`), `link: health:` the reports the writer holds, the packets queued and unacknowledged, how long since the Q2 sent, since the car sent anything and since it acknowledged something new, the packets dropped from a full queue (said once when the queue fills, then counted here) and the quiet against its 10 s. A packet past all its tries while the wire is busy, when the tries cannot count, is said once (`link: retries held`). A lost link says why, and whether q2-iap ends for it (`the iPhone shown again`) or goes on (no `--state`).
- **The kernel.** At a lost link and after a stall of 1 s or more, the last 24 of the kernel's USB lines (dwc2, FunctionFS, the gadget, the UAC1 module) still in its ring buffer (`klogctl`), at most once in 10 s; `q2car.sh` saves `dmesg` again at each relink and at the cable's end.
- **The car's audio.** The car stopping its stream is a change of the streaming interface's alternate setting, counted by the UAC1 module (`alt=+N/-N` in the `car usb` lines) when the request reaches it.

**Tested** (`test/iap_probe.py`): `test_a_stall_names_its_step` (a cover's file that holds its read 400 ms), `test_ep0_s_odd_reads_are_said`, `test_a_silence_is_seen_as_it_grows` (the health lines' ages growing, the tries held, the kernel's lines at the loss), `test_a_lost_link_without_state_says_it_goes_on`.

<a id="the-library-on-idrive"></a>
**Its children hold no ep0.** Every fd q2-iap opens is close-on-exec and each child that does not exec (the report writer, the library's read, the stream-path index, the sent record's writer, the cover's search) closes the ep0s at its start (`child_drop`), so ffmpeg or a child still running at a lost link's teardown never keeps FunctionFS busy (`test/iap_probe.py` `test_the_children_hold_no_ep0`); at its end q2-iap closes the ep0s before it syncs its log to the card.

**How long the loop sleeps.** `poll` waits for the car's requests on ep0, the writer's reports gone, the children's pipes (the library's, the cover's, the stream-path index's) and, at most, until the soonest deadline (`q2_iap.c` `loop_due` with `iap2_link.c` `iap2_due`: a packet's retry, the cumulative ack, the link's quiet and health, the handshake's answers, a held button's scan, a list transfer's wait, Now Playing's look every 500 ms, the rate file, the controller's state every 100 ms until a host addresses the Q2, the verdict's window, the audio's health, the session's gap), 1 s at most. It woke every 10 ms whatever was due: on a charger (a host that never addresses the Q2) 191 wakeups in 2 s on this machine, and 194 with a car identified and its link idle; now 19 and 8 (`test/iap_probe.py` `test_the_loop_sleeps_until_something_is_due`, counted from `/proc`'s voluntary context switches).

## The library on iDrive, playing from it, and Up Next

The user's decision (2026-11-20) replaces the design's decision 4, an empty library: iDrive gets the whole library and plays from it. `patch/q2_iap_lib.c` reads stock's `songtable` (`/mnt/data/database.db`, read-only; audiobooks, podcasts and `.m4b` left out, as Car Remote's library does) into RAM when the car sends `StartMediaLibraryUpdates`, in a child (`lib_load`: the loop answers `8 00` and `9 01` at once and goes on; the child hands the tables over a pipe, which the loop drains every pass, 64 KB at most a pass (`HANDOVER_MAX`; the stream-path index's child sorts its rows before it sends them, so taking them in is no sort and no copy), and the updates begin once they are in; on the 2026-11-22 drive the read in the loop held it 648 ms, `test/iap_probe.py` `test_the_library_s_read_does_not_hold_the_loop`), and `iap2_library.c` sends it as PodBox does: `8 00`, `9 01`, then `MediaLibraryUpdate`s with each song as an item (its id `0x51320001` and its rid, title, album, track and disc, artist, album artist and genre, each name with an id that is its hash under a high word of its own, so iDrive groups albums, artists and genres), as many to a packet as fit, the first with a reset, the last with the revision and progress 100. The songs are the card's (`songtable`) and, while a server is signed in, the server's (`servertable` in `/mnt/mmc/.q2pod/catalogue.db`, attached as Car Remote's library attaches it, with each song's length from `servermeta`): the set the Q2's own Library shows, the catalogue already without the card's duplicates. Composers go too (17 and 18) and play as a collection of type 5. **Lists:** Favorites (stock's `myfav`), Recently Played (`recentplay`, the last played first) and stock's playlists that hold library songs go as playlists (parameter 3: the list's id under `0x5132000F`, its name, a file transfer id), each list's songs then as a file transfer of type 4 (`[ID, 04, size, 00 04, the list's 8-byte id, the library id]`, the songs' ids in order when the car says go), a list at a time, as PodBox's iPhone sends them; a list plays from iDrive by its songs' ids (4C07) or as a collection of type 0. **Revisions:** the revision is the songs' rids and hashes hashed (`q2.<count>.<hash>`); a car that holds it is sent the revision and the lists alone; the songs and their hashes as last sent whole are kept in `/mnt/data/q2-iap.library` (written in one write by a child when the whole has gone; a checkpoint writes nothing, [the link's pace](#the-link-s-pace)), and a car that holds that revision is sent only the songs added or changed and, as parameter 4, the ids of those gone, with no reset. <a id="in-the-car-after-a-standby"></a>
## In the car after a Standby: the owner's checklist

For the next drive with Standby on ([internals: Standby and the USB link](internals.md#standby-and-the-usb-link)). Nothing here needs a computer; Send Logs afterwards takes `q2car.log`, `q2car-summary.log` and the unified log.

1. **Before parking:** play something from the Q2 through the car (USB), and note the library's song count iDrive shows (or that `q2car.log` gives in `library:` lines).
2. **Park with the cable in.** Turn the car off and leave the Q2 on the cable, nothing playing (pause it from iDrive or the Q2 first). Lock the car and walk away for at least 10 minutes, longer than the Q2's Standby time, so the Q2 goes into Standby.
3. **Wake it:** come back and press a key on the Q2. Note the time.
4. **Start the car.** Within a minute iDrive should show the iPhone again, by itself, with no cable handling.
5. **Audio flows:** press Play on iDrive. The song plays through the car's speakers.
6. **The cover is there** on iDrive for the song playing.
7. **The library count is unchanged** from step 1 (the server's songs included).
8. **Pause from iDrive**, wait a few seconds, then Play: listen for a jump back or forward, or a scrap of another part of the song, right after each press. There should be none.
9. **Write down** anything that did not happen, with the time, and Send Logs.

What the logs should show: `standby: Car Audio stopped for it` before the sleep, `standby: the link back, the card mounted: udc ..., role device` after it, a new `start:` in `q2car.log`, and the car's `car usb` lines with `sent` near 1000 and `zero=0` while it plays.
