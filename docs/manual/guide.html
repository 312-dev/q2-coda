Getting Started

1

Get to your first song in about ten minutes: install Q2 Muse, plug in headphones, copy music and play an album.

Q2 Muse brings the iPod classic experience to the Shanling Q2 and runs on top of the stock firmware. Spotify, AirPlay, Internet Radio and Rockbox are optional extras, covered in Chapters 5 and 9. The features list is at the end of this chapter.

Start here: play your first song

Follow these steps in order. If something doesn’t look right, the linked section explains more.

  1. Install Q2 Muse (Installing Q2 Muse). The Q2 restarts and opens on Q2 Muse’s Home screen.
  2. Plug in your headphones. Any 3.5 or 4.4 mm headphones will do. (For Bluetooth, see Chapter 8.)
  3. Copy your music to the card over USB-C (Adding music). Your album folders should now be on the card.
  4. Eject the card on your computer before you unplug the Q2. This makes sure the copy has finished.
  5. Scan your library. On the Q2, choose Library → Update Local Music. When it’s done, Albums, Artists and Songs fill in.
  6. Pick an album. Go to Library → Albums and press Center on one. It starts playing and Now Playing opens.
  7. Learn the controls in Essential controls.

The rest of this chapter and the later chapters are reference.

Installing Q2 Muse

What you need: a Shanling Q2 with a SD card, a USB-C cable, and a computer with an internet connection.

Important: Charge the Q2 before you start. Only insert or remove the SD card while the Q2 is fully shut down (not asleep), or the card can be corrupted.

Install Q2 Muse:

  1. Download update.tar from the latest release at github.com/DiamondBond/q2-pod/releases/latest.
  2. Plug the Q2 into your computer with the USB-C cable. The card shows up as a drive.
  3. Copy update.tar to the root of the card (not inside a folder), then eject and unplug.
  4. Update: on the Q2, open Settings → General → Software Update and choose Install. Leave the card in until the Q2 restarts, and don’t unplug it while it updates.

Tip: Still on the old Legacy build? Software Update can’t move you to this one over Wi-Fi. Install update.tar by hand once: copy it to the card’s root and update, as for a first install.

Finding your way around

  • Home: a list (Playing or Paused, there while a song is playing or paused, with moving bars or a pause sign before it, Library, Streaming, Folders, there once the SD card has folders, Cover Flow, Search, Settings) beside the playing track’s cover; the cover follows the row you’re on, the playing album’s on Playing and your library’s covers in turn on the others. Settings lists Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, General (About, System Update, Storage, Date & Time, App Store, Network, Reset Settings, Restart and Shut Down), Playback, Car Remote, Display, Controls and Battery. It stops at either end. A short Return on it selects its first row, and on Playing or Paused another opens Now Playing without changing playback; Return there goes back to Home. Settings always stays. Folders, once shown or hidden here, stays that way.

The wheel moves through the cards, Center opens one, and Return works as it does on Shanling’s own Home.
  • Settings: slides Playback and System into the list’s place, as an iPod’s submenu, with the cover still beside it. It stops at its ends too; Return goes back.
  • Folder: opens the SD card directly when no OTG drive is connected. Return at the SD root goes back to Home; inside a folder it goes up one level. With OTG connected when Folder opens, choose SD or OTG first. A folder set in Playback → Library & Folders → Folder Root opens directly either way; Return climbs from it to the SD root, then to Home, or to the SD/OTG choice with OTG connected.
  • Lists: four rows per screen with a full-width accent bar; > marks rows that open another list.
  • Status bar: play state, EQ, time, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and battery. The Bluetooth codec (AAC, LDAC…) shows briefly on connect. Under the OLED theme, right to left: the battery, moving level bars while a song plays (tap them for Now Playing), Wi-Fi's signal, a streaming app's sync, Bluetooth (a headset's battery, a car) and the Buttons Lock padlock.

  • Long titles: pause on a row and a title too long to fit scrolls so you can read it.
  • Fast-scroll letter: spinning a long list shows the letter it sorts under (C for The Cure). A list with the A-Z index shows it on the index instead.
  • Artists: open on Albums, with Songs one tap away.
  • Starting up: the Q2 starts on Home. Memory Playback → Location restores your queue, paused; Track restarts the song, unless it resumes. In-Vehicle Mode starts playing; with Headphones Required on and nothing connected, it restores the queue paused.

What Q2 Muse adds

With Q2 Muse you can:

  • Browse with an iPod-style Home, menus, Now Playing, Cover Flow and games, in your choice of Home layout and battery display, with Light Mode and Night Shift for day and night
  • Scroll with a wheel that follows your finger, in menus that remember where you were
  • Shape the sound with three simple tone sliders, or up to 30 EQ bands with a live curve and AutoEQ and Equalizer APO presets
  • Stream Spotify (Premium), AirPlay from your iPhone, and Internet Radio, and install apps and updates over Wi-Fi
  • Send your listens to Last.fm or ListenBrainz over Wi-Fi
  • Rate songs 0–5 stars and like them with the heart, then browse Top Rated, Loved and favorite albums
  • Use favorites, playlists you can edit, Years, Most Played, Long Songs, Play Next, Crossfade, and shuffle by song, album or folder, and pick up the queue where you left it
  • Read synced lyrics, or download lyrics and covers for your songs over Wi-Fi
  • Resume .m4b audiobooks (also split by a CUE sheet) and podcasts where you stopped, with per-folder playback speed, and keep videos on the card
  • Use AirPods stem controls, ear detection, two-way volume sync and smoother AAC audio
  • Look after the battery with an optional 80% charge limit, a Standby limit and a sleep timer

Q2 Muse also fixes the clock resetting after power-off.

Library and Music

2

Put your music on the SD card, build the library, and organize it with ratings, playlists and shuffle.

Adding music

Add music to the Q2:

  1. Copy your music to the card over USB-C.
  2. On the Q2, choose Library → Update Local Music.

Over Wi-Fi: install the free LocalSend app on your phone or computer, join the same Wi-Fi as the Q2, and open Settings → General → Network → LocalSend on the Q2. Send from the app and pick the Q2 from its list; the Q2 asks before it takes anything (turn on Always Accept to skip that next time from the same device). Files land in the Received folder on the card, and songs are added to the library. Send Logs and a device's row send the other way. The Q2 can only be found while that page is open.

These folders at the card’s root each add their own Library entry: Podcasts/, Audiobooks/ and Videos/; EQ/ holds EQ presets. Other folder names, such as Images/, aren’t picked up. See What goes on the SD card for supported formats.

Update Local Music no longer stops at 20,000 songs, so with a larger library run it again after updating. Shanling rates the Q2 for 20,000 songs: a much larger library takes longer to scan and browse, and isn’t guaranteed to work.

What the Library lists: every song a scan finds, loose files beside your album folders included. On a very large card, Songs, Albums and Artists may not list everything; Folder view still plays every file. To list only part of a card, scan just those folders with Scan Folders, below.

A large library: Update Local Music goes through every folder on the card before it starts counting songs, so with tens of thousands of songs or a slow card it can stay at 0 songs found for a long time. Leave it running; Stop or Return stops it. When it ends, the page says Library Updated with how many songs are new, then closes by itself. To add new music later without going through the whole card again, choose Library → Update Library → Scan Specified Folders, tick the folders you copied to, and press Scan. Open a folder, such as Music, to tick the artist or album folders inside it; tapping a folder that has no folders inside ticks it. Ticking the whole folder reads every song in it again, which takes about as long as a full update.

Finding a song: in Library, pull the list down from the top until “Release to search” appears, then type. In Cover Flow, turn fast to jump one initial at a time.

Your own order (Theme: OLED): hold Center on the Library, or hold a row with your finger, for Edit Library. Drag a row by the grip (≡) on its right, or press Center to pick up the selected row, turn the wheel to move it and press Center to put it down; Return while carrying a row puts it back where it was. Press Play/Pause, or tap the circle on a row’s left, to show or hide it: Shuffle, Hi-Res, Long Songs and Upload Scrobbles are hidden until you show them. Return, or holding Center again, finishes. The order and your choice are kept through restarts; Home’s Library list follows them.

Search: Library → Search (or Search on Home) opens a strip of letters along the bottom of the screen, with no keyboard.

  • Typing: turn the wheel to a letter (then the digits, a space and delete) and press Center to type it at the cursor. Or drag the strip with a finger to move along it, and tap a letter to type it. The letter under the highlight pops up enlarged as it arrives.
  • Editing: Previous and Next move the cursor along what you typed; Return erases the letter before the cursor.
  • Results: the songs that match appear above as you type, then the artists, then the albums. Press Next at the end of the text to move into them; turn to pick one and press Center to play a song (the songs listed after it play next) or open an artist or album. Return or Previous goes back to the letters.
  • Tabs: in the results, All, Songs, Artists and Albums sit at the top. Turn up from the first result to reach them, and press Center (or Next and Previous) to change tab. All shows up to 32 of each kind; the other tabs show every match. The tab stays as you keep typing.
  • Leaving: hold Return.

Hiding a folder: to keep a folder out of Songs, Albums, Artists, Cover Flow and library shuffle, put an empty file named database.ignore in it and run Update Local Music again. Its songs still play from Folder view and from playlists, so a folder of singles or needledrops stays reachable without filling your library. An empty database.unignore in a subfolder lets that subfolder back in. Name the markers .database.ignore and .database.unignore, with a leading dot, if other players shouldn’t list them. Remove the marker and update the library to show the folder again.

Settings, play counts, ratings and resume points are stored on the Q2 itself, not the card, so they stay put when you swap cards.

Tagging your music

Q2 Muse builds the library from your files’ tags, so a few tag habits decide how albums and songs show up. Fix tags on a computer (Mp3tag, MusicBrainz Picard or foobar2000), then run Update Local Music and press Settings → Display → Cover Flow → Refresh Cover Flow.

  • An album is its name plus its Album Artist. Cover Flow, Library → Albums, Album groups, Crossfade and album ratings all go by these two tags. Give every track on an album the same Album Artist. A track with a different one, for example “Artist; Guest” where the rest say “Artist”, becomes an album of its own. So does a track that has no Album Artist when the others do. Put guests in the track’s Artist or Title instead, and use one Album Artist such as “Various Artists” for a compilation.
  • No Album Artist on any track: the album’s folder tells it apart instead, and disc folders (CD1, Disc 2) count as one album. Two albums with the same name in one folder then become one album, so give each album its own folder or an Album Artist.
  • Artists list versus albums: Settings → Playback → Library → Artists picks what the Artists list groups by, Album Artist (the default) or Artist. That only decides which artist rows you see. Each album is still identified by its name and Album Artist, so a choice in the Artists list never merges or splits albums.
  • Long genre lists are fine. As many genres as fit are kept. On Now Playing’s info page, a long Genre line scrolls.
  • A song showing genres as its title: run Update Local Music once. A song already in a playlist can keep the old title until you add it again.

Rating songs and albums

Every song can have 0 to 5 stars, and, apart from them, you can like it.

  • Rate a song: on Now Playing, swipe past the lyrics to the stars. Turn the wheel to choose and press Center to save, or tap a star; tapping the rating it already has takes it away. This is the only place to rate.
  • Like a song: tap the heart on Now Playing (or choose Hold Play/Pause → Favorite). A like is not a rating: it gives no stars, and five stars don’t make a song a favorite. Loved and Home’s Favorites shortcut list your liked songs. On a song from a card app that supports it, such as Jellyfin, the heart also makes it a favorite in the app, whether it streams or was synced to the card; the app sends it on the next time it runs.
  • Library → Ratings lists Top Rated (4 and 5 stars), Loved (your liked songs), All Rated and Disliked (1 star), most stars first. A row plays the list from that song.
  • Ratings are kept by file path, so they survive Update Local Music but not moving or renaming the file.
  • A copy of your song and album ratings stays on the card as .ratings.tsv, to read on a computer. It is updated soon after a change, and it is only a copy: editing it changes nothing on the Q2.

Albums can be favorites too.

  • Like an album: in Cover Flow (also an artist’s), Library → Album or an artist’s albums, hold Play/Pause on the album. Favorite makes all its songs favorites, Unfavorite takes them out.
  • Library → Ratings → Albums shows your favorite albums (and albums rated before ratings moved to the stars) in Cover Flow. Its Sort card cycles Rating, Album, Artist, Year (as Years) and Most Played, and is remembered. Return goes back to Ratings.
  • An album is its name with its album artist, or its folder when it has none (disc folders count as one), so a favorite survives Update Local Music, but retagging or moving the album starts it over.

Ear detection

Removing a worn bud pauses playback, and putting the buds back resumes what ear detection paused. A manual playback command, a different player or track, or a disconnect cancels that automatic resume. The first ear report after connecting doesn’t start playback, and with both buds out playback stays paused. Radio resumes by restarting the current station.

Volume

The Q2’s volume is kept when the headset connects. After that, changing the volume on the headset updates the Q2 and its volume controls, within the configured volume limit; zero mutes. A headset that can’t take the volume gets the Q2’s own software gain instead. AirPlay and DLNA keep their own receiver controls.

Updating, Restoring and Rockbox

9

Keep Q2 Muse up to date, go back to Shanling’s firmware, or run Rockbox alongside Q2 Muse.

Updating Q2 Muse

Repeat the install steps in Chapter 1 with the newest release. Your settings, ratings and play counts are kept. The updater skips a version that’s already installed, so a newer release always goes on.

Settings → General → Software Update looks online and on the SD card at once (“Checking for an Update…”, a few seconds). When there is a newer release it shows its version, where it is (“From the internet” or “From the SD card”) with its size, and the version installed, with Download and Install or Install. When both have one, the online one comes first and Install from SD Card instead under the button switches to the card’s; turn the wheel to reach it. With Wi-Fi off only the card is checked, and Turn on Wi-Fi to check online opens the Wi-Fi settings. If the online check fails (no internet, or no answer within about 10 seconds), the card’s result still shows, with Couldn’t check online · Try Again under it.

Install asks Install Q2 Muse 1.8.3? with the file’s size and the version installed. Install and Restart unpacks it (“Restarting to Install”, with a bar) and restarts the Q2, which installs it in about 2 minutes; Cancel, selected when it opens, leaves everything as it was. Instead of an update it says You’re Up to Date when there’s nothing newer, Charge to Update below 16% battery with no cable in, Not Enough Space with less than 100 MB free on the card (it unpacks there), and Update Can’t Be Used for a card file that is damaged (an unfinished copy) or isn’t a Q2 firmware.

Download and Install downloads the newest q2-coda release to the card’s update.tar, replacing any that’s there, with its progress on the page, then checks it and asks as above. Press Return to cancel the download. Each release is signed, and the Q2 checks it (“Verifying…”) before the update can start: a release that isn’t signed by q2-coda, such as Q2 Muse’s own, or one that was changed after signing, isn’t installed, and the message says so.

Installing apps over Wi-Fi

Settings → General → App Store installs and updates Rockbox, the Rockbox themes, Spotify and the card apps from their GitHub releases, instead of unzipping them by hand. Its list of apps, signed by q2-coda, names the exact download for each app, and the Q2 installs nothing else: a list that isn’t signed, or a download that isn’t the one it names, is refused with a message. The list is fetched over Wi-Fi and kept on the card, so the first time it needs Wi-Fi to show any; until then Check for Updates is the one row, and pressing it loads the list. Each app’s row shows Install, Update or Installed; with Wi-Fi on, the page fetches the list again when it opens (at most every 10 minutes), and a newer release shows as Update once q2-coda adds it to the list. Rockbox Themes appears once Rockbox is on the card. An installed app that leaves the list keeps its row, so you can still remove it.

  • Check now: press Check for Updates, the first row. A message says how many apps can be updated, that all are up to date, or why it couldn’t check. When the page checks on its own as it opens, the message shows only if an app can be updated.
  • Install or update: press Center on the row. It shows the download’s progress; press it again to cancel. You can leave the page meanwhile: a message says when it’s done, such as “Spotify is ready in Streaming”. Updates keep your Rockbox settings and Spotify’s login and cache size.
  • Rockbox becomes what the Q2 starts at power-on, as when you unzip it, so the first press says so and a second one installs it. Settings → Battery → Boot to changes that.
  • Remove: press an Installed row twice. Removing Rockbox removes the themes too. Removing a card app deletes its folder, with its login and settings; the music it saved stays. Removing Spotify keeps its login and cache; Streaming → Configuration → Spotify → Erase Spotify Data deletes them.
  • Subsonic comes built in: it is in Streaming with nothing to install, and keeps its login and downloads on the card in .subsonic. It is not in the App Store: new versions of Subsonic come with Q2 Muse updates, and the built-in one runs even if an older Subsonic is still on the card.
  • Card apps each get a row in Streaming once installed, except games, which go in Library → Games (Games); updates keep the settings they save. One with a phone page shows a QR code to scan with a phone on the same Wi-Fi. Plugging in USB storage stops them; they start again after, and Stop Streaming Services stops them too. A game runs only while you play it; close it before plugging in USB storage or turning the Q2 off. An app that streams can make Now Playing’s ⋯ open its own page, such as its playlists, while its music plays. An app that syncs music to the card can have the Q2 update the library and import its playlists afterwards. The Q2 waits until nothing is playing and you’re on Home, Streaming, the app’s page, Now Playing or a settings page, then shows Update Local Music’s progress. Update Local Music stops the player, so while a song is paused it also waits for the screen to turn off.
  • Install only apps you trust: a card app can read anything on the card, including your other apps’ logins and your Last.fm, ListenBrainz and Spotify logins.
  • The card needs room for the download and what it unpacks to, plus 64 MB; otherwise it says how much more space it needs. Keep USB storage unplugged until it finishes.

Custom boot logo

Put boot-logo.jpg in /Q2Muse/boot/ on the SD card. Use an 8-bit, three-component baseline JPEG (RGB/YCbCr), at most 512 KB (524,288 bytes): upright at 375×320 pixels, or 320×375 pixels with the artwork turned 90° clockwise. PNG, progressive JPEG, grayscale and CMYK are not supported.

Start Q2 Muse with the card inserted, or leave USB storage mode, to synchronize the logo. It appears at the following power-on. Booting directly into Rockbox does not import it. To restore the built-in logo, delete the card file and run Q2 Muse with the card mounted once; the following power-on uses the built-in logo.

Restoring stock firmware

  • Back to Shanling’s firmware: install the official Shanling firmware (en.shanling.com/download/150), such as stock V1.36, the same way as Q2 Muse.
  • If the Q2 won’t start up: first try a forced restart (Troubleshooting by symptom). If it still won’t start, copy the recovery-update folder from Shanling’s recovery package to the card, then hold Previous and power on with Center.

Using Rockbox alongside Q2 Muse

Install Rockbox:

  1. Download rockbox.zip from github.com/DiamondBond/q2-rockbox/releases.
  2. Unzip it onto the card’s root. It adds a .rockbox folder.
  3. Turn the Q2 on. Rockbox starts.

Or, over Wi-Fi: Settings → General → App Store installs Rockbox and then the themes (Installing apps over Wi-Fi).

Install the ported themes:

  1. Download the zip from github.com/DiamondBond/q2-rockbox-themes/releases.
  2. Unzip it onto the card. It fills in the .rockbox folder.
  3. In Rockbox, choose Settings → Theme Settings → Browse Theme Files, then pick one and load it.

Switching between Rockbox and Q2 Muse

  • Q2 Muse for one session: hold Play/Pause while turning the Q2 on. The next start is Rockbox again. To always start Q2 Muse, set Settings → Battery → Boot to: Q2 Muse.
  • Copy music over USB: turn on with Play/Pause held, plug in, and drag your files over.
  • No card in it? It starts Q2 Muse.
  • Bluetooth headphones with Rockbox: shut Rockbox down (it goes to Q2 Muse), turn on Bluetooth and connect your headphones, then pick Rockbox on the Home menu (above Streaming). Rockbox starts and plays through the headphones, with their volume raised to your maximum volume limit so Rockbox’s volume reaches full; the Q2’s own volume comes back on return. When it shuts down, you’re back in Q2 Muse.

If something goes wrong

  • Screen unreadable or frozen, say after a theme leaves white text on white: hold Play/Pause for 15 seconds to force a restart.
  • Rockbox is stuck: hold Play/Pause while turning the Q2 on. It goes to Q2 Muse.
  • Rockbox doesn’t start: the card must be exFAT or FAT32, and the .rockbox folder must be in the card’s root.

Tips and Help

10

Things people often don’t know are already there, and where to go when you need a hand.

Things you might not know

  • Hold Play/Pause on Now Playing for the queue, favorites, playlists, shuffle and repeat.
  • Rate songs with stars: on Now Playing swipe past the lyrics, turn to 0–5 stars, press Center to save. The heart likes a song, apart from its stars; Library → Ratings lists both.
  • Double-press the Center button to turn the screen off on any page (videos and Now Playing excepted); on Now Playing a single press does it, and a double press seeks.
  • Hold Return to jump to Now Playing from anywhere; hold it again there to go back where you were. Rather go Home? Settings → Controls → Hold Return: Home.
  • Where is search? In Library, pull the list down from the top until “Release to search” appears, or choose Search in Library. It searches your local songs.
  • Wheel too fast or slow? Settings → Controls → Wheel Sensitivity.
  • Battery longevity: Settings → Battery → Charge Limit.
  • Wi-Fi left on? While it’s connected, the radio naps between transfers and wakes as soon as data arrives, so there’s no need to turn it off between uses.
  • Guest artists splitting albums? Settings → Playback → Library → Artists → Album Artist (the default unless you picked Artist).
  • Cover Flow order: Settings → Display → Cover Flow → Sort picks Artist, Year, Recently Added, Most Played or Random. The default, Album, goes by album title, so one artist’s albums are spread across the alphabet; Artist keeps them together.
  • Screen waking in your pocket? Settings → Controls → Wake: Double Press.
  • Want stock’s EQ presets? EQ → Advanced → Presets → Stock presets has Pop, Rock, Jazz and the rest; the ten default bands work as a 10-band graphic EQ.
  • Wheel click gone? With Settings → Controls → Clicker: Speaker the click is the Q2’s own speaker, which stays quiet while headphones, Bluetooth or a USB DAC are connected; choose Both to keep it clicking.
  • Importing an M3U playlist? It has to be in _explaylist_data/, and a # line without #EXT shows as a broken song: see Chapter 2.
  • Added a few albums? System settings → Update Library → Scan Folders scans just their folders.

Troubleshooting by symptom

Each entry starts with checks you can undo, such as reconnecting or rescanning. Recovery steps come last and link to where they’re explained.

  • The Q2 won’t turn on, won’t charge, or is frozen. Plug it into the charger. Hold Center for 15 seconds, and keep holding through the on-screen shutdown prompt until it powers off and restarts. For Rockbox, hold Play/Pause for 15 seconds instead. A Q2 that stops responding while it’s on restarts by itself within a minute; afterwards q2-crash.log on the card says what it was doing, so attach it to a bug report.
  • A song is missing. Check it’s in a folder the Q2 scans, with a supported format (What goes on the SD card), then run Library → Update Local Music. DRM-protected files don’t appear. Still missing? Check its tags with Tagging your music.
  • An album is split in two. Give every track the same Album Artist (Tagging your music). Then run Update Local Music again.
  • Artwork is missing. The gray disc means no cover was found, and Cover Flow won’t look again until you refresh it. Embed the cover in the songs, or add a cover or folder image (.jpg, .png or .webp) next to them. A folder image is skipped when the folder holds more than one album, and songs with a different Album or Album Artist tag count as another album: match the tags (Tagging your music) or embed the cover. Then press Settings → Display → Cover Flow → Refresh Cover Flow (Browsing with Cover Flow).
  • Cover Flow restarts the Q2 while preparing artwork. Open Cover Flow again: the album it was reading is skipped and shows the gray disc, and its file is named in q2-crash.log on the card, under cover flow skipped. If several albums do this, the Q2 restarts once for each. Shrink that album’s very large embedded covers: about 500 × 500 pixels is plenty for the screen, and many taggers resize covers in bulk. Then press Settings → Display → Cover Flow → Refresh Cover Flow. If it keeps happening, install Shanling’s firmware and then Q2 Muse again (Restoring stock firmware), and attach the card’s q2-crash.log to a bug report.
  • Cover Flow prepares artwork every time it opens. Albums it finishes are never prepared again, even those with no cover, so the count is albums it couldn’t finish. Usually the library still lists songs that were moved, renamed or deleted on the card: run Library → Update Local Music, and the count goes away. If it stays, those albums’ covers are probably too big to read: shrink them as above, then press Refresh Cover Flow. The Q2 doesn’t say which albums they are.
  • No sound, or sound from the wrong output. Unplug and reconnect the headphones, and check Volume and the status bar. Playback uses whichever output is active when it starts: headphone jack, Bluetooth or a USB DAC. For Spotify, pause and play again after switching. If local music stays silent after streaming, use Streaming → Configuration → Stop Streaming Services (Configuration).
  • The PC doesn’t see the Q2 as a USB DAC. Windows needs Shanling’s USB driver (V5.20, for the MTouch players), from en.shanling.com/download/69. Install it, then plug the Q2 in again.
  • Bluetooth Stopped. The Bluetooth chip stopped answering. The Q2 restarts itself after 10 s to bring it back; press Center for Not Now to keep using it without Bluetooth (and Wi-Fi, on the same chip) until you restart it.
  • Bluetooth headphones drop out. Set the quality to Auto, which adapts to the link. LDAC Best is the most likely to break up (Bluetooth headphones).
  • Spotify won’t pair or download. The phone and the Q2 must be on the same Wi-Fi, and the Spotify app’s device list must show Q2. New tracks need Wi-Fi; downloaded songs play without it. Browsing needs q2-librespot v1.3 or later, and Download Playlist needs v1.6 (Installing and pairing Spotify). Playback didn’t start means no track arrived within 15 seconds: go back and try again.
  • The screen turns off when you choose a row. Two quick Center presses turn the screen off, on every page and over the keyboard. Choosing rows quickly, press once and wait a moment.
  • Pocket controls wake the screen or press buttons. Choose Wake: Double Press or set Buttons Lock (Controls).
  • Update or startup problems. Check that update.tar is in the card’s root (not inside a folder), and leave the card in until the Q2 restarts. Rockbox doesn’t start unless the card is exFAT or FAT32 with the .rockbox folder in its root. If Rockbox freezes or a theme leaves the screen unreadable, hold Play/Pause for 15 seconds to force a restart. If Rockbox is stuck, hold Play/Pause while turning the Q2 on (Using Rockbox alongside Q2 Muse). If the Q2 won’t start at all, follow Restoring stock firmware.

Getting help

  • Question or suggestion? Join the Q2 Pod Discord (Q2 Pod is the project Q2 Muse is forked from).
  • Found a bug? Open an issue with the checklist below.
  • Downloads and changelog: Q2 Pod releases and changelog.
  • Recovery: Shanling’s firmware and the recovery steps in Restoring stock firmware.
  • Spotify add-on: q2-librespot releases.

Bug-report checklist:

  1. Versions: the Q2 Muse release (from the zip’s name) and the q2-librespot version, if Spotify is involved.
  2. Steps: what you did, in order, starting from the last restart.
  3. Expected and actual: what should have happened, and what did.
  4. Output: headphone jack, Bluetooth (with the headphones’ name) or a USB DAC.
  5. Exact error: copy any “Can’t play” or other message word for word.
  6. Crash log: if the Q2 restarted on its own, q2-crash.log from the root of the card, and q2-crash.log.1 and q2-crash.log.2 if they are there: the two restarts before it.

Leave out passwords, tokens and API keys. Don’t post .scrobble.ini, the .spotify/cache/ folder or any login file.
