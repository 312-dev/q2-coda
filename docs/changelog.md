# Changelog

- **Unreleased (q2-coda)**:
  - Bluetooth: a song or a click right after the last click's sound no longer fails to start because the headset's audio was still being let go; the Q2 waits a moment for it.
  - Logs: when Wi-Fi does not join a saved network, the log says so 30 seconds after Wi-Fi starts and each minute after: whether it is still scanning, how many networks it saw, how often it rescans and why. Joining and getting an address are logged with the seconds they took.
  - Subsonic: a restart that resumes a song from your server starts the app once, where two copies of it could start and both run until the next restart, each checking the server and writing the same files.
  - Bluetooth: music paused for more than a couple of minutes plays again when you press Play, where AirPods could stay silent, still connected, until you disconnected and reconnected them. After 20 seconds paused the Q2 lets the headset's audio stream stop, as a phone does, and starts it again at Play or a click.
  - Radio: a radio of a song from your server (the Subsonic app's) plays with shuffle and repeat off, as the library's own radio does, and puts them back when it ends. With Repeat One on, every song of the radio played twice in a row, and the dimmed buttons could not turn it off while the radio played.
  - Loading: anything that takes a moment shows one spinner, centred, with a line under it saying what it is doing ("Shuffling songs…", "Sorting songs…"). It appears only after a third of a second, so quick actions never flash it, and a second press meanwhile does nothing. It covers turning Shuffle on in Now Playing, Shuffle Songs, Shuffle Albums and Shuffle Folders, and a Library list sorting after a change; a server's page, Spotify, Internet Radio and Chapters use the same spinner and delay, and Return still cancels those.
  - Logs: a slow moment on the screen is logged with how much of it went to drawing and input and how much to background checks, naming the slowest check, so a freeze can be traced even when nothing else names it.
  - Shuffle: turning Shuffle on or off responds at once on a library of any size, where the Q2 could freeze for a moment while it sorted every song and wrote the queue to the card. The queue is saved in the background, so a slow or busy card never holds up a button.
  - Bluetooth: each paired device has a **Volume Curve** on its page, **Even** or **Gentle**. Even, the default, suits most headphones. Gentle is for headphones that are already loud at half volume (such as the Bowers & Wilkins Px7): each step of the Q2's volume sounds like the same change, and half volume is much quieter. It changes the volume of the connected device at once, and its Volume Limit still applies.
  - Smart Crossfade: FLAC, ALAC, WAV and AIFF songs crossfade for the full length at any sample rate, where a 96 kHz song's overlap was cut to 7.5 seconds and a 192 kHz song's to under 4. The player reads the end of the song from the card a second time instead of keeping it in memory, so a crossfade no longer takes 6 MB of the Q2's memory and keeps it; for other formats that memory is now given back after each crossfade. What you hear is the same, sample for sample.
  - Smart Crossfade: every crossfade overlaps for at least 5 seconds and up to 15, judged from the last 30 seconds of one song and the first 30 seconds of the next. A song that fades out overlaps for as long as its fade, and a next song with a soft intro plays that intro under the end of the one before, where a song with a short fade-out got only a second or two of overlap.
  - Cover Flow: opens straight onto its covers, even the first time on a large library or after new covers arrive, where it showed "Preparing artwork" until every album's cover was made (about a minute on a large server library). Covers not made yet show the placeholder and fill in a few at a time, the ones on screen first, following the wheel; leaving keeps what was made, and the next visit finishes the rest.
  - Library: after your server's library or the songs on the card change, Songs, Albums and Artists are sorted again while you are not using the Q2 (screen off, or a few seconds after your last press), so the next time you open them they show at once, where they waited behind a spinner for a few seconds on a large library.
  - Games: **Cabin Solitaire**, a new game in Games, after Bricks: Klondike on a cabin's card table, over the whole screen, with a deck of its own (a fox, an owl and a bear for the court). The wheel stops only on cards that can move, and a card you pick up hovers over the best place for it, the other places it can go outlined in gold. Draw one or three, winnable deals or random ones, undo up to 64 moves, Next to send a card home, and Play/Pause finishes the game once every card is face up. Your game, wins, best time and fewest moves are kept. The music keeps playing.
  - Bluetooth: each paired device has its own **Volume Limit** on its page, No Limit until you set one. While that device is connected the volume stops at its limit, from the wheel, Control Center, Settings, the Car Remote or the device's own buttons, and Max Volume still applies on top. Wired and USB headphones and your other Bluetooth devices keep their own levels.
  - Lyrics: after you scroll synced lyrics away, they go back to the line being sung and follow the song again once you leave them alone for 5 seconds. Tapping a synced line plays the song from that line; tap the line being sung to show the lyrics full screen as before.
  - Streamed songs: a song from your server starts as soon as its first 3 seconds have downloaded, up to half a second sooner than before, where the Subsonic app waited for its next progress report.
  - Server library: every album gets its cover on a card formatted with large clusters (covers stopped at 2,048 albums on a 128 KB-cluster card, the rest showing the placeholder for good), and playlist pictures are fetched before the albums' covers, so they show soon after connecting.
  - Server library: when the Q2 joins a Wi-Fi network and the first check of your server fails, the app tries again after 15 seconds (then 30 s, 1 and 2 minutes) and at once on a new Wi-Fi address, where the library could stay disconnected for up to 10 minutes until Sync Now.
  - Subsonic: on the app's own pages (an album's page, Top Songs, All Songs), hold Center or a finger on a song for its menu: **Play Next** and **Play Last** add it to the queue playing (a song not on the card downloads first and joins once it is there), and **Sync Offline** chooses it for the next sync, the same rows the Library's songs have. Letting go does not play the song.
  - Search: holding Center, Play/Pause or a finger on a song in the results opens its options, as on any song list.
  - Up Next: a song's options (hold Center or a finger on it) end with **Remove from Queue**, which takes it out of the queue; the song playing plays on, and the shorter queue is what comes back after a restart.
  - The wheel does nothing while the screen is off, whatever Wake and Buttons Lock are set to: turning it no longer wakes the screen, clicks or changes anything, and it does nothing while the Buttons Locked card shows. A headset's own volume buttons and the Car Remote still change the volume with the screen off.
  - Song lists: hold Center on a song (an album's tracks, Songs, a playlist, an artist's songs, Folders, Cover Flow's tracks, Most Played, the Ratings lists or Up Next) for half a second, or hold a finger on it, and its menu opens: Play Next, Play Last, Add to Playlist, Favorite, Go to Album, Go to Artist and the rest, the same menu a Play/Pause hold opens. Letting go does not play the song, and the press does not go on to the Shut Down prompt; holding Center on anything that is not a song still shuts down as before.
  - Shuffle: the queue now shows the real shuffled order. It opens on the song playing, with the songs that will play next below it in the order they will play, and the songs already played above it (scroll up), so Next plays the row below and Previous the row above. Shuffle turned on from Now Playing gets its order at once, Play Next and Add to Queue no longer reshuffle the rest, gapless playback and Smart Crossfade know the next song in Shuffle too (every Shuffle change still crossfades, the same album's too), and the car and phone list the queue in the same order.
  - Logs: opening Cover Flow from Home leaves a line when it opens, one when its covers show and how long that took, and, if nothing shows within 5 seconds, a line a minute saying what it is still waiting for (the album list or the cover lookup), and a line when the covers' art finishes building behind them, or stops, and how far it got.
  - Library > Years: opening a large decade shows its first albums at once and adds the rest over the next second, where the screen froze for about two seconds on a decade of 2,600 albums; Play All on a decade starts in about a quarter of the time. The log names Years' and every Library list's loading, so a slow one shows which.
  - Clicker: with Bluetooth headphones the click is now heard while a song is paused or nothing plays, not only while a song plays.
  - Settings → General has a **Rockbox** row while Rockbox is on the card: it starts Rockbox, which then plays through the Bluetooth headphones Q2 Muse connected.
  - Display: **Home** offers Split and Full only. Carousel is gone: under it there was no way back to Settings, so the setting could not be changed back. A Q2 left on Carousel opens in Split.
  - Car Remote: on the car's now-playing screen, the artist and the album open that artist's and that album's pages (with a Q2 Muse Remote that supports it).
  - Playback: **Crossfade** is now **Smart Crossfade**, one switch. Songs from different albums overlap at the right moment for each pair: a song that fades out plays under the next one's start for as long as its fade lasts, silence at the end of a song or the start of the next is skipped, a song that starts loud comes in at full level, and the overlap never clips. When the Q2 can't look at both songs in time (the next one still downloading, for one) it fades over 5 seconds as before. Switching it on switches Gapless on too, and the row says "Needs Gapless on" if Gapless is later switched off. If Crossfade was on, Smart Crossfade is on; the old length setting is no longer used.
  - Home: dragging the menu with a finger no longer leaves the blue selection on a row under the clock (with a song loaded, "Paused" or "Playing" drawn through the time) or on the faded row at the bottom. When the drag ends, the selection moves to a whole row in the middle of the list.
  - Bluetooth pairing: the code card names the device asking ("Pair with Pixel 10?", or "this device" with its address until its name is known), shows the code large in two groups, and offers Cancel and Pair. It closes by itself as soon as the other device cancels, fails or disconnects, and refuses after 60 seconds, where the old card could stay up for hours and block a software update meanwhile.
  - Bluetooth: with **Link Volume** on, the Q2's volume now spreads evenly over the headphones' whole range, as a phone's volume does. Before, on headphones such as Pixel Buds, the first 90 of the Q2's 100 steps changed little and the last 10 did most of the work. Headphones that do no shaping of their own (the Bowers & Wilkins Px7 seemed not to) are loud by half volume again.
  - Standby now lets go of the SD card properly before the Q2 sleeps and checks it is unmounted, so the card is mounted once after a wake instead of twice, nothing is left writing to it as it powers down, and power-off and restart unmount it cleanly too.
  - Car Audio: when the car stops taking audio mid-song, a stop, a skip or a new song now takes effect at once, where the Q2 could wait up to 10 seconds first.
  - Car Remote: the Q2's log names each command it takes from the phone or the car (play, Next, Previous, seek, a queue pick, volume and the rest) with what the player was doing, so a song skipped from the phone can be told from one that ended.
  - Home: the slideshow's album covers from your server are sharp, made from the app's large covers instead of the small list-row ones scaled up.
  - Car Audio: after the car paused and its Bluetooth dropped, the song could play again from where it was paused once it ended, and every song after it then played one late, under the next song's title and length. The Q2 now stops the song and starts it again once, as it meant to.
  - USB-C headphones or a USB DAC: pressing Play while they are still connecting says "Connecting USB audio..." and starts the song on them once they are ready, instead of refusing with "Connect headphones or Bluetooth to play"; and unplugging them no longer starts a Bluetooth connection to a headset as if a cable had been plugged in.
  - USB-C headphones or a USB DAC plugged in soon after unplugging a computer now get power after about 8 seconds, where they could stay unpowered (and Play said there was nowhere to play) until they were unplugged and plugged in again.
  - USB-C headphones and USB DACs with their own volume in decibels: the Q2's volume now runs evenly in decibels from very quiet (-60 dB, or the device's quietest) at 1 to full at 100, where volume 1 on USB-C earbuds was already loud.
  - USB-C earbuds that the Q2 sometimes took for a charging cable (showing it charging and getting no power) are no longer taken for one; the Q2 asks for a replug instead, which got them recognised when it was tried.
  - A computer that does not see the Q2 after plugging in (both ends trying to be the power source) now gets "Unplug the cable and plug it in again" on the Q2, where nothing said why.
  - USB-C headphones whose power the Q2 cut a second after plugging them in, while they were still connecting, now keep it until they are unplugged. Starting a station, a video or another song while they connect says they are connecting and no longer starts the paused song once they are ready.
  - Car Audio: unplugging, or picking another USB mode, while a song is paused into the car could freeze the Q2's screen, and a cable plugged back in got no iPhone until Play was pressed; the Q2 now lets go of the car's audio first.
  - Car Audio: pausing in the car no longer lets up to a third of a second of earlier and later music play out of order after the press, and a cable pulled mid-song no longer leaves the car's audio half running on the Q2 until the next car.
  - Logs: Car Audio keeps the logs of every car cable from the last 7 days (up to 10 MB), and plugging the Q2 into a computer or a charger no longer pushes a drive's logs out. The unified log now says each button a Bluetooth car or headset sends, what the Car Remote's Play and Pause found the player doing, and why a cable showed the car no iPhone.
  - Streaming: with Download Ahead Off, the Q2 no longer downloads the two songs after the first one of every list you start from your server.
  - Shuffle: on a list streaming from your server, Next plays the song Up Next shows first, where it sometimes jumped to the list's next download instead, which then came round again in the same shuffle.
  - Playback: tapping a song with no headphones, Bluetooth or USB audio connected now only shows "Connect headphones or Bluetooth to play" and leaves you on the list. It briefly opened a Now Playing screen with Shanling's placeholder title. The same goes for Up Next's rows, which now stay open, and for a paused song's row in View Queue or an album's Play.
  - Ratings: stars you give songs from your server now reach the card's `.ratings.tsv` copy, and the Q2 keeps stars for up to 12,285 songs (it stopped at 4,096 with "Ratings are full"). Ratings kept before are kept, and the Q2's old ratings file is saved once beside the new one (`/mnt/data/ringnav-ratings.v1`).
  - Battery: with a server signed in, the Q2 now looks at the streaming app's files every 10 seconds instead of every second while the screen is off and nothing plays, and Now Playing's download spinner stops checking while the screen is off. Your server's stars and hearts arriving all at once are applied in one go, about a hundred times faster on a first sync.
  - Home: the album art slideshow beside the main menu now shows your server's albums too. With a library that is all on a server, it showed nothing.
  - Games: Games lists Bricks alone (then any games on your card). Sudoku, Vortex, Solitaire and Snake are left out of this build while the new games are finished; a game in progress or a best score you had stays saved for when they return.
  - USB DACs: USB-C earphones and simple USB DACs that take only 48 kHz now play every song, converted to what they take, where each song said "Not Supported". A DAC that takes a song's own rate and bit depth still gets it untouched. Unplugging one mid-song no longer runs through the queue marking each song unsupported: the song waits, and goes on when the DAC is back.
  - Logs: Car Audio keeps the logs of every car cable from the last 7 days (up to 40 MB, and always the last two drives), and plugging the Q2 into a computer or a charger no longer pushes a drive's logs out. The unified log now says each button a Bluetooth car or headset sends, what the Car Remote's Play and Pause found the player doing, and why a cable showed the car no iPhone.
  - Streaming: a song from your server starts sooner, once about 3 seconds of it have downloaded instead of 8, and a song with a cover inside the file no longer waits for 5 seconds of it first. On a download of 300 KB/s a song starts in about 2.4 seconds, where it took 4.4.
  - Car Remote: the car's steering-wheel buttons and the phone's commands are no longer lost when two come close together (two taps of Next skip two songs), and they act straight away instead of up to half a second later.
  - Battery: with Car Remote and **Smart Wi-Fi** both on, the Q2 no longer keeps Wi-Fi on and searching when no saved network is in range, or for more than 10 minutes after Car Remote was last used; the across-networks link runs only while Wi-Fi is connected; and the Q2 does less in the background while no phone is connected. Bluetooth pairing and control are unchanged.
  - Car Remote: from another network, only phones paired with this Q2 can reach it, and a phone you forget can no longer reach it at all. Q2 Muse Remote registers itself the next time it connects by Bluetooth or Wi-Fi; an older Q2 Muse Remote keeps Bluetooth and Wi-Fi but not the across-networks link.
  - Car Audio: changing songs or the queue with a server library no longer holds the car link up for a fraction of a second each time, and Q2 Muse Remote's library pages open faster and use less of the Q2's processor.
  - Car Audio: the car log on the SD card grows about a fifth as fast and no longer grows without limit on a long drive, and writing it can no longer hold up the car's connection.
  - Car Audio: after a lost connection the car gets the Q2 back as it should even while a cover is still being made, where it could stay without an iPhone until the cable was unplugged.
  - Car Audio: on a cable, a car's or a wall charger's, the Q2's car service now wakes only when there is something to do, about a tenth to a twentieth as often as before (it woke a hundred times a second all night on a charger).

The highlight moves up with the network you press, and a failed one stays marked until you try again or search. The "Connecting..." line no longer floats between two rows.

- **V4.9R / V4.9C**: Coverflow covers always snap into place when you let go of a swipe, instead of sometimes stopping between two albums.
- **V4.8R / V4.8C**: Coverflow tracks accept the Play/Pause hold queue menu (Play next / Add to queue), Coverflow remembers the last album and each album's highlighted track, and long album, artist and track names scroll. The payload is now built with `-Oz` so compact keeps room in the rootfs.
- **V4.7R / V4.7C**: Coverflow caches artwork in `.coverflow` on the microSD card and checks free space there, so low internal storage no longer prevents artwork preparation.
- **V4.6R / V4.6C**: New **Coverflow** card on Home, after Local Music: flip through your Local Music albums by cover with the wheel, open one to see its tracks, and play from there. The first open prepares the artwork once (from `cover.jpg`, `folder.jpg` or the embedded picture) with progress and Cancel; later opens only add new albums, and the last card, **Refresh library**, rebuilds it. The queue menu no longer shows a message after a successful add; it still shows **Queue unchanged** when it can't add.
- **V4.5R / V4.5C**: Hold **Play/Pause** on a highlighted song, album or folder in the local lists to open **Play next** / **Add to queue**. Tracks are added without pausing, restarting or seeking what is playing; a paused player stays paused, and with shuffle on, Play next is still the next track. Return closes the menu and the release after the hold never toggles playback.
- **V4.4R / V4.4C**: After a restart the PEQ editor now shows **PEQ: OFF**, matching the status-bar EQ icon and what you hear; the PEQ stays off after a reboot until you switch it back on, and your applied bands are kept.
- **V4.3R / V4.3C**: Saved PEQ presets can be deleted from **Presets → Delete a preset**, after a confirmation; the active EQ is unchanged.
- **V4.2R / V4.2C**: PEQ editor messages (Applied, Loaded, errors) now appear in the title bar instead of cutting the list down to two rows while they show. The title names the preset you loaded ("PEQ: HD650") and keeps the applied preset's name when you reopen the editor, until the next reboot.
- **V4.1R / V4.1C**: Artist pages now show their albums straight away; V3.8 highlighted the Album tab but opened an empty page until you switched tabs. PEQ editor lists no longer show a white strip below the last row.
- **V4.0R / V4.0C**: PEQ audio and import fixes. Band and preamp changes no longer click: filters keep their state across updates and the preamp is applied after them as a smooth ramp. Near-silent audio no longer drives the filters into slow denormal math. On low sample-rate files, a band above the file's frequency range is skipped instead of switching the whole EQ off. Imports match Equalizer APO more closely: LS/HS with a Q use APO's corner frequency, shelves without a Q use APO's default slope, comma decimals such as `-3,5` are accepted, empty `None` filter slots are skipped, and a shelf whose APO corner shift leaves 20-20000 Hz is clamped to the range instead of failing the import.
- **V3.9R / V3.9C**: The PEQ editor rows use white text instead of gray. The preamp is shown on one read-only row; set it in the preset (`Preamp:` in AutoEQ / Equalizer APO files) instead of the removed lower/raise controls. The editor drops its Back rows (Return steps back), shows only whole rows, shows the status line only when there is a message, and reads "Nothing to apply" until there are edits or a loaded preset to apply.
- **V3.8R / V3.8C**: Artist pages open on Albums; All Songs stays one tap away. The artist page tabs are now translated: stock firmware showed Chinese 单曲/专辑 there in every language.
- **V3.7R / V3.7C**: The stock equalizer is replaced by a ten-band parametric EQ with peaking and shelf bands, preamp, an immediate on/off switch that also drives the status-bar EQ icon, saved presets and AutoEQ / Equalizer APO `.txt` import from `/EQ` on the microSD card. Return steps back through the editor's screens. At the ends of the local folder and music lists the wheel now hard-stops while you keep turning; pause briefly, then turn again to wrap around.
- **V3.6R / V3.6C**: Compact Local Songs opens search with a pull-down. Start inside the list while it is at the top; the overlay reads "Pull to search" after an 8-pixel downward drag and "Release to search" at 48 pixels. Releasing opens the stock search dialog, and moving back below the threshold cancels. The gesture clears on wheel or button input, navigation, screen-off or interruption and never activates a row. Empty lists work; Folder view and other lists are unchanged, and the normal build keeps its stock search toolbar.
- **V3.5R / V3.5C**: In compact mode, ordinary list-row titles use the full row width on every native relayout, reserving space only for visible artwork and trailing controls. Widths follow scrolling, row reuse and artwork/control visibility changes; short titles stay at their left position and overflowing titles still scroll or ellipsize. Other layouts and the normal build are unchanged.
- **V3.4R / V3.4C**: The local folder and music lists carry over at their ends: the first turn past an end nudges the selection outline against the end, and the next turn in the same direction wraps to the other end of the same list. Wheel navigation now wakes the player's own scrollbar, which fades on its normal timer, instead of drawing a separate position bar. The local and online search result lists navigate with the wheel and the center button; the on-screen keyboard dialogs keep the wheel on volume control. Compact Return now opens Now Playing on hold and stays there after release; the next short Return goes back to where you were, and holding Return while already on Now Playing uses the stock Home shortcut. This replaces the V3.3 single-press latch behavior.
- **V3.3R / V3.3C**: Compact returns from Now Playing with a single Return press again; the stock hold-release latch is cleared on that page instead of swallowing the release and forcing a second press.
- **V3.2R / V3.2C**: Compact rows now fill the client area with 72-pixel rows, so the stock artwork keeps its natural size with an even eight-pixel inset instead of being scaled down. Navigation and all other behavior are unchanged.
- **V3.1R / V3.1C**: Shared normal and compact builds, compact local lists and long Return to Now Playing, and a reproducible dual-variant release procedure.
- **V3.0R**: Long lists accelerate smoothly again: each 100 ms of sustained same-direction spin adds a row to the step, up to eight rows per tick, replacing the V2.7R/V2.8R three-speed ladder. Stopping, reversing or easing off still drops back to one row immediately. The selected-row outline is now a softer translucent white line, so it sits better against the dark theme while the dark separator still keeps it readable over bright album art. The shipped `config.ini` is no longer modified, so a fresh install keeps the stock key tone default; a device that already ran V2.9R keeps its saved setting and can change it in the system settings.
- **V2.9R**: Restores the stock wheel and button input path, removing the V2.8R 25 ms detent hold. The key tone now ships disabled, so wheel and button feedback is silent by default; enable **Key Tone** in the system settings to bring the clicks back. The three-speed wheel acceleration from V2.8R is unchanged.
- **V2.8R**: Wheel acceleration adds a third speed: longer lists step two rows after 300 ms and three rows after 600 ms of continuous same-direction turns. A wheel button press no longer plays a second tick from the capacitive touch, drops the phantom step with it, and the wheel is ready again as soon as the button is released.
- **V2.7R**: Wheel turns move the list immediately instead of animating, and lists longer than 16 rows step two rows after 450 ms of continuous same-direction turns. Touching the screen hides the selection outline until the next accepted wheel or center action, including across page changes. Center confirmation and the double-press screen toggle now use a 200 ms window.
- **V2.6R**: Wheel input replaces an unfinished scroll-view animation so reversing a glide toward a list boundary takes effect immediately. Native clicks also reset wheel acceleration and the home wheel interval when no touch-down event was delivered.
- **V2.5R**: Native clicks cancel pending center confirmation even without a touch-down event. Rejected confirmations leave changed lists and their remembered positions untouched. Double-press still turns the screen off after interrupting a recalled list with touch. Custom boot logos must use the stock renderer's supported JPEG frame format; packaging uses a snapshot of the validated image.
- **V2.4R**: Taps and center presses stay bound to the row they started on, so a rebuilt or still-settling list cannot move the row under your finger or open a different one. Page-snapping views no longer capture the wheel, and a failed rounded outline stroke falls back to the square outline.
- **V2.3R**: Wheel selection and restored positions keep a small margin from the screen edge, and reversing the home wheel responds immediately to correct an overshoot.
- **V2.2R**: Remembers the 64 most recently selected browsing positions, restoring folders and music queries on return. Lists of 16 or fewer items stay at one row per wheel detent, even during quick turns.
- **V2.1R**: Fast-spin acceleration stays within the current menu and resets after a folder/query change, list resize, interrupted gesture or sleep. Oversized rows reveal their title consistently when selected or restored.
- **V2.0R**: Center presses now confirm after 300 ms, allowing a second press within that window to turn the screen off. Home carousel wheel input is paced to make cards easier to select. Includes an orientation fixed boot logo.
- **V1.9R**: The selection outline is now one crisp white line over a dark separator and the subtle dark fill, so it stays readable over bright album art. A canvas that declines rounded drawing keeps the square outline.
- **V1.8R**: Wheel acceleration, gliding music tables, a scoped double-press screen toggle, nested tap-target selection, per-context position memory with row-text identity, multi-pane surface selection, a center-nearest swipe settle and a build-time context audit.

- **V1.5R**: Added menu item highlighting, center-button selection, and music selection features.

Lists glide with AWTK's own scrolling, and the ring does nothing at a list's end or while a page slides, so it never changes the volume by accident.
