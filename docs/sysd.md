# q2sysd

Vamp OS's system service ([roadmap: Who owns what](vamp-os.md#who-owns-what)): the power key, the screen's light and its off time, Auto Standby and Auto Power Off, the battery level, Charge Limit, USB modes and the cable, and Standby, with no `demo` ([vamp-os-base](vamp-os-base.md)). Heat, the jack, the DAC's power, the MCU and the settings store are later slices.

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Source | `sysd/q2sysd.c` (one file, one `poll` loop), `sysd/Makefile` |
| In the image | `/usr/bin/q2sysd`, the `q2sysd` service (`os/package/q2sysd`, `/etc/sv/q2sysd`) |
| Tests | `test/sysd.py` (this machine, a stand-in `/sys` and `/dev/input`), `test/os_image.py`'s boot (the image's own under QEMU) |

## What it does

| Duty | How | Known from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The power key** | Every `/dev/input/event*` whose keys include `KEY_POWER` (116) is watched: `md-gpio-keys`' power button (`PE31`), the AXP2101's key (`axp2101-pek`), and `hyn_ts`, which reports the touchscreen's double tap as `KEY_POWER` while its gesture mode is on. A press shorter than 3 s turns the screen off or on; held 3 s it powers off (`/sbin/poweroff`: init runs `rcK`, which stops the services and unmounts the card and `/mnt/data`, then powers off). The release after a power off, and a release with no press, do nothing | read: [hardware](hardware.md), `module_driver/keyboard_gpio_add.sh`, [internals: Screen off and wake](internals.md#screen-off-and-wake) |
| **The screen's light** | Off after [Screen Off Time](#the-screen-off-time) unused, or at a press. Off: the backlight's `bl_power` 4 (`FB_BLANK_POWERDOWN`) and `/sys/hynitron_debug/hyngescontrol` 1 (gestures on, as stock's `double_onclick_wake` writes as the screen goes off); on: `bl_power` 0 and gestures off. The backlight is the first device in `/sys/class/backlight`, the one `q2guard` and `q2ui` read: `q2ui` takes `bl_power` not 0 as a dark screen and drops input then (`ui/ui.c` `dark`), so a press that lights the screen is nothing else | read: `patch/q2guard.c`, `ui/ui.c`, [internals](internals.md#screen-off-and-wake) |
| **Brightness** | `brightness P` (0 to 100) sets the backlight's `brightness` to P of its `max_brightness`, linearly | inferred: stock's own scale (`toolSetBackLight`, `SYSSET LIGHTNESS`) is not read |
| **The battery** | Every 30 s and at each change of the screen: the CW2015's `cw2015_capacity` and `cw2015_voltage` (`/sys/devices/i2c-0/0-0062/`), `/sys/class/power_supply/battery/status` and `ac/online`; on a change written whole to `/run/vamp/battery` (`level`, `mv`, `status`, `cable`, a `key=value` line each) and told to watchers | read: [hardware: Battery reporting](hardware.md), `patch/q2guard.c` (the same files, logged on a Q2) |

| **Charge Limit** | `Q2POD CHARGELIMIT=1` in `config.ini`, the payload's setting: at each battery read, charging is held off at 80% and handed back at 75% (`CHARGE_STOP`, `CHARGE_RESUME`) through the charger's active-low enable line `PE22`, driven as the kernel's GPIO 150 (`/sys/class/gpio`, exported once, `1` holds it off). Off, the line is never touched | read: [internals: Charge Limit](internals.md#charge-limit) |
| **USB modes** | `usb storage`: the player stopped (Q2PLAY/1 `stop`), the card synced and unmounted (refused while it is busy, nothing else changed), adb's gadget parked (`q2adb-gadget park`), and a mass-storage gadget made as stock's `storage_config.sh` makes one (`0525:a4a5`, one LUN, the card's block device, writable, removable) and bound to the controller. `usb adb`: the gadget unbound and removed, the card mounted again, adb's gadget back (`unpark`). At a cable, the one the USB setting names ([The cable](#the-cable)) | read: stock's `storage_config.sh`, [adb: The USB gadget](adb.md#the-usb-gadget) `usb dac`: the Q2 a USB DAC for the computer, the player stopped, adb parked, a UAC2 gadget with stock's descriptors and the `q2dacloop` service up ([dacloop](dacloop.md)); `usb adb` takes the loop down before its gadget. |
| **Standby** | `standby`: the screen off, Q2PLAY/1 `pause`, Q2NET/1 `off` (not on a card boot, whose Wi-Fi is the developer's way in), `sync`, then `mem` written to `/sys/power/state`, which returns when a wake source (the power key) has resumed the Q2; then the screen on and Q2NET/1 `wake`. A refused suspend (the kernel's error) still brings the screen back | inferred: the Linux interface; stock's own order is [internals: Screen off and wake](internals.md#screen-off-and-wake)'s |

## The screen off time

Stock's **Screen Off Time** (Settings > Display), `[SYSSET] LIGHTTIME` in `/mnt/data/config.ini`: while the screen is lit and nothing uses the Q2 for that long, `q2sysd` turns it off as a power key press does (`bl_power` 4, gestures on, `screen off` told to watchers). The values are stock's: `demo`'s `setlight_time` (`0x4feb04`) maps 0 to 5 through a table of 15, 30, 60, 120, 300 and 1800 seconds and anything else to 0, which never turns the screen off; stock's file says 1 (30 s), and a file without the key reads as 1.

A use is any event on any input device (`EV_KEY`, `EV_ABS`, `EV_REL`: a side key, the Center button, the touchscreen, the wheel's `hyn_sliderbar`), so `q2sysd` keeps every `/dev/input/event*` open (eight at most), not only those with the power key; the screen lit by any means; and Q2SYS/1's `awake`, for a program that shows something with no key pressed (a video, a game, today's Vamp's Smart Awake on Now Playing, which is `q2ui`'s to send). `config.ini` is looked at once a second (its size and time), and a change starts the time again, as stock's `config_lighttime` resets its timer. The check runs on the same 1 s tick as the cable's, so the screen goes off up to a second late.

## Auto Standby and Auto Power Off

Stock's two power settings in `/mnt/data/config.ini`, read with Screen Off Time: **Auto Standby**, `[SYSSET] STANDBYMODE` (1 on: `demo`'s `setstandby_time`, `0x4feaf4`, gives 300 s; anything else off), and **Auto Power Off**, `[SYSSET] SAVEPOWERMODE` (1 to 4: `setsavepower_time`'s table, 60, 180, 300 and 600 s; anything else off). Both are off unless set: Vamp's own defaults leave Standby off (`tools/build.py` `DEFAULT_CONFIG_OFF`), and stock's file says 0 for both.

What `q2sysd` does is what stock's `demo` does, read from its V1.36 binary (notes outside the repository):

- **The counts.** `check_to_poweroption` (`0x4f3c90`, once a second) takes a second off each count only while nothing holds it, so a hold stops a count where it is and its end leaves the rest. Both counts are made whole again by `reset_poweroptions_timer(1, 1, 1)` (`0x4feb38`), which stock calls at a key or touch (`on_wm_keydown_before_fun`, `on_wm_keyup_before_fun`, `on_wm_tsdown_before_fun`), the screen coming on (`screen_action`, `0x501f64`; the screen going off resets nothing), a change of the setting (`config_lighttime`), the end of a Standby (`device_standby`) and a change of the cable, in or out (`get_battery_capacity`, `0x4ff318`). Here the same: a use ([The screen off time](#the-screen-off-time)'s, Q2SYS/1 `awake` among them), a setting change, a resume and a change of `ac/online`. The screen off time turning the screen off is not a use.
- **The holds, both counts.** Music playing (`mclGetPlayStatus` 2; here `state=1` in `q2playd`'s `/tmp/coda-rc.now`, which AirPlay's and DLNA's songs reach too), car mode (`g_carmode`; here Car Audio's `usb car`), USB storage (`g_usblink_status` 2; here `usb storage`). Stock also holds while its Bluetooth receiver page is up, a Wi-Fi transfer runs or a Baidu download runs; Vamp OS has none of those today.
- **Standby only: any cable.** The gate's last test is `g_charge_state` 0 (`0x4f3dd8`). Its only writer is `get_battery_capacity` (`0x4ff2e4`, `0x4ff318`), which stores `usb_detect`'s answer, GPIO `PD17` low, the cable, and 0 when the Q2 itself powers an OTG device. It is not the charge: that is `g_power_chargestate` (`toolsGetChargeStatus`, `0x5cf5ec`: `battery/status` Discharging 0, Charging 1, Full 2). So stock never suspends with a cable in, a charger's included, and a computer's or car's link is never under a Standby (today's Vamp's host hold, [internals: Off by default, held by a host](internals.md#standby-and-the-usb-link), falls inside it). Here a cable is `ac/online` 1. [internals](internals.md#standby-and-the-usb-link) says `g_charge_state` reads 0 at Full with the cable in; the binary gives it no writer that would do that, so the two disagree until a Q2 settles it ([Needs a Q2](#needs-a-q2)).
- **Auto Power Off: no cable hold, then stock's power-off page.** Its gate (`0x4f3e40`) is Standby's without `g_charge_state`. At zero it opens `poweroff_page`, which has no countdown and nothing to choose: `poweroff_page_init` (`0x53e8c8`) runs `into_poweroff` (`0x501a48`: the queue saved, the player stopped, the DAC, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, DLNA and AirPlay off), and with no cable `device_poweroff` at once. With a cable it stays on showing the charge (the level, an animation) and checks once a second (`0x53e790`), powering off once the cable is out or `battery/status` reads Full; a short press there lights or darkens the screen and a long one restarts (`on_poweroff_keylong_fun`, `device_reboot`). Here: no cable, a power off as a held power key does (`power off` told, init's shutdown); a cable, Q2PLAY/1 `pause` and Q2NET/1 `off` (not on a card boot), `charge wait` told (the charge screen is `q2ui`'s to draw), then `battery/status` read each second until the cable is out or it says Full, and the power off. A press in the charge wait lights or darkens the screen as ever, and the 3 s hold restarts (`reboot` told, `/sbin/reboot`) instead of powering off. Auto Power Off goes first when both are due.

## The cable

`ac/online` (`/sys/class/power_supply/ac/online`) is read once a second (`CABLE_MS`): no event for VBUS reaches userspace ([usb: What the hardware can tell us](usb.md#what-the-hardware-can-tell-us)), and `online` is the signal that told a real source from residue on every line of the 2026-11-25 record. A change is a battery read at once (watchers hear `cable=`), and then:

| The USB setting (`[Q2POD] USBPICK`, the payload's picker's) | A cable in |
| --- | --- |
| 0 Automatic, the default, and anything not below | `usb car`: the iPhone. A car keeps it (Car Audio); a computer, once q2car has its gadget down, gets the card (`usb storage`); a charger (nothing answered) and a failed probe are adb, the card left alone |
| 1 Charge Only, 4 USB Debugging | nothing: adb is the mode with no cable, and charging needs none |
| 2 Transfer Files | `usb storage` |
| 3 USB Audio | `usb dac` |
| 5 Ask Every Time | a `usb ask` event for whoever draws the chooser, which answers with a `usb` command; until then, Charge Only |

A cable out is `usb adb` from any mode: the card mounted again, the loop down, or `q2car stop`. The setting is read at each cable in, so a pick made while one is in takes the next. A cable in when q2sysd starts is a cable in, so a Q2 started in a car plays to it; a q2sysd restarted by `runsv` with a cable in starts that mode again. Today's payload reaches the same table through its own poll ([usb](usb.md), `patch/usb_ui.c`); Vamp OS's chooser is `q2ui`'s, not drawn yet.

## Q2SYS/1

On `/run/vamp/sys.sock`: its protocol is Vamp API 1's system page, [Q2SYS/1](platform/system.md), with its commands, events, capability words and the transcripts its conformance test replays here. `q2ui` does not use it yet; a Settings Brightness row and a status row's battery would.

Only root and a package whose user is in the `settings` group get past the hello; anyone else is answered `err permission` and logged ([Packages: Users and groups](platform/packages.md#users-and-groups), `test/perm.py`). The socket is mode 0660 and that group's.

## Tests

`python3 test/sysd.py` (pytest) builds `q2sysd` for this machine and runs it on a stand-in tree (a FIFO for an input device, files for `/sys`, `--poweroff` a command that leaves a file): a short press off and on with the gesture file following, a hold powering off at 3 s and not before and leaving the screen alone, other keys and a lone release doing nothing, the battery file, and Q2SYS/1 (the screen, brightness and its range, a fresh battery read and a status with a space in it, a watcher hearing a press, another version refused), the screen off time (LIGHTTIME 0 dark 15 s after the last use and not before, a key and `awake` each holding it, the watcher told; a value past stock's six never dark, and a change starting the time again), Auto Standby (held while a song plays and while a cable is in, then 300 s from the cable out, run 20 times faster with `--time-scale`; a charger on Charge Only holding it; music stopping the count, not starting it again; the screen off time's dark not a use) and Auto Power Off (SAVEPOWERMODE 1's 60 s from the last key; a cable in starting it again; with a cable, the charge wait with the music paused and Wi-Fi off, ending in the power off at Full or at the cable out, and a hold there restarting through `--reboot`; held by USB storage), neither when both are off. It also checks Charge Limit (held at 81, still held at 76, allowed at 75; the line never touched with the limit off), USB storage (the player's stop, then `sync`, `umount`, `park`, the gadget's LUN and controller, and back: `mount`, `unpark`, the controller cleared; a busy card refused with nothing parked) Standby (the screen, the pause, Wi-Fi off and woken, `mem` written; on a card boot Wi-Fi untouched) and the cable (a stand-in `ac/online`: Automatic's iPhone, then the card for a computer, a car kept until the cable is out, a charger leaving the card alone; Transfer Files and USB Audio at the cable and adb at its end; Charge Only, USB Debugging and Ask Every Time changing nothing, Ask's event; a cable in at the start), against stand-ins for the other services' sockets and the system's commands. Copies with a 1 s hold, without the gesture write, taking a release with no press, taking any key, with the charge line's sense inverted, without the 75% hysteresis, ignoring a busy card, not stopping the player, or turning a card boot's Wi-Fi off each fail it (`Q2SYSD_SOURCE`); so do copies that never read the cable, give Automatic's computer adb, or leave the mode at a cable out, and copies whose screen never goes off unused, whose input is not a use, without `awake`, off by one in stock's table, or not starting the time again at a change of the setting, and copies with no Auto Standby, no Auto Power Off, one not held by music or by a cable, or off by one in Auto Power Off's table, and copies whose holds restart the count, whose dark screen is a use, whose Auto Power Off a cable holds or that power off at once with a cable, that wait past Full, power off at a hold in the charge wait, leave USB storage or a cable change out, let Standby run with a cable in, or leave Wi-Fi on in the charge wait. `test/os_image.py`'s boot runs the image's own: the battery file from stand-in CW2015 files, a press on an input device of its own turning the screen off and on, and a hold starting init's shutdown (`rcK`'s line in the boot log); `--perturb sysd-off` fails those. Its `test_conformance_*` tests replay [system.md](platform/system.md)'s transcripts against it and check that every capability word is sent by one ([conformance](platform/README.md#conformance)).

## Needs a Q2

- Whether `PE22` can be driven through the kernel's GPIO class at all (stock drives it through `soc_gpio.ko`'s own ioctl, bypassing gpiolib), and the AXP2101's charging stopping when it is high.
- The storage gadget on the real controller beside q2adb's park, a computer seeing the card, and the card mounting again after.
- The UAC2 gadget and USB DAC mode's sound ([dacloop: Needs a Q2](dacloop.md#needs-a-q2)).
- `ac/online` under this init as the cable's signal: whether one read a second is enough (a quick replug inside it is missed, as F11 was for the payload's poll) and costs nothing measurable on the PMIC's bit-banged bus, and whether it ever reads a false 0 while a computer has the card (no debounce here: a single 0 remounts it).
- Standby on a charger at Full: [internals](internals.md#standby-and-the-usb-link) has `g_charge_state` 0 then, stock's binary has it follow `usb_detect`'s `PD17` only. Under stock, set Auto Standby on, leave the Q2 idle on a wall charger until its battery reads Full, and see whether it suspends; then check `ac/online` stays 1 at Full under Vamp OS. If stock suspends, `PD17` is not the plain cable and `q2sysd`'s Standby hold should follow `battery/status` too.
- The charge wait's `/sbin/reboot` reaching the AXP2101 and the Q2 coming up again.
- `mem` suspend under Vamp OS: what the drivers do with our services running, which wake sources the kernel arms (the power key, a cable: [internals: Standby and the USB link](internals.md#standby-and-the-usb-link)), and the current drawn.

- Which input devices carry the power button (watching every device with `KEY_POWER` covers both candidates), and whether one press arrives on two of them, which would turn the screen off and on again at once. ponytail: a press seen on two devices within a few milliseconds would then have to count once.
- Whether `bl_power` 4 turns this panel's light off at all, or stock blanks the framebuffer (`FBIOBLANK`) or powers the panel down (`lcd_h0209s`) instead; the brightness scale; and the double tap waking a dark screen through `hyngescontrol`.
- Whether `poweroff` reaches the AXP2101 and the Q2 switches off, and how long the PMIC's own long press takes to cut power (above 3 s, or this hold is never seen).
- The screen off time's uses: whether the wheel's `hyn_sliderbar` and the touchscreen deliver events while lit at the rate the payload's tslib sees (each one wakes `q2sysd`'s loop: the cost of a turning wheel is not measured), and whether more than eight input devices exist.
- The battery's `status` and `ac/online` names are those `q2guard` logs on a Q2; the level's accuracy at the ends of the range is the CW2015's.
