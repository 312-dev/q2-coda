# q2sysd

VampOS's system service ([roadmap: Who owns what](vamp-os.md#who-owns-what)): the power key, the screen's light and its off time, Auto Standby and Auto Power Off, the battery level, Charge Limit, USB modes and the cable, Standby, heat, the jacks' detection, a low battery, the watchdog, the clock's time zone and RTC, and Find My Vamp's jingle, with no `demo` ([vamp-os-base](vamp-os-base.md)). The output a jack selects, the DAC's power, the MCU and the settings store are later slices.

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Source | `sysd/q2sysd.c` (one file, one `poll` loop), `sysd/Makefile` |
| In the image | `/usr/bin/q2sysd`, the `q2sysd` service (`os/package/q2sysd`, `/etc/sv/q2sysd`) |
| Tests | `test/sysd.py` (this machine, a stand-in `/sys` and `/dev/input`), `test/os_image.py`'s boot (the image's own under QEMU) |

## What it does

| Duty | How | Known from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The power key** | Every `/dev/input/event*` whose keys include `KEY_POWER` (116) is watched: `md-gpio-keys`' power button (`PE31`), the AXP2101's key (`axp2101-pek`), and `hyn_ts`, which reports the touchscreen's double tap as `KEY_POWER` while its gesture mode is on. On the Q2 this is the Centre button (`md-gpio-keys` declares 103, 108, 116, 163 and 165, one code a button, read on the owner's Q2), so a press shorter than 3 s while the screen is lit is `q2ui`'s select and `q2sysd` leaves it; on a dark screen its release lights it, and one press seen on two devices (`md-gpio-keys` and the PMIC's key) lights it once, its hold timed from the first. `q2ui` turns a lit screen off itself (Q2SYS/1 `screen off`: Centre's double press, Now Playing's hold). Held 3 s it powers off (`/sbin/poweroff`: init runs `rcK`, which stops the services and unmounts the card and `/mnt/data`, then powers off). The release after a power off, and a release with no press, do nothing | read: [hardware](hardware.md), `module_driver/keyboard_gpio_add.sh`, [internals: Screen off and wake](internals.md#screen-off-and-wake) |
| **The screen's light** | Off after [Screen Off Time](#the-screen-off-time) unused, or at a press. Off: the light out and `/sys/hynitron_debug/hyngescontrol` 1 (gestures on, as stock's `double_onclick_wake` writes as the screen goes off); on: the light back and gestures off. The light is [the backlight](#the-backlight): a `/sys/class/backlight` device's `bl_power` (4, `FB_BLANK_POWERDOWN`, then 0) where the kernel has one, else stock's PWM on `/dev/jz_pwm`, which is what the Q2 has. `q2ui` takes `bl_power` not 0 as a dark screen and drops input then (`ui/ui.c` `dark`), so a press that lights the screen is nothing else; with the PWM there is no `bl_power`, and the `screen off` and `screen on` Q2SYS/1 events are what says so | read: `patch/q2guard.c`, `ui/ui.c`, [internals](internals.md#screen-off-and-wake) |
| **Brightness** | `brightness P` (0 to 100): on the PWM, stock's own `[SYSSET] LIGHTNESS` scale, so P is LIGHTNESS and lights at P + 11 of 111 steps (shown at once while lit, kept for the next on while dark); on a `/sys/class/backlight` device, P of its `max_brightness`, linearly | read: `config_lightness`, `toolSetBackLight` ([the backlight](#the-backlight)); the sysfs scale inferred |
| **The battery** | Every 30 s and at each change of the screen: the CW2015's `cw2015_capacity` and `cw2015_voltage` (`/sys/devices/i2c-0/0-0062/`), `/sys/class/power_supply/battery/status` and `ac/online`; on a change written whole to `/run/vamp/battery` (`level`, `mv`, `status`, `cable`, a `key=value` line each) and told to watchers | read: [hardware: Battery reporting](hardware.md), `patch/q2guard.c` (the same files, logged on a Q2) |

| **Charge Limit** | `Q2POD CHARGELIMIT=1` in `config.ini`, the payload's setting: at each battery read, charging is held off at 80% and handed back at 75% (`CHARGE_STOP`, `CHARGE_RESUME`) through the charger's active-low enable line `PE22`, driven as the kernel's GPIO 150 (`/sys/class/gpio`, exported once, `1` holds it off). Off, the line is never touched | read: [internals: Charge Limit](internals.md#charge-limit) |
| **USB modes** | `usb storage`: the player stopped (Q2PLAY/1 `stop`), the card synced and unmounted (refused while it is busy, nothing else changed), adb's gadget parked (`q2adb-gadget park`), and a mass-storage gadget made as stock's `storage_config.sh` makes one (`0525:a4a5`, one LUN, the card's block device, writable, removable) and bound to the controller. `usb adb`: the gadget unbound and removed, the card mounted again, adb's gadget back (`unpark`). At a cable, the one the USB setting names ([The cable](#the-cable)) | read: stock's `storage_config.sh`, [adb: The USB gadget](adb.md#the-usb-gadget) `usb dac`: the Q2 a USB DAC for the computer, the player stopped, adb parked, a UAC2 gadget with stock's descriptors and the `q2dacloop` service up ([dacloop](dacloop.md)); `usb adb` takes the loop down before its gadget. |
| **Standby** | `standby`: the screen off, Q2PLAY/1 `pause`, Q2NET/1 `off` (not on a card boot, whose Wi-Fi is the developer's way in), `sync`, then `mem` written to `/sys/power/state`, which returns when a wake source (the power key) has resumed the Q2; then the screen on and Q2NET/1 `wake`. A refused suspend (the kernel's error) still brings the screen back | inferred: the Linux interface; stock's own order is [internals: Screen off and wake](internals.md#screen-off-and-wake)'s |

## The backlight

The Q2 has no `/sys/class/backlight` device (read on the owner's Q2 under today's Vamp and in a card boot: the class is empty, the panel is `lcd_h0209s`). Stock's `toolSetBackLight` picks the light by the model its `demo` is built for, the constant 23 that `main` passes to `initToolsModule`: `backlight_sgm3132` for models 4, 7 and 9, `backlight_amoled` for 13 and 21, and otherwise PWM. For 23, `initToolsModule` takes pin `PC00` (`pwm_request`) and configures it at 10 kHz over 111 steps (`pwm_config` `{1, 0, 0, 10000, 111, channel}`), and `toolSetBackLight(L)` sets level L + 10 for L above 0, else 0 (`pwm_set_level`). `config_lightness` and `screen_action(1)` light it at `[SYSSET] LIGHTNESS` + 1; `screen_action(0)` sets 0, waits 50 ms and blanks the framebuffer (`disable_fb`, `FBIOBLANK` 4). libhardware2.so's calls are three ioctls on `/dev/jz_pwm`, the ones Find My Vamp's jingle uses (`patch/findmy_tune.h`): REQUEST `0x8001500b` (the pin's name, the channel back), CONFIG `0x80185021`, LEVEL `0xc004502c` (`{channel, level}`). On the owner's Q2 the running `demo` holds `{fd of /dev/jz_pwm, channel 0}` for it.

`q2sysd` does the same. On a boot from flash, where no `demo` ran before it to light the panel (stock's `config_lightness` at its start), it takes `PC00` at its start and lights the screen at the saved `LIGHTNESS`. On a card boot (`--card-boot`) it takes `PC00` only at the first screen or brightness change, so the light today's Vamp's `demo` left stays until something changes it. A sysfs device is left at the level its driver holds. A REQUEST refused lets channel 0 go and asks once more; the level goes to 0 before CONFIG, which the driver refuses while a channel runs. Off is level 0, 50 ms, `FBIOBLANK` 4; on is `FBIOBLANK` 0 and level LIGHTNESS + 11 (`config.ini`'s, 100 when it has none). A driver that will not give the channel answers `err screen`.

## The screen off time

Stock's **Screen Off Time** (Settings > Display), `[SYSSET] LIGHTTIME` in `/mnt/data/config.ini`: while the screen is lit and nothing uses the Q2 for that long, `q2sysd` turns it off as a power key press does (`bl_power` 4, gestures on, `screen off` told to watchers). The values are stock's: `demo`'s `setlight_time` (`0x4feb04`) maps 0 to 5 through a table of 15, 30, 60, 120, 300 and 1800 seconds and anything else to 0, which never turns the screen off; stock's file says 1 (30 s), and a file without the key reads as 1.

A use is any event on any input device (`EV_KEY`, `EV_ABS`, `EV_REL`: a side key, the Center button, the touchscreen, the wheel's `hyn_sliderbar`), so `q2sysd` keeps every `/dev/input/event*` open (eight at most), not only those with the power key; the screen lit by any means; and Q2SYS/1's `awake`, for a program that shows something with no key pressed (a video, a game, today's Vamp's Smart Awake on Now Playing, which is `q2ui`'s to send). `config.ini` is looked at once a second (its size and time), and a change starts the time again, as stock's `config_lighttime` resets its timer. The check runs on the same 1 s tick as the cable's, so the screen goes off up to a second late.

## Auto Standby and Auto Power Off

Stock's two power settings in `/mnt/data/config.ini`, read with Screen Off Time: **Auto Standby**, `[SYSSET] STANDBYMODE` (1 on: `demo`'s `setstandby_time`, `0x4feaf4`, gives 300 s; anything else off), and **Auto Power Off**, `[SYSSET] SAVEPOWERMODE` (1 to 4: `setsavepower_time`'s table, 60, 180, 300 and 600 s; anything else off). Both are off unless set: Vamp's own defaults leave Standby off (`tools/build.py` `DEFAULT_CONFIG_OFF`), and stock's file says 0 for both.

What `q2sysd` does is what stock's `demo` does, read from its V1.36 binary (notes outside the repository):

- **The counts.** `check_to_poweroption` (`0x4f3c90`, once a second) takes a second off each count only while nothing holds it, so a hold stops a count where it is and its end leaves the rest. Both counts are made whole again by `reset_poweroptions_timer(1, 1, 1)` (`0x4feb38`), which stock calls at a key or touch (`on_wm_keydown_before_fun`, `on_wm_keyup_before_fun`, `on_wm_tsdown_before_fun`), the screen coming on (`screen_action`, `0x501f64`; the screen going off resets nothing), a change of the setting (`config_lighttime`), the end of a Standby (`device_standby`) and a change of the cable, in or out (`get_battery_capacity`, `0x4ff318`). Here the same: a use ([The screen off time](#the-screen-off-time)'s, Q2SYS/1 `awake` among them), a setting change, a resume and a change of `ac/online`. The screen off time turning the screen off is not a use.
- **The holds, both counts.** Music playing (`mclGetPlayStatus` 2; here `state=1` in `q2playd`'s `/tmp/coda-rc.now`, which AirPlay's and DLNA's songs reach too), car mode (`g_carmode`; here Car Audio's `usb car`), USB storage (`g_usblink_status` 2; here `usb storage`). Stock also holds while its Bluetooth receiver page is up, a Wi-Fi transfer runs or a Baidu download runs; VampOS has none of those today.
- **Standby only: any cable.** The gate's last test is `g_charge_state` 0 (`0x4f3dd8`). Its only writer is `get_battery_capacity` (`0x4ff2e4`, `0x4ff318`), which stores `usb_detect`'s answer, GPIO `PD17` low, the cable, and 0 when the Q2 itself powers an OTG device. It is not the charge: that is `g_power_chargestate` (`toolsGetChargeStatus`, `0x5cf5ec`: `battery/status` Discharging 0, Charging 1, Full 2). So stock never suspends with a cable in, a charger's included, and a computer's or car's link is never under a Standby (today's Vamp's host hold, [internals: Off by default, held by a host](internals.md#standby-and-the-usb-link), falls inside it). Here a cable is `ac/online` 1. [internals](internals.md#standby-and-the-usb-link) says `g_charge_state` reads 0 at Full with the cable in; the binary gives it no writer that would do that, so the two disagree until a Q2 settles it ([Needs a Q2](#needs-a-q2)).
- **Auto Power Off: no cable hold, then stock's power-off page.** Its gate (`0x4f3e40`) is Standby's without `g_charge_state`. At zero it opens `poweroff_page`, which has no countdown and nothing to choose: `poweroff_page_init` (`0x53e8c8`) runs `into_poweroff` (`0x501a48`: the queue saved, the player stopped, the DAC, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, DLNA and AirPlay off), and with no cable `device_poweroff` at once. With a cable it stays on showing the charge (the level, an animation) and checks once a second (`0x53e790`), powering off once the cable is out or `battery/status` reads Full; a short press there lights or darkens the screen and a long one restarts (`on_poweroff_keylong_fun`, `device_reboot`). Here: no cable, a power off as a held power key does (`power off` told, init's shutdown); a cable, Q2PLAY/1 `pause` and Q2NET/1 `off` (not on a card boot), `charge wait` told (the charge screen is `q2ui`'s to draw), then `battery/status` read each second until the cable is out or it says Full, and the power off. A press in the charge wait lights or darkens the screen as ever, and the 3 s hold restarts (`reboot` told, `/sbin/reboot`) instead of powering off. Auto Power Off goes first when both are due.

## Heat

Today's Vamp's overheat guardian ([internals: Overheat guardian](internals.md#overheat-guardian)) with its two halves in one service: `q2guard`'s device side and `demo`'s. Its decisions and its PMIC reader are the same code, linked in (`patch/q2guard_core.c`, `patch/q2guard_pmic.c`, built with the system's headers, `Q2GUARD_HOST`), so every rule and number is that page's: three readings in a row at 55 C or more warn once (again only after one under 50 C), three at 62 C or more begin the power-off, a reading that jumps more than 3 C or falls outside 0 to 100 C is a misread, three bad in a row (or one value for 5 minutes) put the sensor down, and with no sensor two minutes at 85% CPU or more warn of hard work and power off 60 s later unless cancelled. The thresholds are still proposed for the owner ([internals](internals.md#overheat-guardian)).

- **A sample** every 5 s: the AXP2101's die temperature through its I2C window (`/dev/i2c-6`, address `0x34`, `I2C_SLAVE_FORCE`), on a thread of its own as each window waits 1 s with the power key and the sockets still served; the CPU's busy share from `/proc/stat`; the charger's state (`ac/online`, `battery/status` Charging), whose change steps the die's reading. A Standby since the last sample (Q2SYS/1's or Auto Standby's) leaves the last reading no bound for the next. With `/dev/i2c-6` missing or refusing the address (the emulator), the reader is off and the load is watched, as `q2guard` does.
- **What is shown** (`demo`'s side, `patch/heatguard.c`'s cards, through [Q2UI/1](platform/notices.md) while `q2ui` serves it; each also told to Q2SYS/1's watchers): the warning, a toast "Q2 is getting hot" (`heat warn`); no sensor, an ask "No Temperature Sensor" with OK (`heat nosensor`), withdrawn when the sensor is back (`heat ok`); hard work, an ask "Q2 has been working hard" with Cancel and Shut Down (`heat loadwarn left=60`), Cancel (or Q2SYS/1 `heat cancel`) holding it off (`heat cancelled`) and Shut Down powering off at once; the power-off, Q2PLAY/1 `pause`, a toast "Q2 is too hot. Shutting down to cool off." (`heat off`), and the power off 10 s later as `demo`'s card counts down, or at the core's 30 s deadline whatever happens. Q2SYS/1 `heat` answers the state.
- **On a card boot** nothing here runs: today's Vamp's own `q2guard` keeps running outside the image ([vamp-os-base: Card boot](vamp-os-base.md#card-boot)) and owns the PMIC's window, which two readers would fight over (each writes `0x34`). It powers the Q2 off by itself when no `demo` answers.
- **Not here**: the heat log and the unified log on the card, the Bluetooth and Wi-Fi recorders `q2guard` also runs. VampOS's boot log and the services' stderr are its logs today.

`--heat-file F` (tests) reads a temperature in tenths of a degree, or `none`, from `F` each sample in place of the PMIC, as `q2guard --dev` reads `/tmp/q2guard.sensor`; `--time-scale` runs the 5 s sample and the 60 s and 10 s countdowns faster.

## The jacks

Stock's jack poll, `check_headset_status` (`0x4f335c`, once a second, [internals: Jack detect](internals.md#jack-detect)): `PA07` the 3.5 mm jack and `PA08` the 4.4 mm, 0 plugged. Here they are read on the 1 s tick through the kernel's GPIO class (`/sys/class/gpio/gpio7` and `gpio8`, port A's base 0, exported as inputs when not yet), where stock goes through `soc_gpio.ko`'s own `/dev/gpio` (`gpio_open`, `gpio_get_value` in its library). A plug counts at the read that sees it; an unplug only when a read at least 300 ms after the first unplugged one still says so, which on a 1 s tick is the next read: today's Vamp's filter (`patch/jack_detect.c`), as powering the DAC up reads `PA07` unplugged for about 120 ms with a cable in. Each change is told (`jack po 1`, `jack bal 0`, ...), Q2SYS/1 `jack` answers both, and at an unplug that counts, music playing is paused (Q2PLAY/1 `pause`), as stock's `config_po_output` and `config_bal_output` (`0x501804`, `0x5015c4`) pause whatever the output when `mclGetPlayStatus` says playing.

At each change the two are written to `/run/vamp/jack` (`po=N bal=N`, whole and renamed), which `q2playd` reads for the jack its DAC is set to before a song ([playd: The DAC's output](playd.md#the-dacs-output)): the headset mode and the DAC's power, as stock's `config_outputchannel`. Whether `PA07` still reads unplugged after an unplug while the DAC stays powered and muted (today's Vamp's finding, `jack_detect.c`) is a Q2 question.

## A low battery

Stock's `check_lowbattery_state` (`0x4f3b38`), which `on_wm_idle_status` runs once a second on the last reading (read from `demo`'s binary; today's Vamp keeps it: [internals: Battery](internals.md#battery)), here on the 1 s tick:

| Reading, with no cable in (`ac/online` 0) | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 3400 mV or under, or 0% | counted; the fifth in a row powers off: `lowbattery off` told, Q2UI/1 `toast Shutdown` (stock's `msg_autoshutdown` dialog, "Shutdown", at `0x4f1a54`, then `poweroff_page`), then the power off as a hold of the power key does it |
| under 16%, the first time | `lowbattery warn` told, the screen lit if it was dark and `toast Low%20Battery` (stock's `on_wm_timer_dialog`: `awake_screen`, then `msg_lowpower`, "Low Battery", for 2 s); not again until a reading of 16% or more |
| anything else | the count starts again |

With a cable in nothing is counted (stock's `g_charge_state`, the cable's line), and a reading of 16% or more lets the warning come again. A read that failed (`-1`) counts for nothing. The battery is otherwise read every 30 s; once a reading is under 20% it is read each second, as today's Vamp reads it there (`BATT_DARK_FLOOR`), so the five readings are five seconds, as stock's are.

## The watchdog

Stock never starts the X2000's watchdog; today's Vamp does, from `demo`'s UI loop ([internals: Watchdog](internals.md#watchdog)). Here `q2sysd` owns it, with the same driver calls (`patch/system.c`'s): `/dev/jz_watchdog` (`soc_watchdog.ko`'s device) opened at start and started at 60 s (ioctl `0x8004570d`, an `int` of milliseconds by pointer), fed (`0x2000570f`) on the 1 s tick, so a kernel stalled that long, or `q2sysd`'s own loop, restarts the Q2. The driver counts through a suspend, so Standby stops it (`0x2000570e`) before the write to `/sys/power/state` and starts it again after; its `close` is a no-op, so a power off, a restart and `SIGTERM` (runit's stop, rcK's) stop it first. No device, or a refused start, is a log line and nothing more. On a card boot too: nothing of today's Vamp has started it before `q2vampos` hands over (its feeder is `demo`, which has not run), and a VampOS that stalls is then restarted into today's Vamp, unless `boot-always` is set. What a stall of `q2ui` or `q2playd` is (runit restarts a service that ends, not one that hangs) is not watched here.

## The clock

Stock's clock ([internals: Clock](internals.md#clock)) is three things: the time zone, the RTC and network time. Network time is `q2netd`'s ([q2netd: Network time](netd.md#network-time)); the other two are here.

**The time zone.** `[SYSSET] TIMEZONE`, the zone page's saved index, names it: line N of `/usr/share/vamp/timezones`, one zoneinfo name a line, which `tools/vampos.py zones` writes at build time from `tools/build.py`'s `TIMEZONES`, today's Vamp's table, so an index means the same zone under both. Four of that table's entries are POSIX rules standing in for zones the stock rootfs's tzdata has wrong; the image has Buildroot's tzdata, which has them right, so those take their names (`ZONE_FOR_RULE`: Etc/GMT+12, America/Mexico_City, Asia/Tehran, Pacific/Fiji). An index past the list, or under 0, is 22, Asia/Shanghai, as stock's `config_init` takes it; a file without the key is a fresh `/mnt/data`, read as today's Vamp's default for a new install, 43 (UTC, `DEFAULT_TIMEZONE`). The zone in effect is `/run/vamp/localtime`, a link to `/usr/share/zoneinfo/NAME`, and the image's `/etc/localtime` is a link to that, so no program needs `TZ` in its environment: glibc's `localtime` and `tzset` read `/etc/localtime` again when it points at another file (`localtime_r` only after a `tzset`). rcS sets it before the services start (`q2sysd zone`, which sets it and ends; its boot log line is `clock: zone /usr/share/zoneinfo/NAME, ... UTC`), and the service sets it again within a second of a change to `config.ini` (looked at with the screen off time's settings) and tells watchers `time zone=NAME`.

**The RTC holds UTC.** The kernel reads it as UTC at boot (`hctosys`), and `q2sysd` writes it with `hwclock -w -u` after each setting of the time, as stock's `toolsSetRtcTime` (`0x5cf290`) does after `date -s`: Q2SYS/1 `time set SECONDS` (by hand: `date -u -s @SECONDS`, then the RTC, `time set` told; a refused `date` writes nothing) and `time save` (the system clock as it is, after `q2netd`'s round). Stock's `hwclock -w` wrote local time, so its clock came back off by the zone's offset after a power off; today's Vamp writes `-wu`, and so does this. An RTC last written by stock holds local time until the time is next set. On a card boot the clock is the kernel's, shared with today's Vamp: a `time set` there sets both.

## The cable

`ac/online` (`/sys/class/power_supply/ac/online`) is read once a second (`CABLE_MS`): no event for VBUS reaches userspace ([usb: What the hardware can tell us](usb.md#what-the-hardware-can-tell-us)), and `online` is the signal that told a real source from residue on every line of the 2026-11-25 record. A change is a battery read at once (watchers hear `cable=`), and then:

| The USB setting (`[Q2POD] USBPICK`, the payload's picker's) | A cable in |
| --- | --- |
| 0 Automatic, the default, and anything not below | `usb car`: the iPhone. A car keeps it (Car Audio); a computer, once q2car has its gadget down, gets the card (`usb storage`); a charger (nothing answered) and a failed probe are adb, the card left alone |
| 1 Charge Only, 4 USB Debugging | nothing: adb is the mode with no cable, and charging needs none |
| 2 Transfer Files | `usb storage` |
| 3 USB Audio | `usb dac` |
| 5 Ask Every Time | a `usb ask` event for whoever draws the chooser, which answers with a `usb` command; until then, Charge Only |

A cable out is `usb adb` from any mode: the card mounted again, the loop down, or `q2car stop`. The setting is read at each cable in, so a pick made while one is in takes the next. A cable in when q2sysd starts is a cable in, so a Q2 started in a car plays to it; a q2sysd restarted by `runsv` with a cable in starts that mode again. Today's payload reaches the same table through its own poll ([usb](usb.md), `patch/usb_ui.c`); VampOS's chooser is `q2ui`'s, not drawn yet.

## Find My Vamp

Today's Vamp's ([internals: Find My Vamp](internals.md#find-my-vamp), `patch/findmy.c`), from the same `patch/findmy_tune.h`: Q2SYS/1 `find` plays the jingle on the internal buzzer through stock's PWM driver, `/dev/jz_pwm` (the channel released, then requested as `PC01`; each note level 0, CONFIG, level 50; a rest level 0; a refused CONFIG takes the channel again once), over and over on a thread of its own whose waits are 10 ms slices. The screen is lit and kept lit (a use each second), `q2ui` is sent `toast Find%20My%20Vamp`, watchers are told `find on`, and `/run/vamp/finding` is there while it plays, for `q2playd`'s now file ([playd: Find My Vamp](playd.md#find-my-vamp)). It stops at any key press, a wheel turn or a touch (the press itself is `q2ui`'s to drop, as `findmy_gate` drops it), at `find stop`, or after a minute; a `find` while it plays starts the minute again. At the end the pin is low and the channel released, and watchers are told `find off`. No `/dev/jz_pwm` answers `err find`. Today's toast lasts the minute; Q2UI/1's toast is 2.5 s, so the lasting one waits for a Q2UI/1 word for it.

## Key Tone

Stock clicks the MCU's buzzer: demo's `buzzeer_switch` runs `cmd_mcu write_str buzzer` and the MCU sounds its own click ([internals: Key Tone](internals.md#key-tone)). The MCU runs nothing until its firmware is loaded, which demo's `buzzer_init` does at its start (`cmd_mcu write_firmware /usr/data/libmcu-bare.bin`, `cmd_mcu bootup`, `cmd_mcu reset`); VampOS has no demo, so `q2sysd` does the same at its start (`mcu_up`), from the image's copy at `/vendor/mcu/libmcu-bare.bin`, through the ioctls `libhardware2.so`'s `mcu_*` send on `/dev/mcu`: the firmware in 4096-byte pieces (`mcu_write_mem`, `0x80044d73`: offset, data, length), then bootup (`0x20004d72`) and reset (`0x20004d71`). Q2SYS/1 `click` then sends the MCU the text `buzzer` with its NUL and cmd_mcu's 300000 timeout (`mcu_write_data_timeout`, `0x80044d76`), on a thread. With the MCU not up (no `/dev/mcu`, no firmware, a refused ioctl: the boot log says which) a click is silent (`err click`), never a tone: an earlier note on the PWM (2637 Hz for 12 ms) sounded as a beep, not stock's click, on the owner's Q2 (2026-11-28). A click while one is with the MCU, or while the jingle plays, is dropped (`err click`); the jingle waits out a click before it takes the PWM channel, which the MCU's buzzer shares. Nothing else of VampOS uses the MCU: demo's other MCU work is this firmware load, and `mclSetDacPwr` reads flag files, not the MCU. The route (Key Tone on, the Clicker's choice, what listens) is [q2gate](gate.md#key-tone)'s.

## Q2SYS/1

On `/run/vamp/sys.sock`: its protocol is Vamp API 1's system page, [Q2SYS/1](platform/system.md), with its commands, events, capability words and the transcripts its conformance test replays here. `q2ui` does not use it yet; a Settings Brightness row and a status row's battery would.

Only root and a package whose user is in the `settings` group get past the hello; anyone else is answered `err permission` and logged ([Packages: Users and groups](platform/packages.md#users-and-groups), `test/perm.py`). The socket is mode 0660 and that group's.

## Needs a Q2

- Whether `PE22` can be driven through the kernel's GPIO class at all (stock drives it through `soc_gpio.ko`'s own ioctl, bypassing gpiolib), and the AXP2101's charging stopping when it is high.
- The storage gadget on the real controller beside q2adb's park, a computer seeing the card, and the card mounting again after.
- The UAC2 gadget and USB DAC mode's sound ([dacloop: Needs a Q2](dacloop.md#needs-a-q2)).
- `ac/online` under this init as the cable's signal: whether one read a second is enough (a quick replug inside it is missed, as F11 was for the payload's poll) and costs nothing measurable on the PMIC's bit-banged bus, and whether it ever reads a false 0 while a computer has the card (no debounce here: a single 0 remounts it).
- Standby on a charger at Full: [internals](internals.md#standby-and-the-usb-link) has `g_charge_state` 0 then, stock's binary has it follow `usb_detect`'s `PD17` only. Under stock, set Auto Standby on, leave the Q2 idle on a wall charger until its battery reads Full, and see whether it suspends; then check `ac/online` stays 1 at Full under VampOS. If stock suspends, `PD17` is not the plain cable and `q2sysd`'s Standby hold should follow `battery/status` too.
- The charge wait's `/sbin/reboot` reaching the AXP2101 and the Q2 coming up again.
- The jacks through the GPIO class: whether `gpio7` and `gpio8` export and read beside `soc_gpio.ko` (stock never exports them). Under VampOS, `cat /sys/class/gpio/gpio7/value` with a 3.5 mm plug in and out (0 and 1), the same for `gpio8` and a 4.4 mm plug, then Q2SYS/1 `jack` and a pull while a song plays (paused about a second later). If the GPIO class refuses them, `soc_gpio.ko`'s ioctl is the way, which needs its numbers traced.
- Heat on a Q2 booted from flash: the PMIC's window from `q2sysd`'s thread (the first conversion's time in the log, `SENSOR pmic first conversion after N ms`), the die's readings beside `q2guard`'s on the same Q2 (boot today's Vamp, read its heat log, then VampOS's `boot.log`), and a warm-up under load (a long FLAC playlist over Bluetooth while charging) reaching the warning toast through `q2ui`. On a card boot only that `heat` answers `off` and the outer `q2guard` still logs (`/mnt/mmc/.q2muse/logs/q2-heat.0` grows).
- A low battery on a Q2: the CW2015's voltage under load near empty (whether 3400 mV, stock's floor, comes before the gauge's 0%); run down under VampOS with the screen on, `cat /mnt/data/vamp/boot.log` after for `battery N% M mV: shutting down`, and that the Q2 then powers off rather than browning out.
- The watchdog under VampOS: whether `soc_watchdog.ko` makes `/dev/jz_watchdog` under this init's mdev (rcS logs `/dev/watchdog`'s state, the kernel's own interface, whose ioctls are not the driver's), then `q2sysd`'s log line `watchdog: 60 s`; and that it bites: `kill -STOP $(pidof q2sysd)` over adb, and the Q2 restarts about 60 s later (into today's Vamp on a card boot with `boot-once`).
- The clock under VampOS: that the kernel sets the system clock from the RTC at boot (`hctosys`: rcS's `clock:` line shows the UTC time it found; `dmesg | grep -i rtc`), that `hwclock -w -u` reaches the RTC (Q2SYS/1 `time set` to a known time, power off for a minute, boot, and the `clock:` line a minute on), and the zone page's choice showing in `date` over adb (`TIMEZONE=38` in `config.ini`: `date` says CST or CDT).
- The backlight on the PWM under VampOS: on a card boot, Q2SYS/1 `screen off` then `screen on` (the light out, then back at the saved Brightness), `brightness 100` and `brightness 10` while lit, and `/run/vamp/services.log`'s `q2sysd: backlight: PC00 on channel N` line. Whether the REQUEST is refused while `demo`'s claim is gone (the line says so after a release), and whether Find My Vamp's jingle on channel 1 still plays after the backlight's CONFIG.
- Find My Vamp under VampOS: that rcS's `soc_pwm.sh` makes `/dev/jz_pwm` (`ls -l /dev/jz_pwm`), then `echo -e 'Q2SYS/1\nfind' | nc local:/run/vamp/sys.sock` (or the Vamp app's Find) plays the jingle with the screen lit, a key stops it within a note, and with no MCU firmware loaded by `demo` the channel is free at the first REQUEST (`logread | grep find`).
- `mem` suspend under VampOS: what the drivers do with our services running, which wake sources the kernel arms (the power key, a cable: [internals: Standby and the USB link](internals.md#standby-and-the-usb-link)), and the current drawn.

- Which input devices carry the power button (watching every device with `KEY_POWER` covers both candidates), and whether one press arrives on two of them, which would turn the screen off and on again at once. ponytail: a press seen on two devices within a few milliseconds would then have to count once.
- Whether `bl_power` 4 turns this panel's light off at all, or stock blanks the framebuffer (`FBIOBLANK`) or powers the panel down (`lcd_h0209s`) instead; the brightness scale; and the double tap waking a dark screen through `hyngescontrol`.
- Whether `poweroff` reaches the AXP2101 and the Q2 switches off, and how long the PMIC's own long press takes to cut power (above 3 s, or this hold is never seen).
- The screen off time's uses: whether the wheel's `hyn_sliderbar` and the touchscreen deliver events while lit at the rate the payload's tslib sees (each one wakes `q2sysd`'s loop: the cost of a turning wheel is not measured), and whether more than eight input devices exist.
- The battery's `status` and `ac/online` names are those `q2guard` logs on a Q2; the level's accuracy at the ends of the range is the CW2015's.
