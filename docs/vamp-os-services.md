# VampOS: the remaining services

Design for five features the owner kept in VampOS's scope ([roadmap: Scope](vamp-os.md#scope); the owner's ruling of 2026-11-26): DLNA/UPnP, scrobbling, LocalSend, the AirPlay sender and Guest Play. Each is today a piece of `demo`'s payload, a helper or a card app; in VampOS each is a service under the supervisor, a client of the [Vamp API 1](platform/README.md) sockets, with the permissions a package of its kind would declare ([Packages](platform/packages.md#permissions)). The order below is the build order: what needs the least that is missing comes first.

| Feature | Today | VampOS | Needs first | Fits now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scrobbling | `patch/nav/playback_power.c` (the listen), `patch/scrobble.c` (the upload, demo's libcurl) | `q2scrobd`: a Q2PLAY/1 watcher writing the same `.scrobbler.log`; the upload a second step | nothing | **yes: built** ([below](#scrobbling)) |
| LocalSend | `patch/localsend_core.c` (protocol), `localsend.c`, `localsend_ui.c` (demo's side) | today's `q2send` on the image's OpenSSL, run by `q2sendd`, which asks its questions over Q2UI/1 | `q2ui` serving Q2UI/1 ([notices](platform/notices.md)) and a LocalSend page that starts it | **the receive side built** ([below](#localsend)); asks nothing until `q2ui` serves the socket |
| AirPlay sender | the `q2-airplay` card app (cliairplay) and the PEQ tap `/tmp/q2cast` | the card app as a `service` package; `q2player` writes the tap, `q2playd` is its host | nothing in this repository: the app's release with an API 1 manifest | **yes: built** ([q2playd: AirPlay](playd.md#airplay)) |
| Guest Play | `q2-guest` (BlueZ D-Bus, a second bluealsa), `patch/guest.c` (pausing the owner's music) | `q2-guest` as a service, its pause and resume through Q2PLAY/1 | Bluetooth from source (BlueZ 5.88, bluez-alsa: `feat/bluez-own`, `feat/bluealsa-own`), in the image | **the daemon built and run** ([Q2GUEST/1](platform/guest.md)); no `q2ui` page yet |
| DLNA/UPnP renderer | stock's DMR inside `demo` (`dmrAction*`) | `q2dlnad`: a UPnP MediaRenderer (SSDP, the three services' SOAP) handing a URI to `q2playd` as a source | nothing: Q2PLAY/1's `source` and `focus` are built | **yes: built** ([q2dlnad](dlnad.md)) |

## Rules for all five

- **A client, never a peer of the player.** Each reaches the music through Q2PLAY/1 alone (`watch` for what plays, the transport verbs), the network through Q2NET/1 (`hold S` while it works: Smart Wi-Fi turns the radio off otherwise), and never opens the DAC, the framebuffer or evdev.
- **Asked, not assumed.** A service that needs the owner's answer (LocalSend's accept, Guest Play's who-may) asks through `q2ui`; until `q2ui` has a notice and confirmation channel ([roadmap: Power, notices](vamp-os.md#the-api)) those services do not run.
- **Its own state, its own folder.** Settings and accounts stay where today's Vamp keeps them (`.scrobble.ini`, `.localsend/`), read in place, so a switch either way loses nothing ([roadmap: User data](vamp-os.md#user-data)).
- **Off by default where it listens on the network.** LocalSend and DLNA bind ports; each starts only when its setting is on, as today.

## Scrobbling

`q2scrobd` (`scrobd/q2scrobd.c`, the `q2scrobd` service) keeps the listen log today's Vamp keeps, from Q2PLAY/1 instead of demo's queue:

- It subscribes (`watch`) and follows each `now` line: a song is its `id`; the seconds heard count while `state=1` and `pos` moves forward by 1 to 3 s at a time (a seek or a pause adds nothing), as `playback_power.c` counts them.
- **A listen** is half the song or 240 s, whichever is less, for a song over 30 s (`LISTEN_MIN_S`, `LISTEN_MAX_S`); with `[SCROBBLE] PERCENT=N` in `/mnt/mmc/.scrobble.ini`, N % of it instead. One a play: a song started again is a new play.
- **The line** is today's: Rockbox's AudioScrobbler 1.1 format in `/mnt/mmc/.scrobbler.log`, the header (`#AUDIOSCROBBLER/1.1`, `#TZ/UTC`, `#CLIENT/Vamp`) first in a new file; artist, album, title, the track number (empty for 0), the length, `L`, the start (now less the seconds heard) and an empty MusicBrainz id, tab-separated, tabs and line ends in tags made spaces. A song with no artist, or a clock before 2020, is not logged. The track number and the album artist (`[SCROBBLE] ALBUM_ARTIST=1`) come from the library row (`library.db`, Library 1); the tags from the `now` line, percent-decoded.
- **The upload**, `q2scrobd upload`, is `patch/scrobble.c`'s: the log's listened lines in batches of 50 to ListenBrainz (`[LISTENBRAINZ] TOKEN`, a JSON `import` with `Authorization: Token`) and Last.fm (`[LASTFM] API_KEY`, `API_SECRET` and `SESSION_KEY` from `tools/lastfm_key.py`, or `USER` and `PASSWORD`, which `auth.getMobileSession` turns into a key once an upload; `track.scrobble` signed by MD5 of the sorted parameters and the secret). A batch leaves the log, for `.scrobbler.log.sent`, only once every service with an account took it; the new log is written and synced before `.sent`, and `.sent` before the rename. It speaks TLS through the image's mbedTLS (the same static build `q2player` links), the server's name and chain checked against `/etc/scrobble-ca.pem` (`assets/scrobble-ca.pem`, today's file), and nothing is sent to a server that file does not vouch for. The log is locked (`flock` on `/tmp/q2scrobd.lock`) while the daemon appends and while the upload reads or rewrites it. It answers 0 (all sent, or nothing to send), 1 (a batch failed: kept for the next) or 2 (no account).
- **The automatic upload**, with `[SCROBBLE] AUTO=1`, is `patch/scrobble_ui.c`'s rule in the daemon: once the oldest unsent listen since it started is 180 minutes old, 4 minutes sooner per listen since, at least every 60; Wi-Fi asked of Q2NET/1 at most once a minute (`status`, and `wake` when it is not connected, the next minute's look sending it); the radio held (`hold 300`) and `q2scrobd upload` run as a child; a failure tried again after 30 minutes. Listens left from before a boot go with the next upload. A manual upload is `q2scrobd upload` until `q2ui` has its Upload Scrobbles row.

Its permissions as a package would be `playback,library,network-hold,background`.

**Tests.** `python3 test/scrobd.py` (pytest) builds it for this machine and runs it against a stand-in Q2PLAY/1 sending `now` lines: half of a 40 s song logged once, with the header, the library row's track number, the decoded album and a start time the seconds heard back; a 1000 s song at 240 s; a seek and a paused scrub not counted; a 30 s song and an artist-less one not logged; `.scrobble.ini`'s `ALBUM_ARTIST` and `PERCENT`; a song played again logged again. The upload against a stand-in HTTPS server on this machine with a CA made for the run (never a real key or host): ListenBrainz's body byte for byte with a quote and a backslash escaped, a skipped, a junk and an album-less line, and the log left holding its header with every line in `.sent`; Last.fm's signed scrobble byte for byte (no duration for a length of 0), and a password's sign-in once with its key used; 120 listens in batches of 50, 50 and 20 to both; a 500 from ListenBrainz and Last.fm's `error` each leaving the log as it was; a server whose certificate the CA file does not vouch for sent nothing; no account, nothing. The automatic upload with minutes of 10 ms (`--minute-ms`): a listen sent when due with the radio held, Wi-Fi off woken and nothing sent, and `AUTO` off nothing at all. Copies that count a seek, drop the 240 s cap, miss a replay or leave out the track number, and copies that verify no certificate, let a batch go that one service refused, send 100 a batch, leave the secret out of the signature, escape nothing in JSON, never wake the radio, never hold it, ignore `AUTO`, ignore the file's session key or Last.fm's `error` each fail it (`Q2SCROBD_SOURCE`). `test/os_image.py` checks the image's service list holds it; the emulator's songs end at once (no sound card), so no listen is heard there.

## LocalSend

Today's daemon, `q2send` (`patch/localsend.c` on `patch/localsend_core.c`, [localsend](localsend.md)), is the protocol on VampOS too, unchanged: built with real headers (`LS_HOST`) against Buildroot's OpenSSL 3, which the image now carries for it (the image's mbedTLS is built client-only on purpose, `tools/thirdparty.py` `MBEDTLS_UNSET`, and a port of the server, its certificate making and the client to it could not be checked here against the reference app, whose CLI is x86-64 only). Its identity, its trusted devices and the Received folder are today's (`/mnt/data/q2send/`, `/mnt/mmc/Received/`), so phones know the Q2 under either firmware. `q2sendd` (`sendd/q2sendd.c`, the `q2sendd` service) runs it as a child and is for it what demo's `localsend_ui.c` is today, through the same `/tmp/q2send.*` files:

- **An incoming request** is asked as a Q2UI/1 card ([notices](platform/notices.md)): `ask recv-N Receive%20files? <alias>%20sends%20<n>%20files%20(<size>) Accept Decline Always%20Accept`. `answer recv-N 1` accepts; `answer recv-N 3` accepts and trusts the sender (`q2send.answer` `<id> 1 trust`, as demo's card's Always Accept switch, off unless chosen: Q2UI/1's card has choices, no switch, so it is the third one, never focused first), so its next send is not asked; any other answer, no `ui.sock`, a refused `ask` or the UI's connection ending declines at once (the sender sees 403), never a wait. A request `q2send` drops first (its sender gone, its 60 s up) withdraws the card.
- **A trusted sender** is not asked: a `toast Receiving%20from%20<alias>` instead, so a receive is never silent (one shown once its transfer is seen running or done; two identical back-to-back sends inside one 200 ms look show one toast).
- **The card a computer's** over USB (Q2SYS/1 `usb storage`, read at the start and watched) is `q2send.hold`, so every request is refused; any other mode lifts it.
- **The radio** is held over Q2NET/1 (`hold 60`, renewed every 30 s) while it runs.
- **A receive done** runs `--after-receive`'s command once, in the image `sv hup /etc/sv/q2libd`: `q2libd` scans the card (unchanged files are not read again, [libd: When it scans](libd.md#when-it-scans)), so the songs received are in the Library. demo waits for the daemon's end and for nothing playing before stock's whole-library update ([localsend](localsend.md)); `q2libd`'s scan reads only the new files, so it runs at once.
- **Off by default**: `/etc/sv/q2sendd` has a `down` file; the LocalSend page `q2ui` will have starts it (`sv up`) and stops it, as demo starts `q2send` with its page today. SIGTERM ends `q2send`, then `q2sendd`.

Not here yet: the send side and the page. Sending needs nothing more of a service: `q2send`'s `cmd` (`send`), `state` and peers files are there for the page in `q2ui` to drive, as demo drives them; only a Vamp API page for another app to send through would be new, and none is designed. Its permissions as a package would be `network-hold,ask,notices,card`.

**Tests.** `python3 test/sendd.py` (pytest) builds `q2send` and `q2sendd` for this machine and runs them beside stand-ins for Q2UI/1, Q2NET/1 and Q2SYS/1, with a sender made in the test speaking LocalSend v2 over TLS with a client certificate: the card's text and a file taken on its first choice, Accept trusting no one; Always Accept trusting the sender so its next send is taken without a card; `--after-receive` run once after a receive and not after a refusal; 2, 0 and `gone` each refused; no UI socket refused in under 5 s; a sender who hangs up withdrawing the card; a trusted sender toasted and not asked; `usb storage` at the start and as an event holding, `usb adb` lifting it; the radio held; SIGTERM ending both. Broken copies (`Q2SENDD_SOURCE`) each fail it: any answer taken as yes, no withdraw, no toast, no hold, no radio hold, a missing UI waited on, `q2send` left running, no third choice, the third choice not trusting, the scan never run, the scan run at a refusal. `test/os_image.py` checks the image carries `q2sendd`, `q2send` and `libssl`, with the service down at boot.

## AirPlay sender

The card app needs a copy of what the Q2 plays. `q2player`'s DSP chain (`player/dsp.c`) already writes it into the shared tap `/tmp/q2cast` while the app beats, as today's Vamp does, so nothing in `player/` changes; `demo`'s half of the tap (the status, the volume, the track's tags, the volume's swap to the speaker) is `q2playd`'s ([q2playd: AirPlay](playd.md#airplay)). The app is a `service` package (`permissions=playback,network-hold,background`) run by `/etc/sv/q2-airplay` once added; its row on the ⋯ page is `q2ui`'s to declare ([extension points](vamp-os.md#extension-points)).

## Guest Play

`q2-guest` already talks to BlueZ and bluealsa over D-Bus and decides on its own; demo's part is pausing the owner's music while a guest plays and resuming it after, which is Q2PLAY/1's `pause` and `resume`. Started with `--socket`, it serves its commands and state to `q2ui` as [Q2GUEST/1](platform/guest.md), which says when the UI pauses and resumes. The image carries it (`os/package/q2-guest`, `etc/sv/q2-guest`) on its own BlueZ and its one bluealsa, which has the sink role, so this build starts no second bluealsa ([Q2GUEST/1: Status](platform/guest.md)).

## DLNA/UPnP renderer

Stock's renderer lives in demo, so VampOS has its own, `q2dlnad` ([q2dlnad](dlnad.md)): SSDP announcements and the MediaRenderer's AVTransport, RenderingControl and ConnectionManager services, written against the UPnP AV specification with no library. A `SetAVTransportURI` and `Play` become a Q2PLAY/1 source ([Audio focus](platform/playback.md#audio-focus)): the URL with its DIDL-Lite title, artist and album, the Library's queue put aside and given back paused. Its permissions as a package would be `playback,network-hold,background`; it listens only while stock's `[SYSSET] DLNA` switch is on.
