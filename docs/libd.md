# q2libd

VampOS's library service ([roadmap: Who owns what](vamp-os.md#who-owns-what)), its first slice: the card's songs and the server catalogues, scanned with no `demo`, into the two databases in the shapes `q2ui` and `q2playd` already read, so neither needed a change ([vamp-os-base](vamp-os-base.md)). Its second adds covers, the card's M3U playlists and duplicates between the card and a server; its third the servers' offline copies and playlists. Its fourth serves the library over a socket, [Q2LIB/1](platform/library.md#q2lib1-the-library-socket) on `/run/vamp/lib.sock`, from a thread of its own. Full-text search, incremental scans by inotify, thumbnails and CUE are later slices.

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Source | `libd/q2libd.c`, `libd/Makefile` |
| In the image | `/usr/bin/q2libd`, the `q2libd` service (`os/package/q2libd`, `/etc/sv/q2libd`), linked against `q2player`'s own static FFmpeg 7.1.5 (its demuxers read the tags) and the image's SQLite; `/run/vamp/lib.sock` (Q2LIB/1, the `library` permission's) |
| Tests | `test/libd.py` (this machine), `test/os_image.py`'s boot |

## The two databases

| File | What | Shape | Read by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/mnt/data/vamp/library.db` | The card's songs | Stock's `songtable`, every column (`id`, `filename`, `fileurl`, `album`, `songer`, `style`, `name`, `composer`, `albumsonger`, `year`, `track`, `cd_num`, `time`, `time_create`, `Initial`, `bitdepth`, `samprate`); `vamp_files` (`fileurl`, `size`, `mtime`, `id`), what the next scan compares with; `vamp_cover` (`id`, `file`), each song's cover ([Covers](#covers)); and stock's `playlist` ([Playlists](#playlists)) | `q2ui --db`, `q2playd --db` |
| `/mnt/mmc/.vamp/catalogue.db` | The servers' songs | `servertable` with the same columns, as the payload's `cat.servertable` (`patch/catalogue.c`), less the ones that are card songs; `serverdup` (`id`, `fileurl`) names those ([Duplicates](#duplicates)); `servermeta` (`id`, `app`, `sid`, `local`) each row's app, server id and offline copy ([Offline copies](#offline-copies)); `serverpl` and `serveritem` the servers' playlists ([The servers' playlists](#the-servers-playlists)) | `q2ui --catalogue` (attached as `cat`, `ui/lib.c`'s union) |

Decisions, by the roadmap's [User data](vamp-os.md#user-data) rule (a switch either way loses nothing, and nothing stock or today's Vamp owns is rewritten):

- **Stock's `/mnt/data/database.db` is never touched by `q2libd`** (only its `myfav` rows are written, by `q2playd`, for likes: [playd](playd.md#ratings-and-likes)), and neither is today's Vamp's `/mnt/mmc/.q2pod/catalogue.db`: both are files of their own. `q2libd`'s index is in `/mnt/data/vamp/`, the roadmap's place for everything new.
- **The catalogue is on the card**, as today's Vamp keeps its own: a 50,000-song catalogue is about 25 MB of rows, past what `/mnt/data` (17.7 MB) holds ([server-library: Where it is kept](server-library.md#where-it-is-kept)).
- **A song keeps its id while its path stays**: q2playd's queue and q2ui's state name songs by id. A file whose size and modification time are what the last scan saw is not opened again; its row is copied.

## The card

A walk of `/mnt/mmc` in name order, at most 16 folders deep, leaving out every dot folder and dot file (an app's, the system's: `.vamp`, `.q2pod`, `.<id>`) and every file whose extension is not one the player plays (`flac mp3 wav aif aiff m4a mp4 alac ogg oga opus ape wv wma aac ac3 dts dsf dff tak`). Each new or changed file is opened with libavformat and its stream info read: `title`, `artist`, `album`, `album_artist`, `genre`, `composer`, `date` (the year), `track`, `disc` (the leading number of `7/12`), the length, the sample rate and width. A song with no title is listed by its file name without the extension, as stock lists it; `Initial` is the title's first letter upper-cased or `#`, as `patch/catalogue.c` gives it.

## The server catalogue

Every card app folder `/mnt/mmc/.<id>` holding both `catalogue.tsv` and `credentials` (signed in: [server-library](server-library.md)), the first four in name order, each a slot as the payload's (`CAT_APPS`): its `s` rows become `servertable` rows with ids from `0x40000000 + slot * 0x01000000`, the path `/mnt/mmc/.<id>/cat/<album id>/<sid>.<suffix>` as the payload makes it, the card-app channel's `%XX` escapes undone. A catalogue whose header is not `#Q2CAT/1` with a `rows=`, or whose `s`, `p` and `i` lines do not add up to `rows=` (a file cut short), is left out whole. `p` and `i` rows are the servers' playlists ([below](#the-servers-playlists)).

## The servers' playlists

Each app's `p` rows (`p <pid> <name>`) are `serverpl` rows (`app`, `pid`, `name`), and its `i` rows (`i <pid> <sid> [card path]`) `serveritem` rows (`app`, `pid`, `pos`, `sid`, `path`), `pos` counting each playlist's items from 0 in the file's order. An item's `path` is a synced song's card file, which has no `s` row ([server-library](server-library.md)); empty, or off the card, it is NULL and the song is the app's `servertable` row of that `sid` (`servermeta` names it). A cut-short catalogue keeps none of its playlists. What shows them is `q2ui`'s, and Car Remote's `coda-rc --library vampos` reads them as they are ([carrd](carrd.md#the-library)); the payload's mirror of them into stock's playlists (`serverlists`) is not done here.

## Offline copies

`servermeta` (`id`, `app`, `sid`, `local`) has a row for each `servertable` row: its app's id, the server's song id, and the path of a whole offline copy on the card (the app's Stream Cache), NULL with none. The copy is first the catalogue's own `s` row's local path; then the app's `/mnt/mmc/.<id>/catalogue.avail` applies in order, as the payload's `cat_avail` does: `<sid>\t<path>` sets a copy, `-<sid>` clears it, a path off the card (not `/mnt/mmc/...`, or with a `..` segment) clears it, a sid not in the catalogue is passed over, and a last line with no newline yet waits for it. A duplicate's row (a card song, [Duplicates](#duplicates)) is left out.

At each catalogue write the whole file is read again. Between writes `q2libd` looks at each app's `catalogue.avail` every second and applies the lines past where it stopped to the live `catalogue.db` in place, in one transaction, and from the start when the file got shorter (rewritten). That is the one change made in place ([Library 1](platform/library.md)); it rewrites nothing else and runs no `--after`, as `q2ui` reads no copy. `q2playd` plays the copies when a server list's app does not answer ([q2playd: Offline copies](playd.md#offline-copies)).

## Covers

An album's cover is kept once, as `/mnt/mmc/.vamp/covers/<key>.jpg` on the card (not `/mnt/data`, which is small), the bytes as found (JPEG or PNG; `q2ui` reads either), at most 4 MB. `<key>` is `%08x` of the FNV-1a of the album artist (else the artist), a tab and the album, lower-cased; with no album tag, of the song's folder. The picture is the file's own (FFmpeg's attached picture), else the first of `cover`, `folder` and `front` `.jpg` or `.png` in its folder. It is looked for when a file is read; an unchanged file keeps its row. A cover file already there is kept, so a changed picture shows once its file is removed. `q2playd` copies the playing song's to `/tmp/externpic.jpg` for `q2ui` ([q2playd: The cover](playd.md#the-cover)).

## Playlists

Each `.m3u` or `.m3u8` in the card's `/mnt/mmc/_explaylist_data/` (stock's import folder), in name order, the first 100, becomes rows of `library.db`'s `playlist`, stock's table: a header row (`id` and `cd_num` the playlist's number from 1, `name` and `filename` the file's name less its extension), then its songs from `id` 101 on in the file's order, each its `songtable` row with `cd_num` the playlist's. An entry is a card path (`/mnt/mmc/...`) or one relative to the folder (`..` allowed, `\` read as `/`); a leading UTF-8 BOM, `#` lines and blank lines are skipped, and an entry that is no song in the library is left out. The numbering from 1 is this slice's own: stock's header ids are below 101 and its songs above, which this keeps. The folder's `.m3u` files are part of what the 10 s look compares, so a new or changed one is read at the next look.

## Duplicates

A server song that is already a card song is left out of `servertable`, so the Library lists the card's file once, and `serverdup` names the card file it was. The match is the payload's tags layer (`cat_same` in `patch/catalogue.c`): the same title, album and track, the same artist or album artist (case aside), and lengths within 2 s, both known. A length further off is another take and stays. Matched at each catalogue write, which now follows each card scan as well. The payload's MusicBrainz-id and same-file layers are not done.

## When it scans

At its start: an empty library first when there is none, so `q2ui` always has one to open. Then, looking every 10 s: when the card's line in `/proc/mounts` changes (inserted, removed, a remount) or a playlist file in `_explaylist_data/` does, the card again, and the catalogue after it (its duplicates); when a signed-in app's `catalogue.tsv` or `credentials` changes (size, time) or an app comes or goes, the catalogue again; and both at `SIGHUP`. Each app's `catalogue.avail` is looked at every second ([Offline copies](#offline-copies)). Each database is written whole beside itself (`.new`) and renamed over it, so a reader never sees half a scan (`catalogue.avail`'s lines alone change `servermeta` in place). After each write it runs `--after`'s command, in the image `sv hup /etc/sv/q2ui`: `q2ui` starts itself again in place ([hot reload](vamp-os-base.md#hot-reload)) and shows the new lists.

## Tests

`python3 test/libd.py` (pytest) builds `q2libd` for this machine against its own FFmpeg and SQLite and runs it on a stand-in card of songs `ffmpeg` makes: the tags, the no-title name, the length, dot folders and other files left out, stock's `database.db` byte for byte the same; a second scan reading nothing and keeping every id, a removed file gone, a changed one read again under its old id, a new one a new id; a signed-in app's catalogue with an escaped tab and both row shapes, a signed-out app and a cut-short catalogue left out; `q2ui`'s own union query over the two files; covers from a file's own picture and from its folder's `cover.jpg` byte for byte, none without, kept for unchanged files; playlists from two M3U files (BOM, comments, CRLF, a relative `..` path with backslashes, an entry not in the library) and a new one read by the running service within its look; a server song with the card's tags left out and named in `serverdup`, one 59 s longer kept; the servers' playlists and their items in order (a synced song's card path, an off-card one none, a cut-short catalogue's none); offline copies from the catalogue's local path and `catalogue.avail`'s lines in order (an unknown sid, a clear, an off-card path, a cut last line), none kept for a duplicate, and as a service a new line applied within seconds in place (the same file, no `--after`), a shorter file read whole again; and as a service, an empty library and `--after` before the card is mounted, the card scanned once it is. Copies without the `rows=` check, without keeping ids, reading dot folders, not asking for `credentials`, without the attached picture, without keeping covers, without the path tidy, without the duplicate delete or without the playlist folder in the look each fail it, as do copies keeping no playlist, every item at one place, or an off-card item path, and copies without `catalogue.avail` at a write, without the 1 s look, taking an off-card path, not reading a shorter file whole, keeping a duplicate's copy, taking a cut last line, without the catalogue's local path, or writing the catalogue again for a new line (`Q2LIBD_SOURCE`). `test/os_image.py`'s boot: the image's `q2libd` scans the boot's card through FFmpeg under QEMU into `library.db`, and the song played over Q2PLAY/1 is that row; with the service replaced (`--perturb libd-off`) the scan check fails; and Q2LIB/1 answers there (its caps, the card's song by id). **Q2LIB/1**: the page's transcripts replayed against `q2libd` running on the page's stand-in card (card songs with a cover, a card playlist, a signed-in app's songs and playlist), every caps word sent by one; a caller without the `library` permission refused at its hello on a 0660 socket; `watch` telling both files written again at a `SIGHUP` scan and `catalogue.db` changed in place by a `catalogue.avail` line; and answers coming within 0.5 s, again and again, while a scan of 3000 new files runs. Twelve copies each fail it: no permission check, songs in id order, the artist filter minding case, search's text not decoded, `watch` minding only a new file, text not percent-encoded, pages ignored, albums with no name listed, an unknown playlist answered `ok`, server songs called `card`, unknown words passed over, and answers held by a scan (one lock for both).

## What is missing

- Duplicates by MusicBrainz id or the same file, thumbnails of covers, a changed cover picked up without its file removed.
- Scans driven by inotify instead of a 10 s look, and progress for a long first scan: measured under QEMU only, not on a Q2's card.
